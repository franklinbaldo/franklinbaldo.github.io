## Material movement: Unified Emergence / Continuum-First Ontology — D/A → F/A, high confidence

**Source/date.** Daryl Costello, *Unified Emergence: Continuum-First Ontology, Relational Coarse-Graining, and Refractive Cosmology*, Veridical Horizon, posted **2026-09-18**: <https://veridicalhorizon.blog/2026/09/18/unified-emergence-continuum-first-ontology-relational-coarse-grainingand-refractive-cosmology/>.

**Proposal.** The programme starts from an undifferentiated continuum Ω and makes particles, fields, spacetime and observers successive coarse-grained precipitates. Its Photonic-Higgs Refractive Layer (PHRL) is intended to replace fundamental spacetime curvature with a refractive medium that recovers light bending, Newtonian gravity and relativistic time dilation.

**Why the tier changes.** The original D placement treated the programme mainly as incomplete: the coarse-graining operator and Standard-Model mapping were explicitly unfinished. A closer audit of the manuscript's own gravitational equation finds a stronger problem. The PHRL dynamics are written as

`□n + μ²(n − 1) = κ T`,

with `μ = m_H c / ħ`; in the static limit this becomes

`(∇² − μ²)(n − 1) = −κρ`.

Outside a localized source, the regular Green-function branch is Yukawa-like, `e^(−μr)/r`, with range `μ⁻¹ = ħ/(m_H c)`. Using the measured Higgs mass near 125 GeV gives a range of roughly **1.6×10⁻18 m**. The manuscript itself instead says this Higgs-scale screening length is consistent with the absence of deviations from Newtonian gravity at macroscopic scales. That implication is backwards: such a massive mode would make the response vanish exponentially essentially everywhere gravity has actually been measured.

This is load-bearing because the PHRL is the proposed mechanism for gravitational refraction and time dilation. The problem is therefore not merely that a future Standard-Model derivation is missing; the current gravitational propagator does not reproduce long-range gravity as written. Under the Arena rubric that moves the present formulation to **F scientific**. The rating is scoped to this version, not to continuum-first ontology in general.

**Uncertainty / recovery gate.** The failure could be repaired only by changing the dynamics materially: e.g. derive a genuinely massless or sufficiently light gravitational/refractive mode, decouple the long-range mode from the Higgs mass, or replace the operator with one whose physical Green function gives the observed long-range `1/r` behavior. That replacement then has to rederive lensing, redshift, Shapiro delay, PPN limits and strong-field behavior. Merely relabeling the Higgs-scale screening as 'microscopic' does not repair the sign/range issue.

**Interest stays A.** The continuum-first/coarse-graining ontology remains a useful foil for discrete pregeometry, and the manuscript is explicit enough to generate unusually clean adversarial tests. That is research interest, not evidence for correctness.

**Clash — Lucron Network (C/A).** Battleground: **pregeometry plus controlled recovery of long-range continuum physics**. Unified Emergence has the cleaner continuum-first ontology but currently fails its own long-range PHRL propagator gate. Lucron is still incomplete on exact Lorentz/Yang–Mills/particle recovery, but its Arena record is presently a recovery debt rather than a directly contradictory long-range gravitational equation. No Lucron tier movement follows.

**Movement history.** 2026-09-19: entered **D/A**, medium confidence. 2026-09-24: **D/A → F/A**, high confidence, after the propagator/range audit.

## Added as adjacent: Einstein–Maxwell from One Ordered Response (SL(3,C)) — NR scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Source/date.** Erik Trangärd, *Einstein–Maxwell from One Ordered Response: An Exact Local SL(3,C) Construction*, Preprints.org v1, submitted **2026-09-22**, posted **2026-09-23**, not peer-reviewed: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202609.2071>.

**Proposal.** The only dynamical field in the parent action is an `SL(3,C)` connection. A degree-one homogeneous adjoint-invariant phase of curvature wedge products supplies the constitutive response. Curvature-derived spectral projectors select an ordered `3+4+1` branch; on its regular Lorentzian neutral sector, the paper claims the geometry, Maxwell propagation and electromagnetic backreaction all descend from derivatives of the same parent phase, yielding the exact Einstein–Maxwell–Λ electrovacuum equations at finite electromagnetic amplitude.

**Evidence for.** This is unusually sharp for a new unification preprint: the claimed recovery is exact on the stated local branch rather than a low-amplitude matching, and the paper advertises explicit invariant completion, two-jet and coupling conventions. That gives independent readers a concrete derivation to reproduce or break.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The scope is not yet ToE scope. The public result is classical gravity + the abelian electromagnetic sector. It does not derive weak/strong interactions, chiral matter, generations, Higgs/Yukawa structure, a realistic Standard Model, quantization or a distinctive empirical discriminator. It is also a single-author non-peer-reviewed preprint with no independent reconstruction located in this scan.

**Placement.** `kind: adjacent`, **NR scientific / A interest**, medium confidence. `NR` is a scope decision, not a claim that the mathematics is poor: the paper does not yet attempt the gravity + realistic Standard Model breadth required for ranked ToE contenders. Interest A reflects the clean benchmark it provides for single-parent action compression.

**Clash — Weyl–Dirac–Born–Infeld (B/A).** Battleground: **economy/exact recovery versus breadth/maturity in gauge–gravity unification**. Trangärd's construction is narrower but more austere: one pure connection and one response phase recover Einstein–Maxwell locally. WDBI is peer-reviewed, technically older and already embeds the full Standard Model with a route to Einstein gravity, but imports rather than derives the SM gauge/matter structure. The new adjacent result does not justify moving WDBI.

**Movement history.** 2026-09-24: first Arena registration as adjacent **NR/A**. A same-parent derivation of non-abelian chiral matter plus independent reproduction would be the natural gate for promotion into the ranked contender set.
