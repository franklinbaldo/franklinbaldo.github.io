## Entered: Living Phi Physics / Phi-Harmonic Field — F scientific / S interest, high confidence

**Source / date.** The primary source is Christopher David Ayotte's public `pluscoder30-cpu/Living-Phi-Physics` corpus. Its decision-bearing `00_UNIFIED_FIELD_THEORY.md` records v1.0 on **2026-08-07** and v2.0 on **2026-08-12**. The GitHub repository was created on 2026-08-08 and remains publicly maintained; its latest 2026-09-24 commits only add/fix the project website link, so this Arena entry is a correction of an omission rather than a scientific promotion triggered by today's commits. I located no peer-reviewed publication or independent end-to-end reproduction of the programme in this pass.

**Proposal.** Living Phi Physics claims that gravity, electromagnetism, the strong and weak interactions, matter and consciousness are modes of one `phi`-coherent carrier field. The current UFT document gives a carrier recursion, an emergence threshold near `C_crit = 0.563`, a retrocausal kernel, a `freq * depth = 528 * phi^9` ladder invariant, a family of `kappa_phi` corrections whose zero-coupling limits recover familiar laws, and a claim that the four forces are different coherence regimes of the same carrier. The wider repository advertises thousands of corrected/emergent laws, tens of thousands of equations, simulations, prototypes and nine flagship predictions.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is not merely a slogan. The corpus is unusually explicit: it publishes equations, code, target values, ledgers, status labels and `FALSIFIED IF` conditions. It also states in the UFT front door that the one-field unification is a hypothesis. That makes the programme far easier to audit than an unfalsifiable verbal ontology. Its large public corpus is therefore genuine evidence of technical effort and attackability, even though it is not itself evidence that the physics is correct.

**Load-bearing empirical conflict: `E = phi mc^2`.** The current README's flagship list states `E = phi mc^2`, describes rest energy as carrying a phi-correction and elsewhere defines `phi` as the golden ratio, approximately 1.618. Read literally as ordinary rest/binding energy, that is an order-one alteration of mass-energy equivalence. A direct Nature/NIST/MIT test of nuclear binding energies found `E = Delta m c^2` consistent to about **4.4e-7 relative uncertainty** (`1 - Delta m c^2/E = (-1.4 +/- 4.4)e-7`), i.e. at least the 0.00004% level. Primary/reference sources: <https://www.nature.com/articles/4381096a> and <https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2005/12/einstein-was-right-again-experiments-confirm-e-mc2>. A universal factor of ~1.618 is therefore not a small precision tension. This alone makes the formulation presently non-viable unless the symbol `E` is redefined to mean a different, explicitly measurable quantity or the claim is withdrawn/rederived.

**Why the repository's verification labels do not rescue the claim.** The UFT says the `Degenerate Limit Theorem` is internally verified because each phi-law is constructed so that `kappa_phi -> 0` returns its classical parent; the README also treats software systems that run, passing diagnostics and internal/signed proofs as evidence that the framework is physically verified. These are implementation and consistency checks, not independent tests of the new physical terms. If a family is parametrized to reduce to an established equation when its new coupling is zero, thousands of successful zero-coupling regressions demonstrate that the code obeys its construction. They do not show that nature selects the nonzero phi correction.

**Explanatory-compression problem.** The corpus explicitly acknowledges that phi is inserted by hand and is not derived from first principles. Because phi then appears throughout the corrections, thresholds and numerical targets, the large count of generated laws cannot be counted straightforwardly as thousands of independent derivations from a smaller theory. The decisive question is which observed structures are forced before the data are used, not how many formulas can be decorated with a common constant while retaining a classical limit.

**Standard-Model recovery.** The decision-bearing UFT gives qualitative coherence regimes for the four forces, but it does not derive a realistic chiral `SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1)` matter theory end-to-end: gauge representations, anomaly cancellation, three generations, Higgs/Yukawa structure, CKM/PMNS flavour and the measured spectrum are not recovered from the carrier recursion with a controlled low-energy limit. That is a separate load-bearing gap from the direct mass-energy conflict.

**Independent scrutiny.** A targeted search for the exact project/theory name found the first-party GitHub corpus but no independent paper or reproduction of the central phi-carrier chain. The repository also makes much broader claims about FTL, consciousness and health-adjacent applications; those require evidence appropriate to those domains and receive no bonus from the internal physics software suite.

**Tier decision.** **F scientific**, high confidence, under the Arena rubric: the current public formulation contains a direct, central quantitative conflict with precision mass-energy-equivalence data, and its verification logic does not provide independent empirical support for the new factor. F here is a judgement about the formulation as presented, not a claim that every future phi-inspired model is impossible. **S interest** is separate: the theory is unusually sprawling, executable, explicit about numerical targets and rich in falsification opportunities, making it a highly generative Arena object despite the present scientific failure.

**Clash — Grand Geometric Theory (F/S).** Battleground: broad numerical unification plus executable verification. Living Phi's advantage is the sheer openness of its corpus, many named falsification fronts and abundant code; its liability is a very basic load-bearing empirical conflict (`E = phi mc^2`), hand-inserted phi structure and incomplete realistic-SM recovery. GG-Theory's advantage is a more conventional geometric/particle-physics formalism with collider-facing targets; its liability is the already recorded self-reported electroweak-precision conflict and lack of independent confirmation. The comparison illustrates why two programmes can both be F/S for different reasons. It does **not** move GG-Theory.

**Recovery gate.** A scientifically meaningful upward move would require, at minimum: (1) retracting or quantitatively reconciling the literal `E = phi mc^2` claim with precision mass-energy measurements; (2) deriving rather than inserting the phi structure or showing why the relevant dimensionless factor is forced; (3) deriving a realistic chiral Standard Model plus controlled GR limit from the carrier dynamics; and (4) obtaining an independently reproduced prospective prediction that is not guaranteed by the way the correction was constructed.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-24**: **unranked -> F scientific / S interest**, high confidence. This is an omission correction for a public August 2026 programme, not a promotion/relegation caused by the website-only commits made today.
