# ToE Arena scan — 2026-09-24 — Ether Theory of Everything

This incremental scan applies `franklinbaldo/skills/toe-arena-tiering`: scientific strength and research interest are separate comparative judgements, not probabilities that a theory is true.

## Entered: Ether Theory of Everything (ÆToE) — F scientific / A interest, high confidence

**Source / date.** The decision-bearing source is the public English seed document in `etoe/etoe`, version **0.1.25**, dated and updated **2026-09-24** (created 2026-06-01): <https://github.com/etoe/etoe/blob/master/docs/seed/en/EToE.md>. The same-day commit by Aleksei Morozov refines the “principle of the etheronic basis of measurement” and the rules for ether units, so this is an actively changing programme rather than an old repository that merely happened to receive maintenance.

**Proposal.** ÆToE posits discrete absolute matter (“etherons”) in discrete Euclidean three-dimensional absolute space and discrete absolute time. The basic space is an integer lattice with toroidal topology; time is likewise cyclic. Higher-level structures — spirals, majors, atoms, fields, bodies and astronomical systems — are intended to emerge hierarchically from etheron organization. The programme extends beyond fundamental physics into life, consciousness and society. Version 0.1.25 strengthens a simulator-oriented metrology: every physical unit is required to reduce to characteristics of a single etheron through basic etheronic units.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The main positive evidence is structural rather than empirical. The seed is large, public and unusually explicit for an independent foundational programme: it defines coordinates, wrapping functions, units, object hierarchies and a notation/grammar intended to become simulator state. It also makes risky claims instead of insulating itself from observation. In particular, it provides nonstandard accounts of cosmological redshift, the CMB, particle structure and galactic rotation curves. That makes the project scientifically easy to challenge even though it does not make those claims scientifically successful.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** Two explicit cosmology claims are decisive for the current tier. First, the seed says cosmological redshift is **tired light**, caused by changes/decay in ether-spiral light packets during propagation. Modern observations show the corresponding cosmological time-dilation signature expected in an expanding universe. The Dark Energy Survey analysis of 1,504 Type-Ia supernovae measured the light-curve stretch as `(1+z)^b` with `b = 1.003 ± 0.005 (stat) ± 0.010 (sys)` and reports that non-time-dilating cosmologies are ruled out at very high significance: <https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/533/3/3365/7738388>. Quasar variability independently shows the same cosmological time dilation: <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-023-02029-2>. The ÆToE seed does not supply a quantitative tired-light mechanism that reproduces these observations.

Second, the seed states that the **cosmic microwave background is caused by proton decay in interstellar and intergalactic space**. COBE/FIRAS measures the CMB as a nearly perfect **2.725 K blackbody**, with exceptionally small spectral distortions: <https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/cobe/firas_overview.html>. Proton decay itself is extremely constrained; the Particle Data Group lists mode-specific proton partial-lifetime limits as high as `2.4 × 10^34 yr`: <https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/download/db2024.pdf>. The present ÆToE document gives no radiative-transfer/thermalization calculation showing how proton decay compatible with those limits produces the observed blackbody spectrum, anisotropies and acoustic structure.

The particle and spacetime correspondence problems are independent blockers. The document introduces an “Ether Standard Model of Matter”, but it does not derive the observed chiral `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)` gauge theory, anomaly cancellation, three fermion generations, Higgs/Yukawa structure, CKM/PMNS mixing or QCD hadron phenomenology from its etheron rules. Its absolute lattice and preferred cyclic time also carry a major Lorentz-recovery burden. Preferred-frame effects in gravity continue to be tested strongly; a 2026 LAGEOS analysis constrains the PPN preferred-frame parameter to roughly `|α1| ~ 2×10^-5`: <https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/v88p-965w>. The seed does not yet demonstrate a continuum limit that suppresses the corresponding Lorentz violations in both matter and gravity.

**Placement.** **F scientific / A interest, high confidence.** F is attached to the present formulation because it contains direct cosmological claims that conflict with well-tested observations and has no demonstrated correspondence map back to the Standard Model and local Lorentz symmetry. It is not a statement that every ether or preferred-frame effective theory is impossible; controlled aether EFTs exist and can be constrained consistently. A interest is a separate judgement: ÆToE is broad, explicit, actively versioned, simulator-oriented and full of concrete failure surfaces. It is therefore more useful to the Arena than a vague unfalsifiable manifesto even though its current scientific standing is poor.

**Clash — The Lucron Model (C/A).** Battleground: discrete fundamental ontology → observed low-energy spacetime, matter and cosmology. Both projects try to replace familiar continuum ontology with elementary discrete constituents and a hierarchy of emergent structures. Lucron currently has the stronger scientific case because its Version 4 exposes explicit relations for gauge/mixing quantities and a DESI DR2 cosmology comparison, and even records numerical failures; its open problems are largely incomplete derivations and independent reproduction. ÆToE is more expansive and more directly simulator-minded, but it has not yet recovered the observed gauge/matter structure and currently asserts tired-light and proton-decay-CMB mechanisms without reproducing decisive cosmological data. **Lucron remains C/A; no tier movement follows from this clash.**

**Movement history.** This is ÆToE's first canonical Arena placement: **unranked → F/A on 2026-09-24**. The entry was triggered by the same-day v0.1.25 revision, not by a retrospective search alone. A recovery path is explicit: derive a quantitative propagation/cosmology model that simultaneously fits redshift and observed time dilation; reproduce the CMB blackbody spectrum and anisotropy structure without violating proton-decay bounds; exhibit a controlled Lorentz-restoring continuum limit; and recover the measured chiral Standard Model from the etheron dynamics. Independent reproduction would then be needed before the proposal could approach B-tier maturity.
