# ToE Arena screen — 2026-09-23 — Nardelli Seventh-Root / Golden Harmony

This screening applies the Arena rule that scientific strength and interest are separate. Because the current paper does not supply a realistic Standard-Model derivation, the framework is **not admitted as a canonical contender in this pass**. If the Arena were forced to assign letters to the current formulation, the provisional result would be **F scientific / A interest, high confidence on the scientific gate**; that pair is recorded only as a screening aid, not as a canonical tier.

## Source and date

Michele Nardelli, *Golden Harmony and Black Hole Stability: The Gravitational Nexus from 26D String Theory to 10D Supergravity*, issue date **2026-09-21**. The current journal page describes it as a definitive unification of a 26-dimensional bosonic-string model with the Nardelli–Miriam Theory of Everything, using a “golden-locking” mechanism, the Hardy–Ramanujan number 1729, 4096/1728 anchors and an 18th-root construction to drive a gravitational nexus toward a target near 1.618665. A preprint version was public in April 2026.

## Proposal

The programme treats a 26D string-inspired action as a primordial source for 10D local dynamics and introduces a scalar gravitational nexus whose stability is tied to golden-ratio/numerical fixed points. The paper extends the construction to black-hole horizons, traversable-wormhole throat stability and a “Golden Remnant,” and interprets repeated numerical proximity to the target golden value as evidence of a self-referential/fractal physical architecture.

## Scientific audit

There are two decisive problems before any numerical coincidences can carry evidential weight.

First, the paper offers as “experimental verification” of boson–fermion transformation the supposed transformation of an electron into a photon. Taken literally, that is incompatible with electric-charge conservation: the electron carries charge −1 while the photon is neutral. The Particle Data Group treats electric-charge conservation as a tightly tested consequence of QED gauge symmetry and quotes a lower lifetime bound of `6.6×10^28 yr` even for the charge-conserving exotic decay `e → ν_e γ`. Ordinary electron–positron annihilation into photons is not an electron turning into a photon.

Second, the dimensional/string-language chain is internally unstable. Early sections call the source a **26D bosonic string action**; later the paper calls essentially the same object a **26D heterotic string action**. In standard heterotic string theory the target spacetime is ten-dimensional: the bosonic-moving sector has the critical 26-dimensional bookkeeping, but 16 of those degrees become internal rather than a literal 26D heterotic spacetime. Therefore the paper needs a specific nonstandard construction and derivation, not a relabeling of the bosonic critical dimension.

The current paper also does not derive the observed `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)` chiral Standard Model, anomaly cancellation, three generations, Yukawa/flavour structure or measured running couplings. Its strongest numerical “locking” evidence is generated from selected constants/exponents and, in places, an explicitly adjustable `Φ_golden` chosen to make the relation close on the desired golden target. Such self-consistency does not by itself provide an out-of-sample prediction.

## Screening decision

**Rejected from canonical entry in this pass.** This is a scope/formalism decision, not a claim that every string-inspired or number-theoretic relation in the programme is false. The current formulation lacks the gravity-plus-realistic-Standard-Model chain expected of an Arena contender and contains load-bearing physical/string-theory inconsistencies before its golden-ratio coincidences can be interpreted as evidence.

**Provisional scientific tier if admitted:** F. The reason is not unconventional mathematics but the present formulation's direct conflict with charge conservation as stated, dimensional/string-theory conflation, and target-calibrated numerical construction without an independent predictive bridge.

**Provisional interest tier if admitted:** A. The work is useful as a highly explicit case study in numerical-attractor theories, dimensional reduction claims, and the distinction between algebraic self-consistency and physical prediction. It is less experimentally sharp than the S-interest candidates added elsewhere in this pass.

## Clash

**Weyl–Dirac–Born–Infeld (B/A, pending Arena batch).** Battleground: higher-level mathematical compression versus demonstrated field-theoretic unification. WDBI starts from a concrete gauge/geometry action, has a peer-reviewed lineage and a controlled Einstein+SM low-energy limit, although it imports rather than derives much of the Standard Model. The Nardelli framework attempts more radical numerical compression but currently lacks the same field-theory bridge and contains the contradictions above. This screen does not move WDBI.

## What would reopen the gate

A new version would deserve re-audit if it (1) specifies the actual string construction without conflating 26D bosonic criticality with 10D heterotic target spacetime; (2) removes or replaces the electron→photon argument with charge-conserving dynamics; (3) derives a realistic chiral Standard Model sector; and (4) freezes at least one nontrivial quantitative prediction before comparison with data, without solving an adjustable golden parameter to hit the target.
