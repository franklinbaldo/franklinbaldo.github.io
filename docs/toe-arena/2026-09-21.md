### Quantized Dimensional Ledger / QDL-QDC — enters C / interest A, medium confidence

**Source/date:** James D. Bourassa / QDL Physics Institute, living *QDL/QDC Unified Field Theory Completion-Gate Series*, current public status updated 2026-08-11; frozen *Toward a QDL Unified Field Theory* archive V1.0 dated 2026-08-10, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21894736`. The broader publication hierarchy includes a peer-reviewed 2026 metrology-layer paper, while the unified-field completion record itself is an open research programme rather than an independently validated final theory.

**Proposal:** QDL begins from typed dimensional closure and physical-admissibility constraints, combines them with a selected finite QDC recurrence/master core, and attempts a staged completion into particle capacity, gauge/Higgs structure, gravity, quantum measure and ultimately a unified field theory. The programme explicitly separates structural results, admitted completion assumptions, computational gates, interpretations and empirical claims.

**Evidence for:** unusually explicit claim firewall and fail-closed completion gates; executable finite-model dynamics; machine-readable/reproducible archives; particle-capacity constructions; a conditional route to Einstein-Cartan/GR universality; and a research record that names its own unresolved selectors instead of presenting them as solved.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** the current programme itself states that it lacks a uniquely normalized parent theory, complete particle phenomenology and an experimentally confirmed distinctive QDL prediction. Cross-sector amplitude normalization, absolute scales, full flavor/Yukawa structure, fermionic exchange/statistics, global chiral measure and large-volume transfer remain open. The strongest unified-field claims have not yet acquired independent peer-reviewed or unaffiliated replication comparable with mature programmes.

**Placement:** C/A, medium confidence. C matches structured speculation: substantial formal and computational structure with meaningful falsification machinery, but unresolved physical completion and weak independent validation. Interest A comes from the unusually auditable completion-gate methodology and the number of concrete failure points; it is not an endorsement of correctness.

**Clash:** Lucron and String/M-theory. Against Lucron, QDL currently has the clearer explicit completion/falsification ledger but also openly lacks amplitude normalization and full phenomenology. Against String/M-theory, QDL is far less mature and independently developed, but its finite executable gates make its failure boundary unusually legible. Neither clash warrants movement of the older entries.

**History:** 2026-09-21 enters C/A, medium confidence as a backlog discovery surfaced by the fresh scan.

Primary: https://qdlphysics.org/research

Publication record: https://qdlphysics.org/publications

### GL(4,C) Unified Geometric Theory — enters C / interest A, low confidence

**Source/date:** Dimitris Mastoridis & Konstantinos Kalogirou, *A Unified Geometric Theory from the Symmetry of GL(4,C)*, Preprints.org v1, posted 2026-02-06; non-peer-reviewed.

**Proposal:** the theory starts from a four-complex-dimensional `GL(4,C)` geometry and symmetry breaking to `U(4)`. It identifies the compact sector with particle/gauge physics and the Hermitian coset with geometric/cosmological physics, including gravity and a dark sector. The manuscript further claims a chain of quantitative predictions for particle masses, coupling unification, dark-sector scales and cosmological observables.

**Evidence for:** genuine ToE scope rather than a single-sector gravity model; an explicit group-theoretic substrate and Lagrangian-level sectors; concrete `GL(4,C) -> U(4)` and `10+5+1` decomposition claims; and numerous numerical predictions that are attackable by independent calculation.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** no located independent reproduction or peer review of the unified claims. Several crucial physical identifications appear to move from representation/dimension structure to observed sectors without the level of independent derivation needed to count as recovery. The very precise agreements with Higgs/top masses and cosmological tensions require a dedicated audit for calibration, post-hoc choices and uncertainty accounting. Quantum consistency, anomaly control, renormalization/unitarity and full low-energy Standard Model recovery are not independently established in the located record.

**Placement:** C/A, low confidence. There is enough non-trivial mathematics and quantitative structure for the Arena's C bucket, but the evidential base is much thinner than B-tier programmes. Interest A reflects breadth and the unusually sharp numerical claims worth adversarial reconstruction.

**Clash:** Holomorphic UFT. Both use complex-geometric routes to gravity-plus-matter unification. Holomorphic UFT currently has a multi-paper peer-reviewed lineage and more explicit Standard-Model recovery work; GL(4,C) advertises broader parameter-level and cosmological predictions. A successful independent derivation of those predictions, or a demonstrated hidden-fit failure, would move this comparison materially.

**History:** first Arena cataloguing on 2026-09-21 at C/A, low confidence. This is a backlog discovery, not a claim that the paper itself was published today.

Primary: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202602.0517

### String theory / M-theory — remains A / interest S

Joshua N. Benabou, Naomi Gendler, Thomas R. Harvey and Jakob Moritz posted *Calculating Axion-Matter Couplings in String Theory* on 2026-09-17 (`arXiv:2609.20911`). They calculate, for the first time in the stated model class, dominant perturbative axion-matter couplings explicitly in heterotic Calabi-Yau line-bundle compactifications, numerically solving for Ricci-flat metrics, Hermitian Yang-Mills bundle metrics and harmonic matter forms. This is a real improvement in the bridge from compactification geometry to potentially observable low-energy couplings.

A separate 2026-09-17 paper, *Massive type 0A string theory and M-theory* (`arXiv:2609.20919`), proposes an M-theory origin for two massive type-0A deformations through a quantum-fibration construction. It strengthens internal duality/structural development but explicitly leaves puzzles about branes/Chern-Simons structure.

**Decision:** A/S unchanged. These are technical advances inside a mature programme, not positive string-specific empirical evidence, a unique vacuum selection mechanism, or a solved blocking problem. The axion calculation is recorded in the canonical card because it materially improves low-energy calculability.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20911

Context: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20919
