## Entered: Neutrino–Antineutrino Plasma Unification — F scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Source / date.** Brian Tillotson, *A neutrino-antineutrino plasma unifies spacetime and the Standard Model*, Zenodo v5, published **2026-05-15**, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20212974. The public lineage is older: Tillotson presented *Dark Matter is the Low-Energy Tail of a Neutrino-Antineutrino Plasma* as poster 345 at the APS Global Physics Summit on **2025-03-18**.

**Proposal.** The theory identifies the vacuum/spacetime substrate with a dense plasma of right-handed neutrinos and left-handed antineutrinos. Weak-magnetic alignment of this plasma is proposed to seed inflation and a binding scale near `10^16 eV`. Ordinary Standard-Model particles are treated as quasiparticles of the medium; gravity, electromagnetism and the strong/color force are described as related gradients in it. The v5 record claims derivations of `ħ`, `c`, `G`, `Λ`, several boson and charged-lepton masses, a neutrino mass, `α` and `α_s`, and advertises predictions for the lightest neutrino mass, dark-matter structure, and high-energy neutrino/gamma-ray cutoffs.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is a concrete and highly attackable proposal rather than an unfalsifiable slogan. It makes cross-sector numerical claims and exposes an astrophysical propagation prediction that can be checked against real data. A precursor was presented in an APS meeting, and the Zenodo preprint provides a stable public record. Those facts justify cataloguing it even though they do not amount to independent scientific validation.

**Decisive problem.** The current v5 description says neutrinos above the plasma's approximately `10^16 eV` binding scale are scattered and uses IceCube's non-observation above that scale as an explanatory success; it also explicitly advertises an upper bound on astrophysical-neutrino energy. But the KM3NeT Collaboration published **on 2025-02-12**, in *Nature*, the cosmic-neutrino event KM3-230213A. Its simulated incoming-neutrino energy has a median of **220 PeV = 2.2×10^17 eV**, with a 90% interval of **72 PeV–2.6 EeV**. Thus even the lower end of the reported 90% interval lies well above `10^16 eV`. The event was public more than a year before v5, so this is not a later failed forecast; it is an observation that the current formulation should already have addressed.

IceCube itself still reports no comparable event well above 10 PeV, so there is a real inter-observatory tension. That does not restore the theory's claimed universal cutoff: a viable account must quantitatively explain why KM3NeT can see such an event while IceCube does not, with source/direction/detector dependence derived in advance rather than introduced as an exception.

**Tier decision.** **F scientific** applies to the *present v5 formulation*: the Arena's F rubric explicitly covers a theory contradicted by strong evidence as presented. It is not a claim that every neutrino-plasma idea is impossible. The load-bearing constants/particle/gravity derivations are also not independently reproduced in the located literature. **S interest** is independent: a theory that turns vacuum into a neutrino medium, makes the Standard Model quasiparticle-like, and commits to hard cosmic propagation thresholds is exceptionally generative for adversarial tests and comparison.

**Clash — Selection–Stitch Model (B/S).** Battleground: structured-vacuum substrate → matter + gravity + discriminating prediction. Both replace an empty vacuum with a physical substrate and try to make observed particles collective structures. SSM currently has peer-reviewed sector papers and explicit D4/Regge/lattice calculations, but its full SM + nonlinear-GR bridge remains incomplete. Tillotson's model has a sharper astrophysical discriminator, which is an advantage for falsifiability but currently a liability because the advertised cutoff collides with KM3NeT. The clash therefore does **not** move SSM.

**Movement history.** First canonical placement on **2026-09-23** at **F/S**, medium confidence. Upward movement requires a frozen quantitative propagation model that accommodates KM3-230213A together with IceCube and other neutrino constraints without ad-hoc exemptions, plus independent reconstruction of at least one central claimed derivation. A revised formulation should be evaluated afresh rather than automatically inheriting F.
