## Entered: 3BF Higher-Gauge Unification — B scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Origin / dates / sources.** The programme originates in higher gauge theory work by Tijana Radenkovic and Marko Vojinovic. A key peer-reviewed foundation is *Higher gauge theories based on 3-groups*, JHEP 10 (2019) 222, published 2019-10-22: <https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP10(2019)222>. The current decision-bearing extension is Pavle Stipsic and Marko Vojinovic, *A 3BF model of quantum gravity coupled to Standard Model matter*, submitted 2026-02-27 and updated 2026-03-02: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23661>. Intermediate peer-reviewed steps include *The 3BF theory as a TQFT*, J. Phys. A 58 (2025) 345203; *Symmetry breaking mechanisms of the 3BF action for the Standard Model coupled to gravity*, J. Phys. A 58 (2025) 215201; and *Correspondence between 3BF and Einstein-Cartan formulations of quantum gravity*, Class. Quantum Grav. 42 (2025) 195009.

**Proposal.** The framework replaces an ordinary gauge group by a 3-group and uses constrained 3BF actions to place Einstein-Cartan gravity, Yang-Mills fields, scalar fields and Dirac/Weyl/Majorana matter in a common higher-categorical gauge language. The 2026 paper goes beyond the classical reformulation by constructing an explicit quantum model on piecewise-flat spacetime, with a discretized action and path-integral measure for gravity coupled to Standard-Model matter.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is a real multi-paper research programme with peer-reviewed mathematical and physical development. It has a defined TQFT sector, an explicit analysis of gauge symmetry and constraints, a treatment of electroweak spontaneous symmetry breaking and Higgs/Proca sectors, and a quantum correspondence to Einstein-Cartan gravity. The 2026 construction is unusually concrete about the nonperturbative path integral rather than advertising a quantum completion without specifying the measure. The programme therefore clears the Arena's gravity-plus-Standard-Model scope threshold and has substantially more technical maturity than most new self-published unification proposals.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The 2026 paper itself calls the semiclassical analysis preliminary. The decisive recovery problem is therefore not closed: the discrete quantum theory still has to demonstrate a controlled route to observed low-energy gravity and Standard-Model phenomenology. There is also a compression problem. Much of the known Standard Model is encoded in the constrained 3BF description rather than derived from fewer physical assumptions; the higher-categorical structure has not yet explained the observed values of masses, mixings and couplings. Empirical discrimination is still thin. The 2025 Einstein-Cartan correspondence identifies possible differences in 4-volume-density and gravitational-wave observables, but the programme does not yet have a comparably sharp, externally tested prediction.

**Placement.** **B scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** B credits the peer-reviewed lineage, explicit matter+gravity formalism and nonperturbative quantum construction. It stays below A because low-energy recovery and empirical discrimination remain incomplete and the framework currently reformulates more Standard-Model structure than it explains. Interest A reflects the unusually rich higher-categorical route to quantizing realistic matter together with gravity.

**Clash — Loop Quantum Gravity / spin-foam programmes.** Battleground: nonperturbative quantum geometry **with realistic matter**. LQG has much deeper development of quantum geometry, canonical/spinfoam machinery and gravitational observables; 3BF's concrete advantage is that matter and Standard-Model gauge structure are built into the higher-gauge architecture from the start rather than appended as a secondary sector. 3BF's liability is that its semiclassical/phenomenological recovery is less mature and its particle parameters remain largely imported. A controlled continuum limit that reproduces GR plus chiral Standard-Model observables, together with a prediction not already encoded by input data, would materially strengthen 3BF. This clash does not move LQG's tier.

**Movement history.** First Arena entry on 2026-09-23 at B/A. This is a backlog correction prompted by the 2026 explicit quantum model; the programme's roots and peer-reviewed intermediate steps are recorded rather than treating it as a brand-new theory.

## Entered: SO(2,16) Gauge Unification — B scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Origin / dates / sources.** This line develops gauge-theoretic unification by enlarging the tangent-space symmetry while retaining four spacetime dimensions. Roumelioti, Stefas and Zoupanos published *Unification of conformal gravity and internal interactions* in European Physical Journal C 84, 577 on 2024-06-06: <https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-024-12949-6>. The recent consolidation is Stelios Stefas and George Zoupanos, *Unification of Gravity with Internal Interactions: the SO(2,16) Scheme Revisited*, submitted 2026-08-25: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24165>. A 2026 review/preprint line also develops conformal and fuzzy/noncommutative gravity inside the same SO(2,16) parent structure.

**Proposal.** The framework gauges an enlarged tangent-space group SO(2,16) that can contain a gravity gauge sector and SO(10) internal interactions simultaneously. The conformal branch starts from SO(2,4), while a fuzzy/noncommutative branch uses a related gauge construction. Spontaneous symmetry breaking is intended to recover Einstein or Weyl gravity and descend through SO(10) toward Standard-Model gauge structure. The programme also studies fermion embeddings, chiral constraints, dark-sector possibilities and GUT/cosmic-string phenomenology.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is not a group-name-only unification sketch. The peer-reviewed 2024 work explicitly develops the symmetry-breaking mechanism and fermion content and shows how Einstein gravity can arise after breaking. The broader programme has a multi-year technical literature on gauge and noncommutative gravity. The 2026 revisit works through spontaneous-breaking chains and low-energy implications, including possible stochastic/cosmic-string gravitational-wave signatures and dark-matter candidates. Relative to many Arena C/D proposals, it has markedly stronger contact with established GUT technology and published field-theory machinery.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The gravitational side remains the central blocker. Higher-derivative/conformal gravity carries the well-known ghost/unitarity problem, and the 2026 authors explicitly list the ghost problem as an open issue rather than a solved UV completion. The fuzzy/noncommutative branch still needs a convincing controlled bridge to continuum low-energy gravity. On the particle side, adopting SO(10) supplies substantial Standard-Model structure instead of deriving it from the SO(2,16) principle itself; flavor, couplings and breaking scales are not uniquely predicted. Proposed proton-decay, gravitational-wave and dark-sector signals have not supplied empirical confirmation of the unification mechanism.

**Placement.** **B scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** B reflects a serious, peer-reviewed and technically developed unification programme with explicit gravity+internal-interaction scope. It stays below A because the ghost/unitarity problem and the fuzzy-gravity recovery problem are load-bearing, while empirical discrimination remains prospective. Interest A reflects the clean four-dimensional gauge-unification idea and concrete phenomenology.

**Clash — Holomorphic Unified Field Theory.** Battleground: four-dimensional geometry/gauge unification with realistic matter. SO(2,16) has the stronger conventional gauge/GUT lineage, explicit symmetry-breaking machinery and independent peer-reviewed ancestry; Holomorphic UFT reaches more directly into claimed Standard-Model masses, mixings and finite quantum behavior. SO(2,16)'s liability is the conformal-gravity ghost problem and reliance on an SO(10) GUT input; Holomorphic UFT's liability is thinner independent scrutiny and disputed structural claims. A genuinely unitary UV treatment plus a derived, successful low-energy prediction would move SO(2,16) closer to A. No existing Holomorphic UFT tier move follows from this comparison.

**Movement history.** First Arena entry on 2026-09-23 at B/A. The recent August 2026 paper is treated as a consolidation of an already peer-reviewed programme, not as independent evidence by itself.
