### Spatial Unit Conservation and Dynamic Reorganization — C/S → F/S, high confidence

**Source/date.** Hongliang Qian & Yixuan Qian, *Spatial Unit Conservation and Dynamic Reorganization: A Unified Framework of Gravity, Cosmology and Quantum Discreteness*, Preprints.org Version 12, submitted 2026-04-24 and posted 2026-04-28; non-peer-reviewed.

Primary: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202602.1314

**Proposal.** The theory models spacetime as a Planck-scale graph `G=(V,E)` carrying a complex node field and builds its broad unification claims around three named axioms: Covariance, Invariance and Shielding. The manuscript claims recovery or explanation of quantum randomness, particle quantum numbers, gauge structure, gravity, cosmology, galaxy dynamics and several prospective signatures.

**New load-bearing finding.** Version 12 defines the Shielding Principle as the mechanism that reconciles microscopic quantum fluctuations with relativistic/macroscopic determinism. It states that an event lasting proper time `τ` in its rest frame is observed in a relatively moving frame for `τ/γ`, and therefore becomes instantaneous as `v→c`. This is the opposite of special-relativistic time dilation for two events occurring at the same position in the rest frame. The proper interval is the shortest elapsed time; a relatively moving inertial observer measures

`Δt = γ Δτ`,

not `Δτ/γ`.

Standard reference: https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/5-3-time-dilation

Because Shielding is one of the theory's three fundamental axioms and is explicitly used to explain why Planck-scale proliferation, vacuum energy and emergent randomness are hidden from macroscopic observers, this is not an isolated notation error. The advertised shielding mechanism does not follow from Lorentz kinematics as written, and downstream claims that depend on it need to be rederived after any correction.

**Evidence for / retained strengths.** The programme remains unusually explicit for an independent ToE proposal: it gives a concrete discrete substrate, named axioms, extensive equations, claimed derivations of spin/color/charge and a Weinberg-angle boundary condition, spin-network/LQG connections, galaxy fits, gravitational-wave-echo claims and stated failure conditions. Version 12 also candidly identifies several calibrated rather than first-principles quantities and unfinished derivations. Those features preserve high investigative value even though they do not rescue the current scientific formulation.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** In addition to the relativity contradiction, there is no located independent reproduction of the load-bearing topological, Standard-Model or gravitational derivations. Version 12 already narrows the earlier Version 5 impression that all Standard Model parameters were first-principles outputs: charged-lepton eigenvalues are calibrated, composite masses are out of scope, quantum constraint closure is unproven and a complete dynamical derivation of the gravitational formula remains pending. The Milky Way `Gaia+VERA 2024` fit also has unresolved data-product provenance in the manuscript appendix.

**Placement.** `scientific_tier: F`, `interest_tier: S`, confidence high. F applies to the current Version-12 broad formulation because a foundational axiom uses an experimentally established relativistic transformation in the wrong direction. S interest remains because the model offers a rare density of concrete cross-sector claims and therefore a strong surface for adversarial tests. This is not a statement that discrete-spacetime programmes in general are false.

**Recovery gate.** A future version could leave F if it (1) replaces the Shielding mechanism with Lorentz-consistent kinematics, (2) explicitly identifies which results depended on the old formula, and (3) rederives those results rather than merely editing the displayed equation. Independent reconstruction would still be needed for a move toward B.

**Clash — Chronon Field Theory (F/S).** Battleground: foundational mathematical consistency before broad unification claims are credited. Qian has a wider numerical/predictive surface but presently fails a load-bearing special-relativity gate. Chronon has a narrower peer-reviewed gravity/U(1) lineage, while its broad unification manuscript fails a load-bearing topology gate by assigning non-trivial homotopy to real `H^3`. Both remain highly interesting, but neither broad formulation should receive maturity credit until its foundational contradiction is repaired. This clash moves Qian C→F; it does not move Chronon.
