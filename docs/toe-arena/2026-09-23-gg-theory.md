# ToE Arena scan — 2026-09-23 — Grand Geometric Theory

This incremental scan applies `franklinbaldo/skills/toe-arena-tiering`: scientific strength and interest are separate comparative judgements, never probabilities that a theory is true.

## Entered: Grand Geometric Theory (GG-Theory) — F scientific / S interest, low confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Katsushi Arisaka, UCLA. UCLA independently lists Arisaka as a professor in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Physics & Astronomy: <https://www.ee.ucla.edu/katsushi-arisaka/>. The first GG-Theory Zenodo wave located in this audit was released on 2026-08-22/23; the current programme index lists 25 DOI-registered preprints and source releases: <https://preprints.arisaka-gg.org/>. The decision-bearing foundation paper for this audit is *GG-Theory with GG-6*, v25.1, dated 2026-09-11, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22064298: <https://preprints.arisaka-gg.org/a2-gg6/>.

**Proposal.** GG-Theory treats observed 4D physics as a projection of a six-dimensional bulk whose extra coordinates are scale and gauge phase rather than additional spatial directions. Its current construction combines a `Spin(1,5)` geometric sector with `SU(5)`, gauged baryon number and an additional phase/information sector, plus a modified Bianchi identity and a fixed integer ledger `(1,3,5;7,35)`. The programme claims that this common structure can generate quantum mechanics, gravity, the Standard Model and its parameters, cosmology, dark matter and new particle states with no continuously adjustable fit parameters.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is not a one-page verbal proposal. The public corpus is large, source-released and organized around explicit dependencies and kill criteria. The Standard-Model paper, *The Standard Model as Theorem of GG-Theory*, explicitly distinguishes what it claims is forced, what requires an additional named step and what remains open; it also states that the Higgs placement is chosen for economy rather than forced: <https://preprints.arisaka-gg.org/a9-sm/>. Most unusually, the particle branch makes a genuinely sharp collider bet. *The GG-6 Lock Cluster* fixes a mass near **3.849 TeV**, gives the color-octet component `sigma × BR(ttbar) = 8.7 fb` at 13 TeV, `Gamma/m = 0.146`, a fixed interference sign and a stated null-test retirement criterion: <https://preprints.arisaka-gg.org/p1-kk/>.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The strongest problem is not merely lack of peer review. The current lock-cluster paper itself reports that the electroweak members of the same tower give an oblique **T parameter of 1.7**, while the fit quoted in the paper is approximately `0.00 ± 0.06`, and it states that no custodial symmetry is available. Independent electroweak-fit machinery likewise constrains T close to zero; for example Gfitter reports `T = 0.05 ± 0.12` with S,T,U all free and `T = 0.02 ± 0.05` for U fixed to zero: <https://project-gfitter.web.cern.ch/Oblique_Parameters/>. Unless a complete calculation supplies a compensating contribution or demonstrates that the oblique treatment is inapplicable, the current implementation carries a severe precision-electroweak contradiction. The same P1 paper is admirably explicit that this is an exposure of the model rather than something to hide.

There is a second, separate maturity problem. Every GG-Theory paper located in this scan is authored by Arisaka and released through the project/Zenodo preprint corpus; I located no peer-reviewed GG-Theory paper or independent end-to-end reconstruction of the six-dimensional geometry to Standard Model plus gravity chain. Many numerical agreements concern quantities known before the 2026 papers were written, so they are retrospective consistency checks rather than prospective confirmations. The A9 Standard-Model paper also narrows its own headline: the Higgs assignment is not forced, the strong-CP choice is delegated to a companion relaxation mechanism, and some of the advertised 28-parameter chain remains open.

**Placement.** **F scientific / S interest, low confidence.** The F is deliberately about the **current published implementation**, not a declaration that every six-dimensional geometric-unification idea is impossible. Under the Arena rubric, F is appropriate when the proposal as presented is in direct conflict with strong evidence; the programme's own `T = 1.7` result provides exactly such a case unless an omitted cancellation is demonstrated. Low placement confidence reflects the size and rapid versioning of the corpus and the fact that this audit did not reconstruct all 7,000+ pages. The S interest tier is independent: a broad programme that publishes source, dependency structure and an unusually specific collider prediction is an excellent adversarial-test target even while its present scientific status is poor.

**Clash — Holomorphic Unified Field Theory.** Battleground: geometry -> gravity + full Standard Model + parameter-level predictions. GG-Theory's concrete advantage is falsifiability: the 3.849 TeV lock cluster specifies mass, width, rate, interference sign and a retirement condition, and the broader corpus attempts a far larger set of parameter-level claims. Its liability is decisive at present: founder-only evidence plus the self-reported electroweak-precision conflict. Holomorphic UFT remains **B/A** because it has a peer-reviewed multi-author lineage and explicit Standard-Model calculations without a comparable current precision contradiction, although its independent uptake and claimed predictivity remain limited. The clash does not warrant moving Holomorphic UFT.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on 2026-09-23 at F/S. This is an entry, not a relegation from a previous letter tier. An independent electroweak reconstruction that removes the T conflict from the already frozen rules, without adding post-hoc tunable freedom, would justify reconsidering the F and could move the programme initially toward C. Independent reproduction of a load-bearing GG-6 -> SM/GR derivation plus a successful prospective prediction would be required for B. Conversely, exclusion of the frozen 3.849 TeV mass/rate/shape package under the theory's own stated acceptance assumptions would independently reinforce F.

## Fresh candidates screened in the same pass

- **Jennifer Lorraine Nielsen, *The Complex Hopf Fibration as the Canonical Space for Gauge–Gravity Unification*, v5 (2026-07-17).** This is a real full-scope claim: it tries to derive `U(1)`, `SU(2)`, `SU(3)`, gravity, masses and mixing from the universal complex Hopf fibration and a Beltrami spectral construction. It remains on the dedicated-audit queue rather than receiving a letter from discovery-level inspection. The key gate is whether the mathematical classifying-space theorem really entails the physical premise that the universe's unified field space itself must be `BU(1)=CP^infinity`, and whether the subsequent `S^3/S^5` shell identifications reproduce the observed chiral Standard Model rather than relabel familiar groups. Primary source: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202604.0315>.
- **Alexandre Harvey-Tremblay, *The Least Surprising Theory*, v29 (2026-09-02).** The current paper derives a Dirac/Yang–Mills-type action from an information-theoretic least-surprisal construction. The accessible current formulation is interesting foundations work but does not yet demonstrate an end-to-end Einstein-gravity plus realistic Standard-Model recovery sufficient for a canonical ToE rank. Primary source: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202404.1009>.
- **Hamada & Yamatsu, *A simple solution to the monopole problem: SU(5) GUT with symmetry breaking into special subgroup*, JHEP, published 2026-09-18.** Serious, fresh peer-reviewed GUT work, but it is a gauge/matter unification result rather than a gravity-plus-Standard-Model theory. Screened out on Arena scope, not scientific quality: <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP09%282026%29210>.
- **Mohamed Samir, Minimum Topological Action Principle, 2026-09-04.** The working paper connects a scalar/topological stability principle to cosmological fractions and the fine-structure constant, but the current source does not derive the complete chiral Standard Model and gravitational quantum dynamics needed for canonical ToE ranking. Screened as adjacent rather than assigned a scientific letter.
