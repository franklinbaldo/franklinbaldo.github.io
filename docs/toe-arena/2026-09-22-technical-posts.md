## Observer-Dependent Theory of Everything (ODTOE) — enters D/A, medium confidence

**Source and date:** Anton Pankratov, *Observer-Dependent Theory of Everything (ODTOE): A Formal Metatheory of Reality Founded on the Observer as the Principal Constructor of the Universe*, published on odtoe.org on **2026-02-01**. The site currently presents a large linked corpus of observer-centred articles, including a March 2026 Standard-Model reinterpretation.

**Proposal:** ODTOE starts from `R = Ô(Ψ)`: observed reality is constituted by an observation operator acting on a field of possible states. The foundational article adds six postulates, an observer state parametrized by contextual belief/coherence, a Langevin-style reconfiguration dynamics and propositions about convergence/divergence of compatible law-sets. The broader programme says quantum mechanics, GR, the Standard Model and other physical theories should arise as configurations of this observer-dependent metatheory.

**Evidence for:** this is an explicit formal programme with a sizeable open corpus rather than a one-line observer slogan. The foundational paper states its variables and acknowledges limitations including the unspecified central functional, operationalization of contextual belief and free parameters. Those admissions give the programme a concrete failure/development surface.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** the present corpus does not yet quantitatively derive established field equations, chiral gauge representations, particle spectra and precision observables from the central `R = Ô(Ψ)` formalism. Key observer variables such as contextual belief/coherence do not yet have independently established physical measurement procedures, which creates substantial underdetermination risk. The corpus is independently published, without independent technical replication or programme-specific empirical confirmation.

**Placement:** **D scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** D matches an early but formalized hypothesis whose physics recovery is still predominantly programmatic/reinterpretive. A interest reflects the useful observer-centred contrast without upgrading the evidence.

**Clash — Observer Patch Holography:** both make observer structure fundamental, but OPH currently has a much stronger finite theorem/certificate surface and explicit Standard-Model/gravity reconstruction obligations. ODTOE makes subjective/contextual observer variables much more central and must show that they can generate conventional physical observables without becoming flexible fit parameters. OPH remains B/S.

**History:** 2026-09-22 enters D/A as a technical-post/backlog correction; the foundational programme dates to February 2026 rather than today.

Primary source: https://odtoe.org/articles/full-article

## Screened / held from this pass

- **Zhengpeng Li, “Unified Field Theory under the Quantum Information Geometric Duality Framework and its Quantum Realization” — APS Global Physics Summit, 2026-03-17.** The conference abstract makes a broad quantum-gravity/unification claim and reports quantum-computer simulations and apparent observational agreements, but this pass did not locate a primary technical paper sufficient to reconstruct the claimed field equations, simulation protocol, statistical comparisons and full Standard-Model recovery. **Held without tier** pending a citable technical manuscript/repository. An APS presentation slot is provenance, not independent validation.

- **Brettell et al., “Probing Matter Unification through Higgs Physics at the LHC” — arXiv:2609.23708, submitted 2026-09-20 and updated 2026-09-22.** This is fresh and technically relevant to unification, but it studies low-scale quark-lepton **matter** unification and collider phenomenology, not gravity-plus-Standard-Model unification. **Screened on scope**, not scientific quality.
