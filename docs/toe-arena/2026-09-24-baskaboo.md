**Proposal.** The physical branch of Baskaboo organizes reality through four recurring roles — Pits, Mits, Klop and Laram — and a `Flip → Square → Flipback → Root` cycle. Version 3 proposes a closed information circuit `Quantum → Atomic → Classical → Atomic → Quantum`, assigning the atom a unique mediator/transformer role, with bound/free electrons, proton and neutron mapped to stages of the cycle. A “Triple Equation” equates products built from labels such as Quantum Field, Wavefunction, Electron, Proton, Energy, Matter, Space and Time. A later four-force document maps electromagnetism, strong force, gravity and weak force onto the four roles and proposes a balance relation `EM × Weak² = Strong × Gravity²`, supplemented by a claimed holographic correction. The experimental manifesto proposes a separate physical hypothesis for structured decoherence: `I_quantum = I_classical²`, plus a cumulative phase-remnant criterion over at least `10^4` cycles.

**Scope.** The corpus reaches well beyond a normal quantum-foundations proposal: it claims bridges among quantum/classical physics, the four fundamental interactions, cosmology, information physics and consciousness. That breadth makes it relevant to a ToE discovery screen, even though the project simultaneously disclaims status as a scientific theory.

**Evidence for / strengths.** Baskaboo is unusually inspectable for a philosophical-to-physical programme: it exposes a large public corpus, version history, an explicit laboratory challenge, named failure criteria and code/simulation material. The experimental manifesto also does something scientifically useful in principle: it distinguishes a proposed empirical relation from a direct derivation and says that a sufficiently clear negative result should kill the physical hypothesis. This creates more adversarial surface than a purely metaphorical manifesto.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The central physical mappings are asserted rather than derived. `Wavefunction → Free Electron`, `Bound Electron → Energy`, the atom as the unique quantum/classical mediator, and the identifications of the four forces with the four symbolic roles are not obtained from a field theory, Hilbert-space dynamics, symmetry principle or controlled effective limit. The Triple Equation treats heterogeneous physical labels as multiplicative quantities without operational definitions or dimensional analysis, so it cannot yet be evaluated as a physical equation.

The four-force relation is especially weak as explanatory compression. The corpus itself calculates a mismatch of roughly `10^64` between its two sides using rough force-strength orders and then introduces a `10^64` “holographic correction” to make them equal. In the present derivation that correction is not independently forced before the mismatch is known, so the numerical closure is post-hoc rather than a prediction.

The proposed decoherence test is more concrete, but it is still under-specified. Quantum mutual information is defined from von Neumann entropies for a specified bipartite state/channel, while the Baskaboo relation does not specify the state preparation, bipartition, classical random variables/channel, normalization or estimator needed to make `I_quantum` and `I_classical` comparable. Mutual information also depends on the logarithm base used to express information; a literal squared equality changes under a bits↔nats rescaling unless a preferred convention/reference scale or compensating coefficient is part of the physical law. The current protocol therefore does not yet define an invariant quantitative observable. See IBM/Qiskit’s definition and explicit `base` parameter: <https://docs.quantum.ibm.com/api/qiskit/quantum_info>.

The programme also does not recover a realistic chiral `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)` matter sector, anomaly cancellation, three generations, Higgs/Yukawa structure, CKM/PMNS, or Einstein dynamics from the four-role cycle. AI-generated “audit” or praise embedded in the corpus is not counted as independent scientific scrutiny.

**Provisional placement.** **F scientific / A interest, high confidence for the present physical formulation.** Scientific **F** applies because the physical core is not yet specified as a coherent gravity-plus-Standard-Model theory and its flagship quantitative relations are either operationally undefined or closed by an after-the-fact scale factor. This is a judgement about the current formulation, not about the value of metaphorical/systemic uses of the framework. Interest is **A** on a separate axis because the corpus is broad, active, unusually explicit about what it wants tested, and rich in concrete claims that can be subjected to adversarial dimensional, information-theoretic and experimental audits. No canonical `knowledge/toe` card is created in this pass because the project’s own public description disclaims being a scientific theory and the physical sub-programme has not yet crossed the minimum specification/recovery gate.

**Clash — Grand Geometric Theory (F/S).** Battleground: whether an unconventional all-domain programme exposes a derivation chain that can actually fail. GG-Theory is also scientifically F in its present Arena classification, but for a very different reason: it publishes a detailed gravity+Standard-Model construction and sharp collider/precision predictions, one of which currently creates a load-bearing electroweak conflict. Baskaboo has no comparably complete gravity+SM recovery chain; its advantage is a simpler and highly generative conceptual/test vocabulary, while its liability is that the quantities and mappings required for the physical claims are not yet derived or operationally closed. GG-Theory’s liability is empirical tension inside a much more specified model. The clash does **not** move GG-Theory from F/S.

**Recovery / promotion gate.** Reconsideration should require: (1) isolate a physical core from the broader philosophical/consciousness architecture; (2) define fields/states, observables, units and dynamics; (3) make the Triple Equation and four-force relation dimensionally and operationally well-defined without a correction chosen to close the observed mismatch; (4) specify the decoherence experiment as a complete state/channel/measurement protocol with a unit-invariant prediction and preregistered statistical analysis; (5) recover at least a controlled GR sector and realistic Standard-Model gauge/matter structure; and (6) obtain independent reproduction of at least one load-bearing derivation or prospective prediction.
