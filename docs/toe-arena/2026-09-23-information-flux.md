## Information Flux Theory — enters C / interest S, low confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Yoshinori Shimizu. The first public Preprints.org version was posted on 2025-05-14. The current decision-bearing version is v9, submitted 2026-05-19 and posted 2026-05-27: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202505.1122>. A Zenodo branch of the programme was published as version 1.6 on 2026-01-10: <https://zenodo.org/records/15399115/latest>. A separate external critical-propositional discussion by Vidamor Cabannas and Denivaldo Silva was published on 2026-07-08: <https://zenodo.org/records/21251839>.

**Proposal.** IFT derives a single-fermion framework from a Unified Evolution Equation and attempts to replace the Standard Model field inventory with one fundamental operator/current structure. The programme claims to preserve gauge symmetry, generate particle generations and mass hierarchies, reproduce the Standard Model mass spectrum including the Higgs and CKM structure without adjustable parameters, and connect color-sector tension to gravity. Across the version history it also extends into cosmology, black-hole information, vacuum energy and claims of proofs for the Yang-Mills mass gap and Navier-Stokes regularity.

**Scope.** Unlike many adjacent unification proposals, IFT explicitly claims both particle-physics and gravity coverage and therefore passes the Arena's scope gate as a ToE contender rather than an adjacent quantum-gravity or GUT-only entry.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The corpus is mathematically and operationally more explicit than a manifesto: it supplies operator equations, a conserved-current framework, generation/projector machinery, exponential mass relations and a proposed gravity construction. It makes sharp quantitative claims instead of stopping at ontology. The public version chain also makes the evolution of claims traceable. An external July 2026 paper demonstrates that the proposal has at least entered outside technical discussion, although that paper is not an independent replication.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The current paper is explicitly a non-peer-reviewed preprint. This audit found no independent reproduction of the central parameter-free particle-spectrum/mixing claims or the emergent-gravity derivation. The proposal carries an unusually high proof burden because it combines complete Standard-Model replacement, gravity and claims to settle two Millennium problems. Those claims are evidence only after independent checking, not because they are numerous. The proposed identification tying color/QCD tension to Newton's constant is load-bearing and needs an independent dimensional, normalization and renormalization audit plus recovery of precision gravity. Existing mass/mixing agreements are not yet prospective discriminators.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, low confidence.** C fits a concrete and broad mathematical proposal with a large attackable surface but weak independent scrutiny, absent reproduction and unresolved load-bearing derivations. S interest reflects the unusually aggressive explanatory compression, quantitative targets and many ways the programme could be stress-tested. Interest S does not raise the scientific tier.

**Clash — Holomorphic Unified Field Theory.** Battleground: low-energy Standard-Model-plus-gravity recovery. Holomorphic UFT currently has a stronger multi-paper and peer-reviewed technical lineage and a more conventional path to independent checking. IFT claims more radical one-field compression and stronger parameter-free mass/mixing recovery. An independent end-to-end reproduction of IFT's mass matrices or its gravity-normalization chain, followed by a genuinely prospective prediction, would narrow the gap. On current evidence the clash does **not** move Holomorphic UFT from B/A or justify IFT above C.

**Distinctive tests / promotion gate.** The most useful next evidence is an external reconstruction of one load-bearing calculation from stated inputs only, with no hidden fit choices, followed by a prediction frozen before new data. Failure of the claimed parameter-free mass/mixing reconstruction or of the gravity-coupling derivation would materially weaken the programme; successful independent reproduction would support reconsideration toward B.

**Movement history.** The 2026-09-23 broad scan left IFT on the watchlist because discovery snippets alone were insufficient. This dedicated primary-source pass moves it from watchlist to the canonical Arena corpus at C/S, low confidence. This is a cataloguing decision, not a scientific promotion from a prior tier.
