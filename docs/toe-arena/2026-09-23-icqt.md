# ToE Arena incremental audit — Information-Complete Quantum Theory — 2026-09-23

Applied rubric: `franklinbaldo/skills/toe-arena-tiering`. Scientific tier measures comparative technical/evidential maturity; interest tier measures generative value. Neither is a probability that a theory is true.

## Entered: Information-Complete Quantum Theory (ICQT / ICQFT) — B scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Zeng-Bing Chen. The programme begins publicly with *The information-complete quantum theory* in 2014. A 9+1-dimensional all-forces-and-matter proposal followed in 2016 and was substantially revised in 2018. A peer-reviewed ICQT formalism appeared in 2022; *Quantum entanglement dynamics of spacetime and matter* was published in *Fundamental Research*; and the most recent consolidation is **“Universal Entanglement and an Information-Complete Quantum Theory,” Advanced Physics Research 5 (2026) e00063**, version of record first published **2026-01-30** and issue-online 2026-04-09.

Decision-bearing sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1002/apxr.202500063 — 2026 peer-reviewed perspective and current programme status;
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fmre.2023.10.004 — peer-reviewed ICQFT spacetime–matter dynamics;
- https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9203196 — peer-reviewed ICQT formalism;
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02662 — 9+1-dimensional all-forces-and-matter unification proposal, v2 2018-12-19;
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.1079 — original ICQT formulation.

**Proposal.** ICQT starts from an information-completeness principle: physical predictions should be encoded entirely in quantum relations rather than requiring classical observers or external measuring concepts. This leads to a trinary picture in which matter fermions, their Yang–Mills gauge fields and quantized spacetime/gravity form an indivisible system. Universal spacetime–matter entanglement programs the matter sector; a second matter–gauge entanglement completes the description. The gravity implementation uses canonical/LQG-style variables and spin-network structure. A separate 9+1-dimensional construction attempts to restrict the matter content through entanglement-driven symmetry breaking `SO(9,1) -> SO(3,1) × SO(6)`, a family-space `SO(3)`, gauge–Higgs unification and three fermion families.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is a genuine research programme rather than a newly posted manifesto. It has persisted for more than a decade, developed in several technically explicit papers and crossed peer review in quantum foundations and quantum-gravity venues. The framework does not merely couple a classical metric to quantum matter: gravity itself is part of the quantum information-complete trinity. The spacetime–matter paper develops entanglement/geometry relations and a route to the classical Einstein equation and cosmological-constant term. The 10D proposal also tries to explain family multiplicity and gauge/Higgs structure instead of treating all Standard-Model matter as arbitrary input.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The decisive matter bridge remains incomplete by the programme's own current wording. The 2016/2018 synopsis says that **possible routes** to the Standard Model are discussed, and the 2026 perspective calls the 3+1-dimensional effective theory **promising** for reproducing its main features. That is materially weaker than an end-to-end derivation of the observed `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)` representations and hypercharges, anomaly cancellation, Higgs/Yukawa sector, CKM/PMNS, measured masses/couplings and running. The necessity of the particular 9+1D Kaluza–Klein realization is also not independently established from the information-completeness principle.

Independent scrutiny is another limiter. The programme has peer-reviewed publications, which matters, but the load-bearing ICQT-to-gravity-and-matter derivation remains overwhelmingly single-author; I found no independent end-to-end reconstruction and no observation uniquely selecting ICQT. Several proposed consequences concern cosmological constant/dark energy or black-hole information and currently lack a sharp near-term discriminator that would distinguish the framework from competitors.

**Placement.** **B scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** B follows the rubric's `serious contender` category: coherent mathematical structure, a long peer-reviewed lineage, an explicit quantum-gravity-plus-matter programme and identifiable low-energy targets. It remains below A because realistic Standard-Model closure, independent reconstruction and theory-specific empirical confirmation are not in place. A interest reflects the broad usefulness of universal entanglement and information-completeness as a unification principle, but the Arena does not equate that fecundity with evidence that the theory is true.

**Clash — From Loop Quantum Gravity to a Theory of Everything (C/A).** Battleground: **LQG-style quantum geometry -> realistic matter + gravity unification**. ICQT has a much longer and more peer-reviewed development history, a stronger explicit matter-selection principle and a concrete higher-dimensional family/gauge proposal. Adrian Lim's 2026 construction has a compact Chern–Simons/Wilson-loop route and cleaner recency as a dedicated LQG-to-ToE proposal, but it is far less mature and likewise lacks end-to-end realistic Standard-Model phenomenology. The comparison supports ICQT's B versus Lim's C but does **not** justify moving Lim's existing tier.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at B/A, medium confidence. This is an omission correction driven by the 2026 peer-reviewed perspective and the discovery that the underlying programme already has a decade-long technical lineage; it is not a promotion from a prior tier.
