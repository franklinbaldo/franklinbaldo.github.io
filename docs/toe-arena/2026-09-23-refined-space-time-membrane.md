## Entered: Refined Space-Time Membrane Model (RSTM) — C scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Paul Swann's original *The Space-Time Membrane Model: Unifying Quantum Mechanics and General Relativity through Elastic Membrane Dynamics* v1 was submitted 2024-11-09 and posted **2024-11-13**: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202411.0967/v1>. The explicitly refined line began with *The Refined Space-Time Membrane Model: Deterministic Emergence of Quantum Fields and Gravity from Classical Elasticity* v1, posted **2025-03-11**: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202503.0736/v1>. The decision-bearing source is **v7, posted 2026-01-21**, still marked non-peer-reviewed: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202503.0736>.

**Proposal.** RSTM treats four-dimensional spacetime as a classical elastic membrane coupled to a mirror domain. Its current master equation is an eight-parameter higher-derivative PDE with `nabla^4` and `nabla^6` terms, damping, nonlinear self-interaction and fermionic coupling, with coefficients anchored to `c`, `G`, `alpha`, `Lambda` and Planck scales. A bimodal decomposition of the displacement field is used to construct an effective spinor; local internal rephasings are then associated with `U(1) x SU(2) x SU(3)`-type gauge structure. Coarse-graining rapid oscillations is claimed to yield Schrodinger-like envelopes and Born-compatible statistics, while the transverse-traceless sector is claimed to recover Einstein-Lambda dynamics in the infrared and higher-derivative dispersion in the ultraviolet. The same programme extends to black-hole cores, flavour mixing and SMEFT/collider observables.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The current paper is not merely an elastic analogy. It contains one explicit PDE, dispersion relations, path-integral/WKB bridges, BRST/GKSL constructions, RG and exploratory FRG calculations, a black-hole solitonic-core programme, CKM/PMNS numerical fits and a minimal SMEFT bridge. It is also unusually explicit about where its own strongest claims stop: v7 says flavour is a consistency check rather than a full derivation and leaves a full multi-field FRG derivation of the absolute mass spectrum to future work. The theory supplies several concrete attack surfaces — laboratory membrane/metamaterial interference, gravitational-wave dispersion/ringdown, short-range gravity and collider/EFT observables — which makes it scientifically useful even before those channels become unique predictions.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The most important issue is explanatory compression. The eight coefficients are calibrated to known constants including `c`, `G`, `alpha` and `Lambda`; the gravity section says the Einstein-Lambda infrared limit follows under **standard spin-2 bootstrap assumptions** and uses the Einstein-Hilbert action as a compact infrared packaging of the strain-metric correspondence; other parts introduce canonical quantisation and obtain gauge fields by requiring local phase invariance. Those moves can produce a coherent effective model, but they weaken the stronger claim that GR, quantum structure and gauge symmetry have all been derived from classical elasticity without importing their characteristic machinery.

The Standard-Model claim is also incomplete. The paper obtains gauge-like `U(1) x SU(2) x SU(3)` structures and a vector-like anomaly-cancellation argument in the displayed truncation, while its own future-work list still asks for a chiral embedding with explicit electroweak charges and zero-mode spectrum. Nonperturbative confinement/mass gap and Higgs-like unitarisation of longitudinal gauge-boson scattering are also left open. The flavour sector is explicitly phenomenological at this stage: the triple-well/basin picture motivates generation labels, but CKM/PMNS are generated by scanning a three-mode ansatz and priors rather than by a unique FRG output; the CP-phase step is polished to match the measured Jarlskog invariant. The paper itself correctly characterises the result as an ability to reproduce observed patterns, not unique fixing.

Independent scrutiny remains weak. This audit located the author's version sequence and mirrors of the same preprint, but no peer-reviewed validation or independent end-to-end reconstruction of the master-PDE -> quantum statistics + Einstein gravity + realistic chiral Standard Model chain. Consequently the numerical agreements should currently be read as internal consistency and retrospective calibration, not independent confirmation.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** C is appropriate under the Arena rubric because RSTM is a concrete mathematical proposal with substantial internal machinery but weak external validation, incomplete low-energy recovery and important load-bearing assumptions/ansatzes. It is too developed for D, while B would overstate the maturity of the chiral-SM and empirical bridge. S interest is deliberately separate: a single deterministic continuum equation attempting quantum foundations, gauge fields, flavour, gravity, black holes and direct laboratory/astrophysical/collider tests is exceptionally generative and easy to attack. The interest letter is not evidence that the model is likely to be correct.

**Clash — Selective Transient Field (STF), currently C/S.** Battleground: single-substrate field dynamics -> GR + quantum behaviour + realistic Standard Model + discriminating tests. RSTM's concrete advantage is its more explicit one-PDE/operator/EFT scaffold and a direct membrane/metamaterial laboratory route; its liability is that standard spin-2, gauge and quantisation structure re-enters the derivation and flavour is still fitted through phenomenological scans. STF's advantage is a distinctive causal finite-memory/two-clock mechanism plus an unusually visible correction ledger; its liability is a conditional 10D compactification and imported GR/Peters/fermionic structure. Neither programme currently has independent end-to-end reconstruction or a completed chiral-SM derivation. The clash therefore moves **neither** tier.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at C/S. This is an entry, not a promotion from a prior scientific tier. Movement to B would require at minimum: (1) independent reconstruction of a load-bearing derivation from frozen rules; (2) a complete anomaly-safe chiral low-energy spectrum with masses/mixings following from the theory rather than a scan; and (3) at least one prospective discriminating prediction that succeeds against a well-specified alternative. A robust failure of a frozen, load-bearing prediction or a demonstrated inconsistency in the PDE-to-low-energy bridge would move the scientific tier downward.
