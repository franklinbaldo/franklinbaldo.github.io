**Proposal.** Shadow Theory separates a richer source structure from bounded physical readouts. Papers 1–6 develop exact criteria for when a readout loses target-relevant information, what the coarsest sufficient completion must retain, when such completions admit geometric realization, when projected dynamics close autonomously and what an internal observer can identify. Paper 7 instantiates that architecture inside positive-tension Randall–Sundrum gravity: different five-dimensional source states can give identical instantaneous brane readouts yet different future brane evolution. The broader programme treats quantum, geometric, gravitational, matter, cosmological, temporal and observer sectors as coupled readouts of a common realization carrier, but several of those sector derivations remain programme targets rather than results of the canonical foundation.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The main reason this crosses the Arena entry threshold is technical structure. The current sequence is not merely an ontology: it contains explicit definitions and theorems about quotient/readout maps, minimal completion, closure and identifiability. The RS2 witness derives the projected Einstein equation and gives a concrete example of dynamical nonclosure, together with a parameter-free relation linking cosmological and weak-field residues inside that model. The September measurement branch is also unusually explicit about constitutive assumptions: one construction obtains a controlled Bell-path limit with material records, while a separate massive-configuration theory uses its own guidance and equilibrium premises. The project consistently separates internal mathematical closure from external verification.

A second positive is revision discipline. The public history says directly that the older Everything Equation shorthand invited over-reading, and the current sequence does not carry forward the old synthesis/law-packet authority. That makes the historical development scientifically easier to audit rather than harder.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The decisive ToE gap is also stated by the project itself: **Standard-Model gauge group, fermion content and family structure remain an open problem**. Fine-structure constant, dark-sector structure and other historical parameter claims are likewise downstream targets unless rebuilt under the current canonical framework. The RS2 construction is a model-relative witness, not a derivation that Nature must be RS2 or that the abstract source object uniquely yields observed four-dimensional gravity. The quantum-measurement results add physical detail but require extra guidance/preparation/interaction premises that do not follow from source/readout incompleteness alone. I found no independent peer-reviewed end-to-end reconstruction or empirical confirmation of the programme's load-bearing physical chain.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** C matches the rubric's `structured speculation`: there is substantial mathematics, a concrete gravitational witness, branch-level physical constructions and disciplined falsifiability/scope bookkeeping, but the realistic Standard Model and unique source-level dynamics are not derived and independent uptake is weak. S interest is separate: the obstruction/completion language is unusually useful for interrogating other unification programmes and for asking when apparent low-energy closure hides source degrees of freedom.

**Clash — Observer Patch Holography (B/S).** Battleground: **observer/readout limitation -> concrete recovery of physical law**. Shadow Theory's advantage is its theorem-level treatment of quotient loss, minimal completion and internal identifiability, plus a sharply delimited RS2 witness. Its liability is physical incompleteness: the Standard Model is still an explicit open programme target. Observer Patch Holography has a broader direct physics programme that attempts observer spacetime, Einstein dynamics and particle-structure recovery and has received at least some external red-team calculation; its liability is that several bridges remain conditional and its physical claims are more ambitious than what has yet been independently reconstructed. A successful independent Standard-Model derivation or prospective physical discriminator from Shadow Theory would materially narrow the gap. **No existing OPH tier moves from this clash.**
