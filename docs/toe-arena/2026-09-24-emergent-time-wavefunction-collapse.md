## New adjacent entry: Emergent Time from Wavefunction Collapse — NR scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin, dates and primary sources.** Sung-Sik Lee first posted *A theory of time based on wavefunction collapse* on **2024-08-21** (arXiv:2408.11905), later published as *Theory of time based on wave function collapse*, **Physical Review D 112, 064091**, on **2025-09-29**: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11905>. The broader gravitational extension *Emergent time and more from wavefunction collapse in general relativity* was first posted on **2025-10-23** (arXiv:2510.20207) and published in **JHEP 04 (2026) 180** on **2026-04-22**: <https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04(2026)180>. Perimeter Institute has a seminar devoted to the proposal and its extra graviton modes scheduled for **2026-09-24**: <https://world-wide.org/seminar/emergent-time-more-wavefunction-4qv3y9dn>.

### What the programme claims

The proposal attacks the canonical quantum-gravity problem of time by changing the role of the gravitational constraints. Instead of treating every physical state as already annihilated by the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints, it interprets states that violate them as instantaneous moments of time. Stochastic fluctuations of lapse and shift then drive a non-unitary evolution that gradually collapses the state toward diffeomorphism invariance.

In the 2026 extension, the cosmological scale factor grows monotonically during this relaxation and can act as a clock. Scalar, vector and tensor gravitational excitations appear. At long times the tensor modes approach emergent unitary dynamics; vector modes are strongly damped; scalar-mode damping scales with wave number, allowing very long-wavelength scalar excitations to survive and motivating their interpretation as a possible dark-matter sector.

The published demonstration is for a cosmological-constant-dominated universe, using semiclassical and adiabatic approximations whose control improves in the large-spatial-dimension limit.

### Evidence for / strengths

1. **Peer-reviewed technical lineage.** This is not merely a recent speculative post. The core theory of time reached Physical Review D in 2025 and the gravitational extension reached JHEP in 2026.
2. **A concrete dynamical proposal.** The programme specifies what time is supposed to correspond to, what drives the evolution, what the asymptotic state is and how different gravitational modes behave. That makes it substantially more falsifiable than an interpretive slogan about emergent time.
3. **Recovery target.** Tensor gravitons are claimed to recover unitary late-time dynamics even though the fundamental relaxation is non-unitary. Whether this survives a full 3+1-dimensional treatment is a sharp calculation rather than an undefined aspiration.
4. **Phenomenological opening.** The scalar/vector damping pattern and the proposed long-wavelength scalar dark-matter sector provide potential contact with cosmology and gravitational phenomenology.
5. **Current technical attention.** A dedicated Perimeter seminar on 2026-09-24 shows that the construction remains an active object of specialist discussion. This counts as evidence of research activity, not as validation of the claims.

### Evidence against / unresolved

1. **Not a full Theory of Everything.** The current public programme does not derive the Standard Model, its gauge group, chiral matter, generations, Higgs/Yukawa structure or a unified gravity-plus-matter dynamics. This is the reason for `NR`, not a scientific demotion.
2. **The collapse mechanism is postulated.** Stochastic lapse-and-shift fluctuations and the physical non-unitary relaxation are foundational assumptions. The reviewed sources do not derive them from a smaller microscopic principle or show direct experimental evidence for them.
3. **Controlled regime is narrower than our universe.** The JHEP result is demonstrated in a cosmological-constant-dominated background through semiclassical/adiabatic approximations controlled at large spatial dimension. Reproducing the mechanism quantitatively in physical 3+1 dimensions with realistic matter is a load-bearing recovery gate.
4. **Dark-matter contact remains preliminary.** Calling the long-lived scalar a viable dark-matter candidate is an interesting consequence, but this scan did not locate a frozen prediction for abundance, structure formation, lensing or another observable that already distinguishes the model from conventional dark matter.
5. **Independent reconstruction is limited.** The programme has peer review and a broader research context around the problem of time, but the central collapse-to-diffeomorphism-invariance mechanism remains principally the originator's construction rather than an independently reproduced quantitative framework.

### Placement

**Adjacent: NR scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** Under the Arena rubric, ranking it C/B/A as a full ToE would blur scope: it currently solves a narrower quantum-gravity/foundations problem and explores gravitational phenomenology rather than providing gravity plus a realistic Standard Model. `NR` therefore protects the scientific record from a misleading cross-scope comparison.

Interest is **S** because the mechanism links five normally separate questions — gravitational constraints, the problem of time, objective collapse, the arrow of time and extra graviton/dark-matter phenomenology — in a single explicit construction. High interest does not imply high probability of correctness.

### Clash — Loop quantum gravity (A/A)

**Battleground: canonical constraints and the problem of time.**

Lee's programme has the sharper direct answer to the time question: constraint violation is physical time, and stochastic relaxation supplies directed evolution. It also predicts mode-dependent damping. Its liabilities are equally sharp: the non-unitary law is postulated, the controlled calculation is restricted, and there is no Standard-Model recovery.

Loop quantum gravity's advantage is maturity and breadth of independent technical development as a background-independent quantization of geometry, including long-running work on constraints, black holes and cosmology. Its liabilities are the still difficult recovery of smooth low-energy dynamics and the absence of automatic full Standard-Model unification. The existing Arena card remains **A scientific / A interest**.

**What could change the comparison?** For Lee's programme: a controlled 3+1-dimensional derivation with realistic matter, an independently reproduced calculation of the extra-mode spectrum, and a quantitative dark-matter or gravitational signature fixed before comparison with data. For LQG: stronger low-energy recovery and distinctive empirical signatures. The present clash moves **neither** tier.
