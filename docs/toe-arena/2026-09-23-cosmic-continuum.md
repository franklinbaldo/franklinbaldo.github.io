## Entered: Cosmic Continuum Component Model (CCCM) — D scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Xijia Wang's Cosmic Continuum line is publicly traceable to *The Ideal Model on the Cosmic Continuum: A Unified Mechanism on Cosmic and Material Structure*, version 1, posted on **2024-06-29**: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202406.2046/v1>. A later ideal-model formulation appeared in *Applied Physics Research* in 2026 (DOI 10.5539/apr.v18n1p56): <https://www.ccsenet.org/journal/index.php/apr/article/view/0/53076>. The decision-bearing full-scope source for this audit is *A First-Principles Axiomatization of Physics—The Cosmic Continuum Component Model with Its Scale Topos and Braided Tensor Category Realization*, **v4, posted 2026-07-16**, explicitly labelled non-peer-reviewed: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202606.0967>.

**Proposal.** CCCM starts from 15 axioms A0–A14. A0 says every state change arises from energy redistribution; later axioms distinguish mass, energy and a proposed dark-mass sector, define a scale-dependent notion of continuum, package a particle and gauge degree of freedom as a component `C = p ⊗ g`, and introduce a braided/fibred categorical realization using `U_q(e8)`. The programme claims that gauge interactions can arise from fusion/braiding, that a semiclassical limit recovers Einstein and Maxwell dynamics, that quantum probabilities reflect limited access to information in a dark-space sector, and that thermodynamics and cosmology can be organized in the same axiomatic language.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The current paper is no longer merely the earlier continuum ontology: it supplies a named axiom system, scale-topos definitions, braided/fibred category machinery, an exceptional-algebra realization and tables that distinguish different proof statuses. Its advertised scope genuinely crosses the Arena threshold, attempting gravity, quantum theory, Standard-Model gauge structure, thermodynamics and cosmology. It also publishes an attack surface: `w_a > 0`, a CMB oscillation amplitude near `0.032`, `eta/s` near or above the KSS scale, a gravitational-wave-background peak near `0.2 Hz`, and a black-hole-shadow correction near `0.3` are presented as future falsification targets.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The most important issue is explanatory compression. The paper itself says it is a **conceptual axiomatic proposal** and leaves full formalization for future work. Several advertised recoveries also sit very close to assumptions. A8 postulates a fusion `P ⊗ P*` containing a photon, graviton and other gauge bosons and states that its classical limit contains `SU(3)c × SU(2)L × U(1)Y`; A11 postulates self-adjoint Hamiltonians with Schrödinger evolution; and A12 supplies the desired Einstein/Maxwell semiclassical correspondence. A framework can legitimately use such axioms, but then those outputs have not yet been compressed into a derivation from A0 alone.

The Standard-Model claim is likewise incomplete by the manuscript's own status table. The familiar gauge group is embedded through the exceptional-group chain, but the observed chiral matter representations, anomaly cancellation, three generations, masses and flavour data are not derived end-to-end. The paper calls fermion masses and Higgs quantities semi-quantitative and CKM/PMNS structure only structural, and explicitly says that it has not solved all 19 Standard-Model parameters. The stated formula for gauge couplings also still needs a complete low-energy normalization and renormalization-group calculation: categorical fusion multiplicities and Yang–Mills Lie-algebra structure constants are different objects, so identifying them requires a nontrivial derivation rather than a notation-level correspondence.

There is also no independent confirmation located in this pass. The peer-reviewed 2026 paper is a precursor by the same author, not an independent reconstruction of the July categorical theory. The quoted `w0/wa` agreement uses already available cosmological information and is therefore retrospective consistency, while the CMB, LISA-band and shadow numbers are still prospective/conjectural or semi-quantitative. I found no external calculation reproducing the full CCCM -> GR + chiral Standard Model chain.

**Placement.** **D scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** D reflects a mathematically structured early programme with substantial missing formalization, incomplete recovery of established low-energy physics, weak independent scrutiny and several target structures encoded directly in the axiom set. It is not F: the proposal is falsifiable in meaningful parts, internally inspectable and not presently shown to conflict decisively with a strong observation. A interest is separate: the scale-dependent continuum, topos/category architecture and explicit observational targets make it a fertile comparison object even though the evidence base is currently thin.

**Clash — E8 × omega-E8 Octonionic Unification.** Battleground: exceptional-algebra structure -> gravity + Standard Model + emergence of quantum physics. CCCM's concrete advantage is compactness: it exposes a small axiom ledger, proof-status tables and several cosmology/GW tests. Its liability is that the full July construction remains unreviewed, important outputs are axiomatized or only semi-quantitatively recovered, and there is no independent end-to-end reconstruction. E8 × omega-E8 remains **B/S**: it has a deeper multi-year technical lineage, peer-reviewed trace-dynamics/octonionic precursors and an explicit generalized-trace-dynamics calculational programme, but it too lacks a closed common dynamical derivation and broad independent reproduction. This clash does **not** move the existing E8 × omega-E8 tier.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at D/A. This is an entry, not a promotion. Movement toward C would require full formalization plus a derivation in which realistic Standard-Model/GR structure is output rather than correspondence input, ideally including numerically correct gauge running and flavour data from frozen categorical rules. B would additionally require independent reconstruction and at least one successful prospective discriminating prediction. Failure of one of the explicitly frozen cosmology/GW/shadow predictions would push in the opposite direction, depending on how load-bearing the failed claim is for the framework.
