## Material entries

### Geometric Action Principle for Gauge–Gravity–Matter Unification — enters B / interest A

**Source/date:** Pedro D. Alvarez, Fernando Izaurieta & Cristian Quinzacara, arXiv:2601.19734, first submitted 2026-01-27; current 2026 manuscript develops a Clifford-algebraic geometric action for Yang–Mills fields, gravity and matter fermions.

**Proposal:** one gauge-curvature-based invariant generates gravitational, Yang–Mills and first-order fermionic sectors in four dimensions without extra dimensions or an externally introduced symmetry-breaking field. The coefficient map intersects known linearized ghost-free quadratic Poincaré-gravity regions.

**Evidence for:** real component reductions; nontrivial correlations among gauge, fermion and gravitational terms; comparison against established healthy linearized gravity sectors; continuity with a substantial gauge-gravity/Clifford literature.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** the paper explicitly leaves realistic Standard Model representations and fermion content open; complete ghost freedom of the coupled nonlinear theory is not established; the natural interpretation is still EFT-like below torsional-mode scales; no distinctive empirical confirmation exists.

**Placement:** B/A, medium confidence. This is materially more than an early ontology sketch, but not yet a complete Standard-Model-plus-gravity construction. It remains below A until the realistic SM representation, full stability and discriminating phenomenology are demonstrated.

**Clash:** Holomorphic Unified Field Theory. Battleground: single-action geometric economy versus explicit low-energy Standard Model recovery. No tier movement for Holomorphic UFT follows.

**History:** 2026-09-18 watchlist → 2026-09-19 B/A after dedicated evaluation.

### The Lucron Network — enters C / interest A

**Source/date:** Guillermo Antonio Cabello Rivas, *The Lucron Network: A Relational Pre-Geometric Framework for Spacetime, Quantum Mechanics, Gravity, and Cosmology*, Version 3, Preprints.org, posted 2026-09-15; non-peer-reviewed.

**Proposal:** a discrete pre-geometric relational network of identical Lucrons, with information-bearing links and QCA-like microdynamics, is proposed as the substrate from which spacetime, quantum mechanics, particles, gravity and cosmology emerge.

**Evidence for:** explicit ontology and variables; broad but inspectable mathematical programme; unusually clear epistemic labeling; explicit falsifiers including a minimum black-hole mass and possible fine-structure-constant drift; the author directly records Lorentz emergence, unitarity and quantitative Yang–Mills recovery as open problems.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** exact Lorentz invariance is not derived; Standard Model gauge structure and particle spectrum remain partly qualitative; several numerical relations are calibrated rather than first-principles predictions; no peer review, independent replication or empirical confirmation is yet available.

**Placement:** C/A, medium confidence. It is structured speculation rather than an early verbal hypothesis, which places it above D, but the load-bearing continuum/SM derivations are not mature enough for B.

**Clash:** Loop quantum gravity / causal dynamical triangulations. Battleground: discrete pregeometry and continuum recovery. Lucron is broader in ToE ambition and explicit falsifiers; LQG/CDT are far deeper as mathematical/numerical research programmes.

**History:** 2026-09-18 watchlist → 2026-09-19 C/A after dedicated evaluation.

### Unified Emergence / Continuum-First Ontology — enters D / interest A

**Source/date:** Daryl Costello, *Unified Emergence: Continuum-First Ontology, Relational Coarse-Graining, and Refractive Cosmology*, Veridical Horizon, posted 2026-09-18.

**Proposal:** a pre-ontological continuum Ω is coarse-grained through an operator stack into fields, particles, spacetime and observers. A Photonic-Higgs refractive layer is used to reinterpret time dilation, while ontological distance and stabilizing asymmetry organize the emergence hierarchy.

**Evidence for:** internally coherent continuum-first architecture; an explicit contrast with discrete-first programmes; attempts to connect weak-field relativistic behavior to a refractive description; identifies possible extreme-gravity/quantum-interference discriminators; the author plainly lists the framework's own missing mathematics.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** the manuscript explicitly says it is not yet a fully developed formal theory; the foundational coarse-graining operator is not specified sufficiently to carry the dynamics; Standard Model quantum numbers/couplings are not derived; several empirical successes are reinterpretations of known relativity rather than new predictions; no independent scrutiny or peer review.

**Placement:** D/A, medium confidence. High conceptual/generative interest does not compensate for the missing load-bearing dynamics.

**Clash:** Lucron Network. Battleground: continuum-first versus discrete-first ontology. Unified Emergence avoids primitive lattice structure but lacks a quantitative coarse-graining engine; Lucron is more explicit dynamically but must recover Lorentz/continuum physics.

**History:** 2026-09-19 enters D/A.
