## Entered: Universal Awareness–Information–Computation (UAIC) — C scientific / S interest, low confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Hemant K. Gupta, Gupta Institute of Unity Science. The earliest public UAIC framework located in this audit was posted on **2026-08-15** under a title stamped `08142026`. The decision-bearing combined master paper was published on TOE-Share on **2026-09-02**: [The Theory of Everything: A UAIC Approach — Combined Framework and Master Paper 09022026](https://theoryofeverything.ai/frameworks/the-theory-of-everything-a-uaic-approach-combined-framework-and-master-paper-09022026-mtk8fgyq). TOE-Share currently labels it approved by its AI review but **pending publication**; that is not scientific peer review.

**Proposal.** UAIC starts from a pre-geometric `c=1/2` Ising-class substrate and a Universal Cosmic Loss Function integrated over MERA depth. A 13-stage ternary MERA cascade and an exceptional-group route `E8 -> E6 × SU(3)_F` are claimed to produce 3+1 spacetime, Yang–Mills structure, the Higgs and matter sectors, three generations, gravity and a thermodynamic observer/awareness sector. The corpus advertises one fitted running parameter, `alpha_run ~= 0.354`, while classifying many internal statements with `[RE]`, `[HC]`, `[PT]` and `[OE]` tags.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is an unusually explicit single-originator programme: the master document links a large companion corpus, maintains a named open-problem register, publishes corrections and attaches concrete falsification criteria. The prediction surface is broad and specific: a protocol-dependent cryptochrome FAD ODMR anomaly near **22.8 MHz**, `Z=126` as the next proton magic number, a **170–258 GeV** electroweakino window and cosmological fraction targets are all exposed as things that can fail.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The central gravity claim is not cleanly closed. The quadratic construction claims two ghost-free graviton polarizations, but the all-orders truncation and dynamically generated local diffeomorphism symmetry remain conditional on further structure; later companion papers claim to resolve part of this, but inside the same corpus. The advertised 'one fitted parameter' also understates the assumption ledger: quantities and identifications such as `alpha_GUT^-1=24`, the `F4`/`H4` constructions and other anchors are tagged as hard assumptions or phenomenological targets rather than being independently forced. Remaining Standard-Model parameters and the absolute lepton scale are still open.

The prediction history matters. The cryptochrome ODMR headline moved from a **2.87 GHz placeholder** to about **22.8 MHz**. The master paper is transparent about the correction, which is a positive epistemic practice, but it means the current ledger is not yet a long-frozen prospective record. The same source notes that 20–30 MHz radical-pair ODMR is not itself unique; the discriminating claim is an anomalous coherence-state-associated feature under the specified controls.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, low confidence.** C follows the rubric's `structured speculation` category: the formal and computational surface is substantial and the framework is falsifiable, but the nonlinear-gravity/realistic-SM chain is partly conditional and independent scientific scrutiny is currently thin. S interest is separate: MERA, exceptional algebra, information theory, cosmology and named experimental protocols form an unusually generative comparison object.

**Clash — E8 × ωE8 Octonionic Unification (B/S).** Battleground: **exceptional/pre-geometric structure -> realistic Standard Model + gravity + prospective discriminator**. UAIC's advantage is its explicit status/open-problem ledger and relatively accessible falsifiers; its liabilities are structural ansätze, a moving prediction history and same-corpus resolution of core gaps. E8 × ωE8 has a materially longer peer-reviewed trace-dynamics/noncommutative lineage; its liability is that several decisive emergence, mass and phenomenology claims also remain originator-driven and not independently confirmed. External reconstruction of either gravity-plus-matter chain, or a frozen successful prediction, would materially change the comparison. **No existing tier moves from this clash.**

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at C/S, low confidence. The history explicitly preserves the August-to-September revision sequence and the 2.87 GHz -> 22.8 MHz ODMR change rather than treating the latest number as an original frozen prediction.

## Entered: Selection–Stitch Model (SSM) — B scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Raghu Kulkarni / SSMTheory Group. A unification-bearing foundation, [The Cosserat Vacuum](https://www.idrive.com/ssmtheory-physics/the-cosserat-vacuum), was public by **February 2026** (Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.18645619`). The programme subsequently accumulated arXiv and peer-reviewed sector papers. The current fresh peer-reviewed item is [Defect Migration in the D4 Vacuum Lattice](https://doi.org/10.3390/sym18091571), *Symmetry* 18, 1571, **published 2026-09-20**. Other decision-bearing papers include the EPJ Plus black-hole paper (`10.1140/epjp/s13360-026-08148-9`), the Quantum Reports microgram-threshold paper (`10.3390/quantum8030078`) and the **2026-09-05** dark-matter paper (`10.3390/quantum8030089`).

**Proposal.** SSM treats the vacuum as a close-packed FCC/D4 discrete substrate carrying an error-correcting structure. Matter is incomplete crystallization: defects in tetrahedral or octahedral voids carry particle-like properties, while hopping and verification dynamics are used to model quantum behavior and mass. A chiral Cosserat continuum construction is used for gauge/spinor kinematics; D4 Regge geometry is used for the gravitational sector; bond counting and defect geometry are then extended to black-hole thermodynamics, dark matter and experimental signatures.

**Evidence for / strengths.** Unlike many independent ToE proposals, SSM has crossed into multiple peer-reviewed sector publications. Its strongest technical gravity result is specific rather than metaphorical: the EPJ Plus paper reports that an explicit D4/Regge continuum kinetic operator matches the linearized Einstein/Fierz–Pauli operator term by term. The corpus also exposes sharp tests: a **13.1–22.6 microgram** two-step quantum/classical threshold, a fixed black-hole area quantum `Delta A = 4 ln 2 l_P^2` with ringdown-line implications, a **1.719 GeV** dark-matter candidate and model-specific primordial-black-hole evaporation behavior.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The FCC/D4 substrate and code interpretation are working assumptions, and the strongest gravity result is still linearized. A controlled nonlinear, Lorentz/diffeomorphism-safe Einstein limit and precision recovery remain open. The complete Standard Model is also not reconstructed end-to-end: gauge/chiral pieces exist, but anomaly cancellation, complete generation/flavour structure, Higgs/Yukawa data and RG behavior are not independently closed from the same primitives.

Several numerical successes require careful bookkeeping. The [Master Equation](https://www.idrive.com/ssmtheory/master-equation-for-the-selection-stitch-mode) explicitly says that the 1836 verification schedule is a **dynamical realization of prior counting, not an independent derivation**. The peer-reviewed dark-matter mass uses the proton mass as a calibration input plus a stated standing-information postulate. And the gamma-line history is adaptive: an original simple `chi chi -> gamma gamma` expectation at about **1.7195 GeV** lay roughly 10% above a reported 1.5–1.6 GeV feature, after which a new `chi chi -> gamma + chi'` residual channel was introduced to resolve the tension. That may be a legitimate model refinement, but it is post-data adaptation and therefore should not count as prospective confirmation.

**Placement.** **B scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** B is justified by the rubric's `serious contender` criterion: coherent and nontrivial mathematics, several peer-reviewed sector calculations, and identifiable falsifiers now exist. It is not A because full gravity + realistic Standard Model recovery is incomplete, the decisive chain is largely originator-authored, and independent replication is absent. S interest is separate: the lattice/error-correction/particle-defect/gravity bridge generates a large number of concrete experiments and comparisons.

**Clash — Refined Space-Time Membrane Model (C/S).** Battleground: **mechanical/discrete substrate -> realistic Standard Model + Einstein gravity + prospective test**. SSM currently has the stronger publication record and more explicit D4/Regge and code-level calculations; its liabilities are a bigger stack of substrate assumptions and some calibrated/adaptive phenomenology. RSTM has a more compact PDE/EFT architecture and a direct membrane/metamaterial analogy, but less external publication and likewise no independently closed GR+SM chain. An independent reconstruction of the nonlinear-gravity or chiral-SM bridge would be decisive. **The clash supports SSM's higher maturity placement but does not move RSTM.**

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at B/S, medium confidence. The record deliberately preserves both the programme's peer-reviewed maturation and the post-data dark-matter channel revision.
