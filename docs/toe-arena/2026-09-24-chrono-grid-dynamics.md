## New contender: Chrono-Grid Dynamics — C scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Canonical source/date.** Nebojša Jovanović, *Chrono-Grid Dynamics: a discrete unitary framework for emergent gravity*, *European Physical Journal C* 86, 752, published **2026-07-03**: <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16018-y>. The article was received 2026-03-16 and accepted 2026-06-01. It cites an earlier 2026 Zenodo formulation, *Chrono-grid dynamics: a unified framework for emergent gravity from discrete proper time* (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18756136); the exact posting date of that earlier record was not exposed by the primary index available in this scan, so it is not guessed here.

**Proposal.** CGD postulates a universal proper-time quantum `τ`, the **Takt**, and evolves a quantum state unitarily in discrete steps. Its microscopic substrate is a cubic or tetrahedral 3D lattice with spin-1/2 site degrees of freedom and dynamical `SU(2)` link variables. The continuum limit identifies the links with an Ashtekar connection and their conjugate momenta with densitized triads. The central normalization claim is `8πGβ = τ²`, tying Newton's constant and the Barbero–Immirzi parameter to the discrete clock. The paper then defines lattice Gauss, diffeomorphism and Hamiltonian constraints, claims recovery of the Dirac algebra, imports spin-network quantization, develops area/volume/length spectra, reduces to an FRW sector and interprets the lattice as a Planck-scale UV cutoff.

**Scope.** This is presently a quantum-gravity/emergent-spacetime programme, not a complete gravity-plus-realistic-Standard-Model unification. The site spin is an abstract matter degree of freedom. The current published core does not derive `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)`, observed chiral representations, three generations, anomaly cancellation, Higgs/Yukawa structure or measured flavour parameters.

### Evidence for / strengths

- The microscopic rule is explicit enough to attack: Hilbert space, link algebra, Hamiltonian pieces and discrete unitary update are written down rather than left as an ontology-only proposal.
- The continuum target is concrete. The paper identifies lattice variables with Ashtekar–Barbero variables and gives a falsifiable normalization claim, `8πGβ = τ²`.
- It connects its construction to established quantum-gravity machinery — spin networks, `6j` symbols, BF/spinfoam structures and quantum groups — and makes clear which parts are inherited from standard LQG methods.
- The central 3+1D formulation is a journal article rather than only an unpublished manifesto. Companion work explores lower-dimensional constraint algebra, a tetrahedron numerical problem and cosmological/dispersion phenomenology, giving the programme a larger technical surface than a one-paper sketch.

### Evidence against / unresolved

**1. The strongest 3+1D anomaly-freedom claim is not fully derived in the public paper.** Section 4 says the key lattice commutators can be derived but that the detailed derivations will be given in a separate publication. Appendix A labels itself a **sketch** and again says a complete derivation with all factors will appear later. That matters because closure of the Hamiltonian-constraint algebra is one of the hardest and most load-bearing parts of canonical quantum gravity. The text's conclusion that the lattice theory is “free of anomalies” is therefore stronger than the calculation shown in the same article.

**2. The published geometric-spectrum normalizations do not agree with each other as written.** The paper states

`L(j) = β l_Pl sqrt(j(j+1))`,

along with `8πGβ = τ²` and `l_Pl² = G`. Direct substitution yields

`β l_Pl = τ² / (8π sqrt(G))`,

not the paper's alternate expression `τ/(8πG)`. Later, the FRW section uses `L = τ sqrt(j(j+1))`. Those three expressions cannot all follow from the stated relations without an additional normalization/definition that is not supplied. This is not treated as a refutation of the entire microscopic idea, but it blocks treating the published length/FRW chain as a clean derived prediction until repaired.

**3. Much of the quantum geometry is inherited from LQG rather than newly derived.** The paper explicitly says it quantizes using standard loop-quantum-gravity methods. That is legitimate, but the Arena should not count standard LQG area/spin-network structure twice as independent evidence for CGD. The distinctive burden is whether the Takt microdynamics actually selects and controls the LQG/GR continuum.

**4. Standard-Model recovery is absent.** A generic spin-1/2 degree of freedom and `SU(2)` link field do not yet amount to the observed matter/gauge sector. This keeps CGD far from full ToE scope despite its title-level ambition about emergent gravity.

**5. Independent scrutiny is thin.** In this scan I did not locate an independent reconstruction of the central `8πGβ = τ²` derivation, the 3+1D constraint algebra or the companion cosmological fits. First-party calculations are useful evidence of technical activity but are not independent replication.

### Placement

**C scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** C follows the skill's “structured speculation” bucket: CGD is mathematically concrete and has journal publication, but two load-bearing recovery steps remain weaker than the headline language — full 3+1D closure is deferred and the geometric-spectrum normalization is internally inconsistent as printed — while realistic matter is missing and independent scrutiny is limited. It is above D because there is a genuine Hamiltonian lattice theory and a calculational programme. It stays below B until the central closure/recovery derivations can be checked end-to-end and the normalization issue is repaired.

Interest is **A**, independently of the scientific C. The discrete-proper-time hypothesis is a sharp intervention on the LQG landscape and produces unusually clear things to calculate: constraint closure, continuum Lorentz recovery, the Takt/Immirzi relation, lattice dispersion and cosmological reduction.

### Clash — Loop quantum gravity (A/A)

**Battleground: microscopic dynamics and controlled recovery of general relativity.**

CGD's advantage is an explicit local discrete-time update law and a proposed relation tying the microscopic time step to `G` and `β`. Its liability is that it borrows much of LQG's kinematical apparatus, while its own distinctive 3+1D closure and normalization are not yet comparably mature.

Loop quantum gravity has decades of independent mathematical development, multiple formulations and a much larger scrutiny base. Its liability remains the familiar one already recorded in the Arena: dynamics, smooth low-energy recovery, complete matter/force unification and distinctive accessible empirical signatures remain difficult.

**What would change the comparison?** A full public derivation of the CGD Hamiltonian-constraint algebra with all factors, an independently reproduced continuum limit, a repaired geometric-spectrum normalization and a genuinely CGD-specific prediction that survives data would move CGD upward. Failure of the Takt dynamics to recover Lorentz/Dirac structure would move it downward. The new entry does **not** justify moving LQG from A/A.
