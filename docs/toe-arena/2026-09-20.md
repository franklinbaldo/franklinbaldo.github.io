### Spatial Unit Conservation and Dynamic Reorganization — enters C / interest S, low confidence

**Source/date:** Hongliang Qian & Yixuan Qian, *Spatial Unit Conservation and Dynamic Reorganization: A Unified Framework of Gravity, Cosmology and Quantum Discreteness*, Preprints.org Version 12, posted 2026-04-28. The Arena had held the Version 5 lineage on 2026-09-19 for dedicated validation rather than assigning a premature tier.

**Proposal:** spacetime is a Planck-scale graph `G=(V,E)` carrying a complex node field. Three stated principles — Covariance, Invariance and Shielding — are used to claim emergence/recovery of quantum mechanics, particle quantum numbers, gauge structure, gravity and cosmology. Version 12 claims first-principles results including `g=3`, spin `1/2`, `N_c=3`, charge quantization, a Planck-scale Weinberg-angle boundary condition, a massless lightest neutrino, no fourth fermion generation, scaling relations and gravitational-wave echoes.

**Evidence for:** this is not merely a verbal ontology. It gives an explicit discrete substrate, named axioms, many equations, integration with spin-network/LQG machinery, a list of prospective failure conditions and attempted cross-sector numerical tests. The latest version is also more epistemically useful than Version 5 because it explicitly lists calibrated quantities and unfinished derivations.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** there is no located independent reproduction of the load-bearing topological, Standard-Model or gravitational derivations. Version 12 materially narrows the earlier Version 5 impression that all 28 Standard Model parameters were first-principles results: it says the charged-lepton eigenvalues used for muon/tau ratios are experimentally calibrated, composite masses are out of scope, quantum closure of the constraint algebra is unproven, and a complete dynamical derivation of the gravitational formula remains pending.

**Empirical-audit concern:** the Milky Way section reports a reduced chi-squared of 1.2 after fitting parameters to a table labeled `Gaia+VERA 2024`. In the appendix, that table is embedded directly in code rather than loaded from a traceable data product. I did not locate a corresponding public joint Gaia+VERA 2024 release in the sources searched. Published Gaia DR3 analyses instead emphasize a declining outer rotation curve, including a ~30 km/s drop from 19.5 to 26.5 kpc in Jiao et al. This does not prove the manuscript's table is wrong, but it means the claimed galaxy validation is not independently reproducible from the cited provenance as presently written and should not count as confirmation.

**Placement:** C/S, low confidence. C matches the skill's 'structured speculation' bucket: substantial formal structure and explicit predictions, but weak independent validation and unresolved derivations. Interest S reflects the unusual breadth and number of concrete cross-sector claims worth attacking; it is not an endorsement of correctness.

**Clash:** Loop Quantum Gravity and Holomorphic UFT. Qian claims a more complete discrete/topological bridge all the way to matter parameters and cosmology, but LQG has vastly deeper independent mathematical development and Holomorphic UFT has a multi-paper peer-reviewed programme. Independent reconstruction, not claim breadth, is the bottleneck for any move toward B.

**History:** 2026-09-19 held without tier pending claim-level audit → 2026-09-20 enters C/S with low confidence.

Primary: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202602.1314

### The Lucron Model — remains C / interest A after Version 4

**Source/date:** Guillermo Antonio Cabello Rivas, *The Lucron Model: A Single-Scale Ontology for Space, Matter, Gravity, and Quantum Mechanics*, Version 4, posted 2026-09-18, DOI `10.20944/preprints202608.1622.v4`; non-peer-reviewed.

**What changed:** Version 4 is a material extension and partial reformulation of the Version 3 Lucron Network audited yesterday. It introduces a single-scale/coupling presentation, claims roughly twenty dimensionless constants/relations, compares a cosmological volume law with DESI DR2 BAO, and explicitly lists quantitative misses for CKM/CP/PMNS quantities.

**Important ontology change:** Version 3 described Lucrons as relational units with no intrinsic properties; Version 4 explicitly assigns intrinsic minimal length, time, mass, charge and action. That is a foundational change rather than a cosmetic rewrite and needs an explicit supersession/derivation story before results from both versions can simply be pooled.

**Evidence for:** more quantitative exposure; explicit failed predictions; a BAO comparison that rejects a naive alternate volume law; concrete GUT/mixing/hierarchy formulae that outsiders can attempt to reproduce.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** several claimed agreements are factor-level rather than precision derivations; the new candado/geometric formulae remain author-derived and unreproduced; matching LambdaCDM's BAO chi-squared is compatibility, not discrimination; no peer review or independent replication has appeared.

**Placement:** stays C/A, medium confidence. The update strengthens the programme *within* C but does not solve the independent-reproduction, low-energy-recovery or discriminating-evidence blockers required for B.

**History:** 2026-09-18 watchlist → 2026-09-19 C/A on Version 3 → 2026-09-20 remains C/A after Version 4; source/title and ontology history updated.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202608.1622.v4

### String theory / M-theory — remains A / interest S

A fresh 2026-09-17/18 public discussion highlighted the CMS null search for microscopic black holes/string balls. The underlying peer-reviewed JHEP result excludes semiclassical black holes at roughly 8.4–11.4 TeV and string balls at roughly 9.0–10.7 TeV in the specific large-extra-dimension models tested. That is useful negative evidence against a restricted low-scale regime, not a falsification of string/M-theory as a whole, so no broad-programme tier move follows.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2026)098
