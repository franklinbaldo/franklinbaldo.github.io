## Material updates

### Holomorphic Unified Field Theory — entered B / interest A

**Source:** John W. Moffat & Ethan J. Thompson, *Holomorphic Unified Field Theory of Gravity and the Standard Model*, arXiv:2506.19161, first submitted 2025-06-23, with peer-reviewed and follow-up work through 2026.

**Why B:** it is a multi-paper technical programme that explicitly targets gravity plus the Standard Model, with concrete constructions for chiral matter, symmetry breaking, masses and mixings. It is materially more developed than a one-off speculative proposal. It remains below A because independent scrutiny and reproduction are still limited, there is no direct empirical discriminator, and structural objections to the claimed holomorphic/unified character remain unresolved.

**Clash:** String theory. Battleground: four-dimensional specificity versus ultraviolet maturity.

### USMEG-EFT — entered C / interest A

**Source:** Farrukh A. Chishtie, *Classical spacetime as a gravitational condensate: USMEG-EFT emergence in comparison to Verlinde’s entropic gravity*, Physics Letters B, September 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2026.140803.

**Why C:** the framework has explicit path-integral, renormalization-group and BRST machinery and a peer-reviewed calculation. However, the current construction itself places the breakdown of its controlled geometric description around 10^18 GeV, so it is not yet an ultraviolet-complete Theory of Everything. The proposed disordered/pre-geometric phase is not yet a full high-energy completion.

**Clash:** Asymptotic safety. Battleground: finite-domain EFT versus ultraviolet fixed point/completion.

### ZEBTS / χ-Reality — entered D / interest A

**Source:** Anatolii Mukha, *ZEBTS — Zero-Entropy Topological Background: χ-Reality The Theory of Everything — SUPER 12 Version*, Version 3, Cambridge Open Engage, 2026-09-09.

**Why D:** the proposal is explicit enough to expose an operator-spectral core, a very broad set of claimed consequences and candidate failure modes, but it is an unreviewed working paper without established independent validation. Its advertised scope is far larger than the current body of reproduced calculations and low-energy recovery.

**Clash:** mature unification programmes. Battleground: breadth of claims versus independent recovery of established physics.

### Asymptotic safety — held at A / interest A

**New source:** Christof Wetterich, *Fermi scale from quantum gravity scaling solution*, accepted in Physical Review D on 2026-09-14.

**Decision:** no tier movement. The result materially strengthens contact with the electroweak hierarchy by deriving a scaling solution in which an ultraviolet fixed point can make the cosmon-Higgs coupling predictive and generate a tiny Fermi-to-Planck scale ratio. It does not by itself establish the required fixed point with full systematic control or provide decisive empirical discrimination.

### Time as Concatenation / Informational Time — held NR / interest A

**New repository evidence:** `franklinbaldo/papers/audits/prior-art/informational-time-critical-recognition-2026-09-18.md`.

**Decision:** no tier movement. The audit materially narrows generic novelty claims: causal-depth/time-like distinctions, recognition latency, active recognition, predictive intentional modelling and sequential stopping-time ingredients all have substantial prior art. The narrower combined criterion may still be distinctive, but the paper remains an exhibition entry rather than a Theory of Everything contender.
