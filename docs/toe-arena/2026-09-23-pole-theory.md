## Material entry

### Pole Theory — enters D / interest A, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Prem Raika. The minimized primary preprint was submitted on 2025-05-19 and posted on 2025-05-20 at <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202505.1520>. The expanded/maximized version was submitted on 2025-06-02 and posted on 2025-06-03 at <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202506.0237>. The public record states that the work is not peer reviewed.

**Proposal.** Pole Theory posits a fixed 3+1D Planck-scale lattice of fundamental poles carrying tension and angular-curvature variables. Their product, `phi = T * K_theta`, is intended to provide a single scalar substrate from which spacetime, quantum dynamics, gravity, cosmology, gauge interactions and particle structure emerge. The corpus supplies a lattice action, a Klein-Gordon-like field equation with curvature source, a discrete curvature prescription, gauge-link/plaquette language, fermion/Higgs terms and proposed phenomenological deviations.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The programme clears the Arena's scope threshold: it does not stop at quantum gravity, but explicitly attempts gravity plus quantum/gauge/matter recovery. It is also concrete enough to audit. The papers write down microscopic variables, lattice derivatives, a variational action and several proposed observables. The gravitational-wave section, for example, derives a modified lattice dispersion relation and estimates the resulting group-velocity correction rather than leaving the prediction purely verbal.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The present derivation has substantial circularity and parameter-import debt. The advertised fundamental equation already contains the Ricci scalar `R` and a quantum amplitude `Psi` while gravity and quantum behavior are also claimed as emergent. The Standard-Model section inserts measured/standard structures — fermion masses `m_f`, Yukawa factors `y_f`, and CKM/PMNS matrix elements `V_ij` — into the geometric rewrite, so it does not yet derive their values, the chiral representation content, three generations or anomaly cancellation from the pole rules. Lorentz invariance is argued from the formal `l_p -> 0` continuum limit even though the physical model retains a nonzero fundamental lattice spacing. The sharpest worked GW correction is about `10^-68` in group velocity near 100 Hz and is explicitly too small for direct detection; the suggestion that cumulative effects might become observable is not yet a frozen quantitative forecast. I located no independent end-to-end reconstruction of the gravity/gauge/matter chain.

**Placement.** **D scientific / A interest, medium placement confidence.** D is appropriate because there is genuine mathematical structure and true ToE-scale ambition, but the central recovery claims remain too dependent on imported continuum objects and measured parameters to count as structured unification at C under the Arena rubric. A interest is independent of that weakness: the single-field discrete ontology, explicit equations and multiple possible failure modes make it useful for comparative testing and visualization.

**Clash — Quantized Spacetime from First Principles.** Battleground: discrete substrate -> precision Lorentz + chiral Standard Model + Einstein recovery. Quantized Spacetime remains C/A because its decision-bearing source presents a cleaner direct gauge-and-metric construction, although it pays a different explanatory cost by assuming QM and GR. Pole Theory aims for deeper ontological compression, but currently re-imports more of the target physics through `R`, `Psi`, masses, Yukawas and flavor matrices. The comparison therefore leaves Quantized Spacetime at C/A and places Pole Theory at D/A. A faithful reconstruction that starts only from pole rules and independently produces anomaly-consistent chiral matter, Einstein gravity and precision Lorentz behavior would be the relevant gate toward C.

**Movement history.** Pole Theory was explicitly left untiered on the 2026-09-23 watchlist pending a primary-source reconstruction. This audit moves it from watchlist to canonical contender at D/A. That is a catalogue-status change, not a claim that new evidence suddenly made the theory more likely to be true.
