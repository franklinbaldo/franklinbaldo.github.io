## Material result: String/M-theory — still scientific A / interest S (medium placement confidence)

Aggarwal, Apers and Farakos, [arXiv:2610.13586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13586), submitted 9 October 2026, develop resolved G2-orientifold compactifications of massive IIA supergravity into AdS3. They report flux-based stabilization of geometric blow-up moduli in a parameter regime retaining scale separation and supergravity control. Resolution removes apparent intersections of orientifold loci, while curvature-induced source terms address a corresponding tadpole-charge issue. The brane-dual analysis supplies a finite-interval background and low-energy decoupling for selected modes.

**For:** resolves specific geometry/moduli consistency questions that weaken earlier singular-orbifold idealizations. **Against / unresolved:** work is in AdS3, not a 3+1D Standard Model vacuum; supergravity, source and scale assumptions are important. Authors leave a full superpotential for additional axionic moduli, D-brane position stabilization and a complete analytic brane-network geometry for further work; some stabilization arguments are perturbative/parametric. Independent low-energy spectrum or experiment is absent.

**Clash — String/M-theory A/S versus BFSS Matrix Theory A/S, compactification / microscopic control.** This work strengthens supergravity compactification control but does not independently identify a microscopic BFSS vacuum or derive the observed chiral matter spectrum. A matched protected spectrum or independently reproducible vacuum dynamics, followed by contact with observed particles, would materially strengthen the comparison. No tier change.

## Contextual findings (not standalone ToE contenders)

* **Amplitude peak chaos** — Basu & Sarkar, [arXiv:2610.13240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13240), 9 October: show quartic adjacent-peak spacing `p_nn(s) ~ s^4` for sufficiently smooth, nondegenerate amplitude ensembles; numerical examples include highly excited tree-level string amplitudes. Because the exponent also occurs in unrelated smooth-function ensembles, it is not a string-specific fingerprint or a demonstration of dynamical chaos. NR / interest A; relevant methodological caution in comparison with holographic chaos diagnostics.
* **SU(N) Einstein–Skyrme** — Henríquez-Baez, Medina, Rodríguez & Vera, [arXiv:2610.13566](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13566), 9 October: generalize specific exact black-hole, wormhole and Bianchi-IX cosmological solutions from SU(2) to arbitrary flavor N. Thermal features, throat size and scale-factor behavior depend on N. The wormholes use an asymptotically AdS NUT ansatz and restricted nonlinear-sigma-model sector; stability, observational realization and unification are not established. NR / interest B.
* **Brane-space T-duality** — Han & Mathai, [arXiv:2610.12825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12825), 9 October UTC: establish localization of exotic twisted cohomology on sphere-map brane space to H-twisted cohomology of the target and graded genus-zero T-duality via Hori maps at nonzero level. Rigorous geometric advances do not by themselves demonstrate physical 4D vacuum or complete quantum duality. NR / interest A.
