## Entered: The Stiff Medium Model — C scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Source / date.** The primary source is the public `H-XX-D/substrate-theory` repository. Its full design specification is dated **2026-04-29**; the current repository state reviewed on **2026-09-24** contains later stress tests and gap audits through the current weak-sector work. I found no peer-reviewed publication or independent reconstruction of the complete programme in this pass.

**Proposal.** The model posits one three-dimensional elastic medium as the only fundamental substrate. Stable and transient excitations of that medium are intended to appear as matter, electromagnetism, gravity, spin, black holes, dark sectors and cosmology. The current encompassing action combines a scalar substrate field with fermion/gauge/Yukawa terms and a 45-degree cone constraint; a newer compact-geometric candidate rewrites the cone in a substrate null geometry and adds a symmetric-traceless neutral-stress sector. Möbius half-flux topology is used for spin-1/2, substrate back-reaction for forces, charge-asymmetric/symmetric channels for electromagnetism/gravity, saturation for black-hole/cosmological behavior and multi-kink/neutral-stress structures for dark matter.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is more developed than a qualitative mechanical analogy. The repository contains simulation modules, unit tests and demonstration scripts covering atomic structure, QED containment, gravity/EM hierarchy, GR benchmark observables, QCD-scale quantities, cosmology and dedicated negative/gap tests. The current README explicitly calls the compression claim provisional and names the sectors that are still weak. That is good scientific hygiene: failure modes are visible and the model supplies enough machinery for adversarial checks.

**Limits / uncertainties.** The same README shows why the programme remains C rather than B. The canonical `alpha_audit` concludes that the fine-structure constant is **not derived yet**; the best fixed two-loop scan point is reported low by `0.528795527` in inverse-alpha, about **0.385880739%** relative to CODATA 2022. The programme also lists CKM/PMNS/flavour mixing, lepton excitation energies, current-quark masses and SU(3)-breaking, matter-sector orientation, Planck-scale closure, precision CMB/Hubble transfer functions, the quantitative dark-stress sector and full nonlinear strong-field GR as active gaps. These are load-bearing pieces of a gravity-plus-realistic-SM claim, not peripheral polish.

**Containment is not emergence.** The repository itself describes some of its best-performing regions as `QED inheritance` or standard-physics containment. Reproducing Michel parameters, electron `g-2`, Lamb shift, Mercury precession or light bending through equations that already contain the corresponding low-energy structures is useful consistency evidence, but it does not carry the same evidential weight as deriving those structures uniquely from the substrate. The decisive recovery question is therefore not how many benchmark scripts are green; it is which observed structures are forced by the substrate with fewer uncredited inputs and survive genuinely prospective tests.

**Lorentz / preferred-frame gate.** A literal three-dimensional medium makes the status of Lorentz invariance central. The model supplies cone/null-geometric and relativistic-looking effective structures, but this audit did not locate an independent end-to-end derivation establishing that no observable preferred-frame residue survives across particle, gravitational and cosmological regimes. This is recorded as an unresolved recovery gate rather than an automatic contradiction: emergent Lorentz symmetry is possible in principle, but it must be shown quantitatively here.

**Independent scrutiny.** I found the first-party repository and its internal test/audit suite, but no external reproduction of a load-bearing chain from medium dynamics to the realistic chiral Standard Model plus gravity. That absence materially limits the scientific placement despite the framework's breadth and code coverage.

**Tier decision.** **C scientific** under the Arena rubric: the proposal is concrete, mathematical and executable, but decisive recovery bridges and independent scrutiny remain missing. **A interest** is separate: the unusually broad executable substrate, explicit failure ledger and many possible discriminators make it a productive target for comparison and attempted falsification. The A is not evidence that the proposal is likely true.

**Clash — The Lucron Model (C/A).** Battleground: substrate/pregeometry → realistic Standard Model + gravity with explanatory compression. Stiff Medium's advantage is an explicit candidate action plus a large executable benchmark/audit surface and unusually visible negative results; its liability is that many successes are inherited/containment checks while flavour, UV, precision cosmology and nonlinear GR remain open. Lucron's advantage is a compact single-scale ontology with a published preprint, explicit constant/mixing relations, a DESI DR2 BAO comparison and a ledger of failed predictions; its liability is the unresolved Version-3→Version-4 ontology change and lack of independent reconstruction of the load-bearing numerical/geometric relations. The comparison does **not** move Lucron from C/A. Stiff Medium would need independent reconstruction of at least one central emergence bridge, controlled Lorentz/GR recovery, realistic chiral/flavour closure and a frozen prospective discriminator before a B-tier case becomes plausible.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-24** at **C/A**, medium confidence. This is an omission correction from the current repository scan, not a promotion from a prior Arena tier.
