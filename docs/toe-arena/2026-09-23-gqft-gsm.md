## Entered: General Standard Model / Gravitational Quantum Field Theory — A scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin / dates / sources.** Yue-Liang Wu's GQFT programme is not new in 2026; this is a backlog correction prompted by fresh phenomenology. The foundational preprint *Quantum field theory of gravity with spin and scaling gauge invariance and spacetime dynamics with quantum inflation* was submitted 2015-06-05 and later published as Phys. Rev. D 93, 024012 (2016): <https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01807>. The explicitly unificatory branch appeared in *Unified field theory of basic forces and elementary particles with gravitational origin of gauge symmetry in hyper-spacetime*, Science Bulletin 62 (2017) 1109–1113: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06365>, followed by *Hyperunified field theory and gravitational gauge–geometry duality*, EPJC 78, 28 (2018): <https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-017-5504-3>. The current GSM synthesis is *Theoretical Foundations of the General Standard Model: A Unified Framework for Particle Physics and Cosmology*, submitted 2025-08-26, revised 2026-01-29 and published in Science China Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy 69 (2026) 241011: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20128>. The fresh trigger for this audit is Zhang, Tang & Wu, *Vector Dark Matter from Gravitational Quantum Field Theory*, submitted 2026-09-17: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25069>.

**Proposal.** GQFT treats gravity as a gauge interaction associated with spin/scaling symmetries and a bicovariant gravigauge field in a biframe spacetime, rather than beginning from metric geometry alone. The GSM builds on that framework while retaining the familiar Standard-Model gauge sector and adding conformal inhomogeneous spin, scaling and related gauge symmetries, with the stated aim of a common description of the four interactions, Higgs sector and cosmology. The older hyperunified branch is more radical: it places quarks and leptons into a single hyper-spinor in a proposed 19-dimensional hyper-spacetime governed by hyper-spin gauge symmetry. I treat these as one research programme in the Arena for now rather than creating overlapping cards; the card explicitly distinguishes the better-tested weak-field GQFT sector from the more speculative hyperunified claims.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is a decade-long, multi-paper and partly peer-reviewed programme, not a single recent manuscript. It has unusually concrete phenomenological contact for a unification proposal. Gao et al., Phys. Rev. D 109, 064072 (2024), derive five propagating gravitational-wave polarizations — two tensor, two vector and one scalar — plus a Newtonian limit and constraints from light deflection/Shapiro delay: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.064072>. Chen, Xu & Wu, EPJC 85, 228 (2025), derive a first post-Newtonian solution and obtain a bound on a combined GQFT coupling from Solar-system tests: <https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-13993-6>. Importantly, Gao & Ni, Phys. Rev. D 111, 084039 (2025), are not the originators of the framework and independently derive its frame-dragging Lense–Thirring effect and constrain it with LARES/LAGEOS data: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.111.084039>. More recent work develops GW generation/detection and a Taiji/LISA null-response test for the transverse scalar mode: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13483>. The September 2026 dark-matter paper constructs a low-energy EFT for a chirality boost-spin gauge field with axial-vector couplings to Standard-Model fermions and confronts direct, indirect and collider constraints, which gives the GSM another experimentally attackable interface.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** Passing weak-field bounds is compatibility, not positive confirmation: no extra GQFT GW polarization, spin/chirality gauge force or GSM dark-sector field has been detected. The current GSM also claims first-principles construction while explicitly extending the conventional `U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3)` Standard-Model symmetry; therefore substantial observed particle structure remains embedded rather than uniquely explained by a smaller principle. Masses, mixings, couplings and the detailed chiral matter spectrum remain decisive compression/recovery tests. The hyperunified 19D branch makes much stronger claims than the weak-field gravitational sector but has far thinner independent reconstruction and precision phenomenology. The central scientific gate is therefore an end-to-end, independently reproduced low-energy derivation that recovers chiral Standard-Model data and produces a successful distinctive prediction from the same underlying structure.

**Placement.** **A scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** A follows the skill's definition of a mature, technically developed research programme with direct constraints and major remaining conceptual/empirical gaps. It is stronger than B because the programme has a decade of technical development, peer-reviewed gravity tests, an independent frame-dragging calculation and several discriminating observables. It remains below S scientific because its genuinely new sectors have no positive empirical signal, significant Standard-Model structure is still input, and the most radical unification claims are less independently tested. Interest S is separate: extra GW polarizations, space-interferometer null channels, dark-vector phenomenology and the unusual gauge/gravity/hyper-spin architecture make the programme unusually generative even if the scientific verdict remains open.

**Clash — 3BF Higher-Gauge Unification.** Battleground: **gravity + realistic matter with empirical discrimination**. GQFT/GSM has the stronger phenomenological surface today: classical/PPN bounds, frame dragging, distinctive GW polarizations and a new vector-DM EFT. Its liability is that much Standard-Model structure remains imported and the hyperunified extension adds large speculative commitments without comparable independent validation. 3BF has the cleaner higher-categorical common framework for Einstein–Cartan gravity, gauge fields and chiral matter and an explicit nonperturbative path-integral construction; its liability is that continuum/low-energy recovery and direct experimental discrimination are much less developed. A confirmed non-GR polarization or independently reproduced GSM dark-sector signal would strongly favor GQFT/GSM in this battleground; conversely, a controlled 3BF continuum limit deriving realistic chiral phenomenology with a novel successful prediction would narrow the gap. This clash does **not** move 3BF from B/A.

**Movement history.** First Arena entry on 2026-09-23 at A/S, medium confidence. This is explicitly a backlog correction triggered by current September 2026 phenomenology, not a claim that GQFT was invented this week. Future tier movement requires more than another internal paper: positive empirical discrimination, strong independent replication of the new sectors, or a demonstrated failure/contradiction in the load-bearing derivations.
