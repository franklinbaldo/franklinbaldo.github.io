## Entered as adjacent: Singularity as Class-Relative Non-Extendibility — NR scientific / A interest, high confidence

**Origin / date / source.** This is a self-authored, unpublished and exploratory research front in `franklinbaldo/papers`, merged on **2026-09-24** in commit `add579ad5574f8ddc0646bde3864f69bd6e5ec91` (#1389). The decision-bearing source is `formalizations/research_contracts/SingularityRelativeExtendibility.lean`: <https://github.com/franklinbaldo/papers/blob/add579ad5574f8ddc0646bde3864f69bd6e5ec91/formalizations/research_contracts/SingularityRelativeExtendibility.lean>. A related same-day contract, `CapabilityIdentityUnderCollapse.lean`, is at commit `a346e4be27fbb761115bb75a08aba5d56ddeff7a`: <https://github.com/franklinbaldo/papers/blob/a346e4be27fbb761115bb75a08aba5d56ddeff7a/formalizations/research_contracts/CapabilityIdentityUnderCollapse.lean>.

**Proposal.** The front asks whether a trajectory that cannot continue inside one declared class is genuinely singular, or merely non-extendible relative to that class, and when a larger class that restores continuation still describes the same system. The Lean contract defines `SingularIn` as failure of global continuation inside a class, proves its equivalence to a finite maximal existence horizon, relates failure of class closure to existence of a singular datum, and proves a monotonicity result: enlarging the class can remove class-relative singularities but cannot create them. It then proposes `CanonicalEnlargement` — inclusion plus global and unique development — as a candidate same-system criterion. A toy dynamics is singular in a small class, global in two larger classes, but canonical in only one because the other branches.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The contribution is unusually explicit for a semantic claim: the core statements are executable Lean propositions rather than prose analogies, and the repository records both the proof contract and its limits. The toy model demonstrates a nontrivial distinction between mere continuation and canonical continuation. The companion capability-identity work strengthens the conceptual boundary by proving that, for a surjective collapse, inherited tasks descend exactly when they respect collapse fibers, while a fiber-splitting task is lost. That supplies a second formal lens on the question of when a transformed representation preserves what the original system could do.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The present result is deliberately abstract. Time is `Nat`; the dynamics is a step relation; there are no norms, weak solutions, curvature tensors, causal structure, continuous time, or Navier-Stokes/Einstein equations. The one ambient step relation makes conservativity under enlargement true by construction rather than establishing it for a physical extension. The `Selected` enlargement in the toy model is an ad hoc selection rule, so uniqueness does not yet answer which continuations count as physically legitimate. Nothing in the contract derives quantum gravity, Standard-Model matter, or an empirical prediction. Kernel checking therefore validates the encoded mathematics, not the physical applicability of the premise.

**Placement.** **NR scientific / A interest, high confidence.** `NR` is a scope decision under the Arena schema: the work is fundamental-physics-adjacent methodology, not a gravity-plus-Standard-Model unification candidate. A interest is separate and reflects the potential leverage of a precise class-relative definition for auditing claims that a singularity has been “resolved”, especially where a theory changes solution class, ontology, or continuation rule. S is withheld because the present formalism has not yet been instantiated in a load-bearing physical example.

**Clash — Loop Quantum Gravity.** Battleground: **what counts as resolving a singularity rather than changing the admissible description.** The local formalism's advantage is a clean criterion separating non-extendibility, class enlargement, global continuation and uniqueness; its liability is that it says nothing yet about real gravitational dynamics. Loop Quantum Gravity's advantage is an actual background-independent quantum-gravity programme with concrete singularity-resolution results in symmetry-reduced settings and a mature mathematical literature; its liability is that the physical completion/recovery of full four-dimensional dynamics remains nontrivial. The comparison would move materially if `CanonicalEnlargement` were instantiated for a genuine GR or quantum-gravity singularity with physically justified admissibility and conservativity conditions. The clash does **not** move Loop Quantum Gravity from A/A.

**Movement history.** First Arena placement: **unranked → adjacent NR/A** on 2026-09-24. This is an inclusion decision, not a scientific promotion. Because the work is local, self-authored and unpublished, it receives no provenance bonus from being in `franklinbaldo/papers`.
