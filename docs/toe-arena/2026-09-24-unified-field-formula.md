## Dijana Tolic, “Unified Field Formula” — screened, not promoted to canonical contender

**Source and date.** Dijana Tolic, *Unified Field Formula*, rxiVerse `2609.0051`, v1 submitted **2026-09-22 20:23:44**: <https://rxiverse.org/abs/2609.0051>. The public record is an e-print, not a peer-reviewed journal article.

**Proposal.** The manuscript proposes a single “master action” containing an Einstein-Hilbert term, a fluid/plasma kinetic term, Maxwell electromagnetism, a Higgs-like scalar sector, a Yukawa term, and an additional time-dependent `R_pulse(t)` contribution. Its central interpretive move is an analogy between cardiac electrophysiology and a planet-to-vacuum closed loop: the core dynamo is treated as a pacemaker, ionospheric/magnetospheric plasma as branches of a circuit, the Higgs vacuum as a resting membrane potential, Yukawa coupling as conversion of a pulse into inertial structure, and `R_pulse(t)` as a universal ECG-like rhythm.

**Scope.** The claim is intentionally broad, spanning geophysics, plasma dynamics, gravity and quantum-field sectors. That makes it relevant to the ToE discovery pass, but breadth alone does not satisfy the Arena's unification/recovery gate.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The proposal is more specific than a purely verbal manifesto because it writes an explicit action and identifies the familiar sectors it wants to connect. A variational formulation is, in principle, the right kind of object from which equations of motion and consistency conditions could be derived. The manuscript also makes a concrete structural claim — that one dynamical rhythm should couple scales from planetary plasma to particle mass — which is falsifiable once the pulse term and cross-sector couplings are defined.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** In the current public formulation, the action mostly places already-known sector terms side by side. It does not derive the Standard Model gauge group, fermion representations, chirality, anomaly cancellation, generations, observed couplings or masses from a smaller common structure. The extra ingredient `R_pulse(t)` is not given a field definition, covariance law, dimensions, coupling structure, equation of motion or independently testable amplitude/frequency prediction. The cardiac/geophysical analogy therefore remains an interpretation rather than a demonstrated physical correspondence. No quantitative derivation is given connecting a planetary dynamo to Higgs/Yukawa dynamics, and no independent reproduction or empirical test of the proposed cross-scale rhythm was located.

A further concern is explanatory compression: writing the Einstein-Hilbert, Maxwell, scalar and Yukawa pieces in one integral is not itself a unification unless the framework explains why those terms, fields, representations and coefficients arise together. At present the construction imports most of the established structures it claims to unify and then adds a new pulse term whose physical content is unspecified.

**Provisional placement.** **F scientific / D interest, high confidence for the present public formulation.** The F is not a claim that every future theory with a rhythmic or cross-scale coupling is false. It records the rubric condition “not developed enough to count as a scientific theory” at the level required for a ToE contender: the novel term is undefined, the known sectors are juxtaposed rather than derived, and there is no discriminating quantitative prediction. Interest is **D** because the cardiac analogy is unusual but currently supplies little calculational or experimental surface beyond the already-known action terms. No canonical `knowledge/toe` card is created in this pass; the assessment remains a discovery/audit record.

**Clash — Geometric Action Principle (B/A).** Battleground: whether a “single action” actually compresses assumptions. Alvarez–Izaurieta–Quinzacara's Geometric Action Principle uses one Clifford-algebraic invariant to fix the relative structure and normalization of gravity, Yang–Mills and fermion kinetic terms, with explicit ghost-control questions and a technical derivation. Tolic's current formula instead sums familiar gravity/fluid/Maxwell/Higgs/Yukawa pieces and appends an undefined pulse. The latter could become a genuine unification mechanism only if the pulse/common structure *derives* sector content or produces new cross-sector constraints rather than co-locating known terms. This clash does **not** move the Geometric Action Principle from B/A.

**Recovery / promotion gate.** Reconsideration would require at minimum: (1) a covariant definition of `R_pulse` and its dynamical degrees of freedom; (2) dimensional consistency and a closed set of Euler–Lagrange/field equations; (3) a derivation or nontrivial constraint linking the gravity, gauge, scalar and Yukawa sectors rather than importing them independently; (4) recovery of realistic chiral Standard-Model structure; and (5) at least one frozen quantitative prediction distinguishing the rhythm mechanism from ordinary GR + MHD/plasma physics + the Standard Model.

**Movement history.** Newly screened on 2026-09-24 from a v1 submitted 2026-09-22. Provisional discovery-level assessment: **unranked → F/D**, high confidence, with no canonical contender card because minimum specification/recovery gates are not met.
