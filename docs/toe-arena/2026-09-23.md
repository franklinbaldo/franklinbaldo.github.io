### CronNet-Holo / 600-Cell Spectral Unification — enters C / interest S, low confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Luis Morató de Dalmases, CronNet-Holo Initiative. The earliest 600-Cell Spectral Triple release located in this audit was published on 2026-04-15: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19592588>. The decision-bearing current synthesis is *CronNet-Holo Framework: Holographic Computational Language*, published 2026-09-18, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22827896>. Relevant intermediate records include the 2026-07-07 *Spectral Unification* synthesis: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21238915>.

**Proposal.** The programme uses the regular 600-cell, binary icosahedral symmetry, spectral/noncommutative constructions, Hecke operators and an invariant 53 to seek a common origin for the Standard Model gauge group, three generations, Higgs structure and emergent gravity. Earlier records advertised a complete or parameter-free derivation. The September synthesis is more bounded: it explicitly makes the connection to physical reality conditional on five hypotheses, including the relevant representation-selection rule and the spectral-action bridge.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is not merely a verbal ontology: there is a substantial technical corpus, numerical machinery, group/spectral constructions and sharp advertised discriminators. The September synthesis predicts a vacuum-absorption feature at **12.8 ± 0.2 THz** with **0.15 ± 0.05 THz** linewidth, plus spectral-statistics, Weinberg-angle and CMB-modulation targets. The newer source also distinguishes its mathematical core from physical-realization assumptions, which materially improves auditability.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The five physical hypotheses are load-bearing and are not independently established. I found no independent reproduction of the claimed 600-cell → Standard Model + gravity map or the headline numerical predictions. The programme has also changed both formulation and claim strength during 2026; an adversarial reconstruction must establish which earlier results survive the current conditional framework instead of treating every historical claim as cumulative evidence. Agreements reported inside the same research programme are not independent confirmation.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, low confidence.** C matches a concrete mathematical proposal with a large calculational surface and real falsifiers but weak external validation and an unclosed physical bridge. S interest is justified by unusually sharp, laboratory-scale tests and the generative geometry-first comparison. A null result for the stated 12.8 THz prediction under an experiment that actually realizes the claimed observable would be unusually informative.

**Clash — Holomorphic UFT.** Battleground: explicit geometry-first recovery of matter + gravity. Holomorphic UFT currently has the stronger peer-reviewed/multi-paper lineage; CronNet-Holo makes sharper parameter-level and laboratory-scale claims. Independent reconstruction of the 600-cell representation-selection and spectral-action chain, followed by a successful genuinely prospective prediction, could narrow that maturity gap. No existing tier moves from this clash.

**Movement history.** First Arena entry on 2026-09-23. Historical claim evolution is recorded rather than erased: April-July sources used stronger complete/parameter-free language; the 2026-09-18 synthesis explicitly frames physical recovery as conditional on H1-H5.

### AME(4,6) Theory of Everything — enters C / interest S, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Adam Bazih. The synthesis paper was published on 2026-04-14: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19564202>. A contemporary status report is <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19579831>. The gravitational-sector record is <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19639544>. Several programme papers now carry a June 2026 notice that the original “zero free parameters” framing and multiple numerical predictions were partially retracted.

**Proposal.** The programme treats AME(4,6) quantum-information structure, modular tensor categories and Walker-Wang topological phases as a substrate from which Standard-Model flavor/gauge properties, neutrino and Higgs quantities, cosmology and gravity might emerge.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The underlying AME(4,6) perfect tensor is an independently established mathematical/quantum-information object; e.g. Rather et al., *Physical Review Letters* 128, 080507 / arXiv:2104.05122. The ToE programme itself is unusually explicit about its scorecard: its April status report records several claims rejected as overfitting, unresolved quantities and quantitative failures instead of silently dropping them. The corpus has concrete particle/cosmology calculations and named future discriminators rather than only a philosophical mapping.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** Independent existence of the AME state is not evidence that its quantum dimensions/fusion data are the laws or constants of nature. That mapping is the core physical hypothesis and lacks independent derivation. The gravity sector remains exploratory: Walker-Wang/topological constructions motivate Newton scaling, cosmological-constant mechanisms and a composite spin-2 graviton, but do not yet amount to controlled low-energy Einstein recovery. Multiple headline claims were softened or partially retracted in 2026, so surviving claims must be tested under the revised assumptions. I found no independent programme reproducing the AME → Standard Model + gravity chain.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** C recognizes substantial formal structure, multiple calculations and an unusually visible negative-results/retraction history, while keeping the missing physical bridge and weak independent scrutiny central. S interest reflects the highly generative quantum-information route to unification and the unusually useful falsification history; it does not upgrade scientific confidence.

**Clash — Observer Patch Holography.** Battleground: information-theoretic reconstruction of physical law. OPH currently has stronger formal reconstruction/certification machinery and a more explicit ledger of physical-realization debt. AME(4,6) reaches further into parameter-level phenomenology but has more overfitting/retraction history and a much less developed gravity derivation. The clash does not warrant a tier move for OPH or AME.

**Movement history.** First Arena entry on 2026-09-23 at C/S. The historical record explicitly preserves the programme's April strong claims, April self-audit, and June partial retractions rather than treating the current card as if those changes never occurred.
