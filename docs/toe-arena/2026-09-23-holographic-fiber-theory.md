**Proposal.** HFT models the vacuum as an informationally discrete trivalent cell complex whose continuum topology realizes the Hopf bundle `S^3 -> S^2`. Cohomology channels pair into tension and phase fields, braid constraints and a chirality-lock transition are claimed to fix `U(1)`, `SU(2)` and `SU(3)`, and trapped topological energy is used to construct the Standard-Model mass spectrum. The dynamical extension reads deterministic cell updates as a signed-measure Markov chain, claims the Tsirelson bound and path-integral measure after coarse-graining, and connects a short-time heat kernel plus a spin-2 tension mode to the Einstein-Hilbert gravitational sector.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The programme exposes far more audit surface than a purely verbal unification proposal: concrete topology, braid/cohomology machinery, explicit numerical claims and a growing collection of Python checks. The v24 record includes scripts for Bell/CHSH behavior, channel counts, control operators, curvature and drift gates. It also makes prospective claims that can in principle fail, notably `sum m_nu ~= 61.2 meV` and `T_CnuB = T_CMB ~= 2.725 K`.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** I found no independent peer-reviewed end-to-end reconstruction of the central HFT chain. Recovering familiar gauge groups from topology is not yet the same thing as independently deriving the observed chiral representations, anomaly cancellation, three generations, flavour structure and RG behavior. Likewise, obtaining the form of an Einstein-Hilbert term from a heat kernel and identifying a spin-2 mode do not by themselves establish controlled recovery of full low-energy GR. The numerical history also matters: v17 advertised a lightest neutrino mass near **0.8 meV**, whereas later releases moved it to roughly **1.77-1.8 meV**. That does not falsify the programme, but it means the prediction ledger must be frozen before future agreements can be treated as prospective evidence.

A second substantial recovery burden is the v24 claim that today's cosmic-neutrino-background temperature equals the CMB temperature near **2.725 K**. Standard relic-neutrino cosmology predicts about **1.95 K** after electron-positron annihilation heats photons relative to already decoupled neutrinos. Direct CnuB thermometry does not yet exist, so this is not a direct observational falsification. But any alternative thermal history must reproduce the successful BBN/CMB radiation-density constraints; absent that calculation, the 2.725 K claim is a serious unresolved cosmological gate rather than positive evidence.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, low confidence.** C follows the skill's `structured speculation` criterion: the mathematics/computation are concrete and the programme has discriminators, but the physical bridge, precision recovery and independent scrutiny remain weak. S interest is separate: the combination of Hopf topology, braids, quantum-correlation claims, mass predictions, gravity and executable tests is unusually generative for adversarial comparison.

**Clash — CronNet-Holo / 600-Cell Spectral Unification (C/S).** Battleground: **discrete/topological substrate -> realistic Standard Model + gravity + prospective discriminator**. HFT's advantage is a compact Hopf/braid ontology, executable control scripts and sharp neutrino/CnuB targets; its liabilities are version drift, weak external scrutiny and an unclosed cosmological/GR bridge. CronNet-Holo's advantage is a richer spectral/noncommutative construction and an unusually laboratory-like 12.8 THz target; its liabilities are likewise originator-driven validation and multiple load-bearing physical-realization hypotheses. A genuinely independent reconstruction plus a frozen successful prediction would change the comparison. **No existing tier moves from this clash.**
