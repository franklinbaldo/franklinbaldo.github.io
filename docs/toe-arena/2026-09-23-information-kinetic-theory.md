## Entered: Information-Kinetic Theory (IKT) — C scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Source / date.** Andrey Starikovskiy, *Information-Kinetic Theory: Unification of Gravity and the Standard Model on Discrete Quantum Walk Graphs*. Version 1 was posted on Preprints.org on **2026-06-09**; the current Version 2 was submitted 2026-06-21 and posted **2026-06-24**. The current version is explicitly marked as a non-peer-reviewed preprint.

**Proposal.** IKT makes a dynamic graph, rather than continuum spacetime or a list of fundamental fields, the microscopic substrate. Its local unitary quantum-walk operator acts on oriented edges carrying complex phase/spin data. Under prethermal coherent and statistically isotropic conditions, the coarse-grained dynamics is intended to yield a Weyl/wave envelope. Electromagnetism is represented by an Abelian link phase, strong interactions by an internal `SU(3)_route` color-routing interface, weak interactions by a chiral gateway, and gravity by phase refraction. Stable graph cycles act as particles; mass is a monodromic phase gap / algorithmic route cost; the Topological Transition Calculus replaces continuum UV integrals with finite sums over graph transitions. The same programme adds vortex dark matter, throughput/noise dark energy and a large prospective test matrix.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is structured speculation rather than a manifesto. The paper supplies an explicit graph Hilbert-space/quantum-walk kernel, a prethermal Floquet-to-Weyl route, equations for the interaction interfaces, a weak-field gravity/PPN construction, particle/topological-cycle machinery, cosmological sectors and a long list of proposed discriminators. It also exposes its own unfinished layers: graph-kernel constructions, phenomenological interfaces, channel/topological anchors and predictions are not presented as having identical evidential status. That separation makes the programme unusually auditable for a single-author independent proposal.

**Limits / uncertainties.** The title is stronger than the present derivational closure. The Standard Model is not yet recovered end-to-end from the graph kernel: familiar `U(1)`, `SU(3)`-route and chiral weak structures enter at interface level, while full running QCD / `Λ_QCD` / hadron spectrum remain incomplete and an observed chiral representation/anomaly/generation/Yukawa/CKM/PMNS/Higgs chain has not been independently reconstructed. Gravity is likewise strongest in the weak-field interface: the paper supplies an effective PPN route and proposes a strong-field continuation, but I found no external derivation showing that the microscopic update uniquely reproduces full nonlinear Einstein dynamics. The dark-sector construction still requires the joint CMB/LSS/BAO/SN/growth programme stated by the paper itself.

**Anchor audit.** IKT labels `α_top^-1 = 4π^3 + π^2 + π` as a leading no-fit channel/topological anchor while reserving running/polarization/matching corrections for later work. Numerically the stated leading expression is `137.0363037759`; CODATA 2022 gives `α^-1 = 137.035999177(21)`, an offset of about **2.22 ppm**. That is far outside the experimental uncertainty, so the bare anchor cannot count as a precision empirical success. Because the manuscript explicitly treats it as a leading anchor subject to further graph matching, the mismatch is not by itself a clean falsification of the whole framework either. The important gate is whether the required correction is independently derived before being tuned to the measured value.

**Independent scrutiny.** I located the Preprints.org corpus and mirrors/discovery pages, but no independent end-to-end reconstruction, peer-reviewed validation or programme-specific empirical success of the central graph → realistic Standard Model + gravity chain. This is the main reason not to place the programme at B despite its breadth.

**Tier decision.** **C scientific**: a concrete mathematical proposal with explicit microdynamics, continuum/interface calculations and falsifiable targets, but major load-bearing derivations and independent scrutiny are still missing. **S interest**: the programme is exceptionally generative for comparison and experiments because one discrete quantum-walk substrate is asked to carry gauge sectors, gravity, particles, dark sectors and several prospective high-energy/cosmological signatures. The S is an interest/fecundity score, not evidence that IKT is probably correct.

**Clash — The Lucron Model (C/A).** Battleground: discrete pregeometry → realistic Standard Model + gravity with explanatory compression. IKT's advantage is a more explicit unitary quantum-walk kernel, a Floquet/Weyl continuum route, a weak-field gravity interface and a broader predeclared test matrix; its liability is that many gauge/matter identifications remain interface-level and the whole chain is still first-party and non-peer-reviewed. Lucron's advantage is a compact single-scale ontology, an explicit DESI DR2 comparison and a useful published ledger of numerical failures; its liability is the material v3→v4 ontological change and the absence of independent derivation of the key candado/constant relations. The comparison does **not** move Lucron from C/A. A move of IKT toward B would require an independent reconstruction of either the realistic chiral Standard Model or nonlinear Einstein dynamics from the same graph operator, plus at least one frozen discriminating prediction that succeeds prospectively.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at **C/S**, medium confidence. This is an omission correction revealed by the current literature scan, not a promotion from a previous Arena tier.
