## Entered: Quantum-Gravitational-Informational Theory (QGI) — C scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin / dates / sources.** Marcos Aquino's first public QGI preprint was posted on **2025-05-13** as *Quantum–Gravitational–Informational Theory (QGI): A Unified Approach Based on Informational Principles*: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202505.0942/v1>. The decision-bearing source for this audit is the latest public **v4**, submitted 2025-11-05 and posted **2025-11-10**, retitled *Quantum-Gravitational-Informational Theory: A First-Principles Framework for Fundamental Physics*: <https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202505.0942>.

**Proposal.** QGI treats information as the primordial substrate of physics. It fixes a dimensionless informational constant, `alpha_info = 1/(8 pi^3 ln pi)`, from Liouville/phase-space normalization, a Jeffreys-prior construction and a Ward-closure condition, then propagates an informational deformation through gravity, neutrino/flavour physics, electroweak quantities and cosmology. The current manuscript presents a unified Lagrangian, zeta-function/spectral calculations for gravitational coupling, a topological neutrino spectrum using windings `{1,3,7}`, PMNS/flavour relations and several cosmological/electroweak observables.

**Evidence for / strengths.** QGI is substantially more concrete than a verbal informational ontology. The current manuscript supplies equations, a Lagrangian, explicit discrete constructions, numerical fits and a falsification section. It also offers several crisp prospective tests: a normal-ordering neutrino spectrum with `sum m_nu ~ 0.060 eV` and mass-squared-splitting ratio `1/30`, a conditioned electroweak slope relation, and a negative spectral correction to the effective gravitational coupling. The version history is informative rather than cosmetically smooth: earlier versions reported a positive gravitational spectral exponent, while v4 says corrected spectral formulas reverse the sign to approximately `-0.1355`. That correction is a scientific positive in transparency even though it lowers confidence that the derivation is yet stable.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The strongest issue is explanatory compression. The v4 scope notice explicitly fixes a heat-kernel truncation, SU(5) normalization, reference renormalization scales and several discrete geometric choices. More importantly, the displayed QGI Lagrangian still contains an Einstein-Hilbert term and `L_SM`; the framework deforms and correlates established structures but does not yet derive the full Standard Model and gravity uniquely from the informational postulates. The neutrino sector also illustrates why the paper's language of complete validation must be discounted: the atmospheric mass splitting is used to anchor the overall neutrino scale and is then reported as an exact agreement, so that particular match is not an independent prediction. The paper itself says targets are computed from existing PDG-2024 inputs, and the claimed DESI/BBN validation is a comparison against a cosmological-fit quantity rather than a direct DESI measurement of primordial helium. No independent peer-reviewed end-to-end reconstruction of the central QGI chain was located in this audit.

There is also a provenance/rhetoric mismatch worth preserving in the Arena record. The same v4 source describes the theory as having 'complete experimental validation' while later saying it is 'awaiting only experimental validation'. The Arena therefore treats existing numerical agreements as retrospective consistency checks unless the quantity was frozen prospectively before the relevant data, and it gives much more weight to the still-future neutrino/electroweak/gravity tests.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** C fits a structured mathematical proposal with broad scope, explicit calculations and testable claims, but weak independent scrutiny, imported low-energy structure, retrospective/anchored validation and material dependence on scheme/discrete choices. It is above D because there is a real formal/numerical programme to reproduce and falsify. It remains below B until at least one load-bearing derivation is independently reconstructed and a genuinely prospective prediction succeeds without re-fitting. S interest is separate: the framework has an unusually dense prediction ledger and several near/mid-term observations that could move it sharply up or down.

**Clash — Information Flux Theory (IFT).** Battleground: **explanatory compression plus independent reconstruction of an information-first gravity + Standard-Model chain.** QGI's concrete advantage is a more explicit versioned Lagrangian, numerical closure and named test protocols; its liability is that Einstein-Hilbert and the Standard Model remain visible ingredients and several headline agreements are anchored to existing measurements. IFT's advantage is a more radical attempt to collapse the field inventory to a single fermionic/evolution structure; its liability is a thinner independent validation record and a larger gap between its strongest unification claims and external reconstruction. A clean outsider reproduction of either theory's load-bearing derivation, followed by a frozen successful prediction, would materially change the comparison. The clash does **not** warrant moving IFT from C/S.

**Movement history.** QGI moves **watchlist/unranked → C/S** on 2026-09-23 after the dedicated audit promised in earlier scans. This is a first canonical placement, not a promotion from a prior scientific tier.
