## Entered: Hypercomplex Steering–Spinor Unification (E6/Sedenion) — C scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Source / date.** Jau Tang, *Emergent Gravity, Gauge Curvature, and Spacetime Geometry from E6 Hypercomplex Spinor Structure*, Preprints.org v1, posted **2026-05-27**. The matter-side lineage is older: Qiang Tang & Jau Tang published *Sedenion Algebra Model as an Extension of the Standard Model and Its Link to SU(5)* in *Symmetry* on **2024-05-17**, following 2023 arXiv work on three-generation sedenion constructions. The associator-gravity line was made explicit in 2025–2026, including a sedenionic Yukawa-gravity preprint on **2025-09-23** and an emergent-quantum-gravity preprint on **2026-05-05**.

**Proposal.** The programme uses a 16-dimensional Cayley–Dickson/sedenion steering-spinor algebra, split into Γ, Θ, U, V and W sectors, as a common substrate for spacetime, interactions, fermion generations and gravity. Γ is associated with spacetime/U(1), Θ with weak structure, mixed external/internal sectors with SU(3)-like color, and U/V/W with the three generations. Commutators of generalized steering derivatives are interpreted as Yang–Mills-like gauge curvature, while non-associative associators generate an effective metric and gravitational curvature. The strongest version therefore proposes a compact algebraic division of labor: commutator → gauge, associator → gravity, internal sectors → matter generations.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is more than a one-off unification note. The sedenion/matter side has a peer-reviewed 2024 publication and explicit 2023 precursor papers; the later gravity papers supply concrete generalized derivatives, bilinear metric candidates, commutator/associator curvature objects and weak-field calculations. The programme also exposes a large attack surface—three-generation structure, gauge embedding, Einstein/Newton/Schwarzschild-like limits, vacuum curvature and cosmological extensions—rather than insulating itself from calculation.

**Limits / uncertainties.** The central gravity-plus-realistic-SM chain remains first-party and incomplete. The E6 paper itself leaves complete quantization, non-associative operator structure, renormalization, zero-divisor control and the exact relationship to conventional E6 representations unresolved. Standard-Model recovery is still schematic: the work does not yet derive the full observed chiral representation content, anomaly cancellation, Higgs/Yukawa sector, CKM/PMNS structure, running couplings and measured spectrum from the substrate. On the gravity side, an effective metric and associator corrections are proposed, but I found no independent end-to-end reconstruction showing that the same non-associative connection yields full GR with controlled dynamics. The three-generation decomposition is suggestive but not yet shown to be uniquely forced by the underlying algebra rather than selected as a useful embedding.

**Tier decision.** **C scientific**: a structured mathematical programme with a real peer-reviewed precursor and a nontrivial multi-paper lineage, but with the decisive gravity+SM derivation, quantum/RG control and independent replication still missing. **S interest**: the commutator/gauge versus associator/gravity split and three-generation sedenion geometry are unusually compact, generative and easy to attack mathematically. The S is an interest/fecundity score, not evidence that the theory is likely true.

**Clash — E8 × ωE8 Octonionic Unification (B/S).** Battleground: exceptional/hypercomplex algebra → realistic matter + emergent gravity. Hypercomplex Steering–Spinor Unification is algebraically compact and has a peer-reviewed sedenion/SU(5) precursor, but its current E6/gravity bridge is mainly a first-party preprint construction with schematic SM recovery. E8 × ωE8 has a deeper generalized-trace-dynamics lineage, a broader quantitative/falsification programme and a more developed emergence story, while still lacking a complete common dynamical derivation and broad independent reproduction. This clash does **not** move E8 × ωE8 from B/S.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at **C/S**, medium confidence. This is an omission correction surfaced by the fresh literature scan, not a promotion from a previous tier. A move toward B would require an independently reconstructed derivation of either the realistic chiral SM sector or the Einstein limit from the same algebra, ideally coupled to a frozen discriminating prediction that succeeds prospectively.

## Screened/watchlist: Primordial Timeless Frequency Domain (PTFD) — provisional D scientific / A interest if forced, no canonical card yet

**Source / date.** Patrik Przyhoda's public exploratory notebook contains *Does Reality Compute Itself? A Spectral Ontology Beyond Information* (**July 2026**) and two explicitly Standard-Model/gauge-facing essays from **September 2026**, *A Spectral View of the Standard Model: From Gauge Symmetry to Modal Structure* and *Why Gauge Fields May Be Emergent*. The public pages give month-level dates for the September essays rather than a secure exact day, so the Arena should not invent one.

**Proposal.** PTFD treats a timeless frequency-domain substrate, coherence functionals and spectral/topological stabilization as more primitive than spacetime, particles and interactions. Gauge structure is interpreted as an effective organization of admissible phase/spectral configurations, and gravity is discussed as emergent from spectral structure rather than fundamental force dynamics.

**Why it is not canonical yet.** The author's own pages are unusually clear that the programme remains exploratory and partly heuristic. It has not derived the observed Standard-Model gauge group, a complete Yang–Mills action or particle spectrum, and the gravity treatment does not yet supply a controlled derivation of the Lorentzian metric plus Einstein equations. I found no peer review, independent reconstruction or distinctive validated prediction. If forced into Arena letters today, **D/A** would be the most defensible provisional reading: D for an early but formalizing hypothesis, A for its broad conceptual connectivity. I am keeping it in watchlist rather than assigning a canonical card until at least one load-bearing quantitative bridge is derived.

**Clash — Observer Patch Holography (B/S).** Both programmes make conventional spacetime secondary. Observer Patch Holography currently has formal reconstruction machinery, explicit consistency conditions and machine-checked components, while PTFD is still largely a spectral-ontology research programme whose physical correspondences are not derived end-to-end. PTFD's conceptual reach is interesting, but there is no basis for moving Observer Patch Holography.
