## Material entry

### Quantized Spacetime from First Principles — enters C / interest A, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Iván Moreno, *Quantized Spacetime from First Principles: A Unified Framework for Quantum Fields and Gravity*. Version 1 was published on 2025-09-11; v3 on 2025-09-12; the decision-bearing v4 was published on 2025-09-23. Current record: <https://zenodo.org/records/17187656>.

**Proposal.** The framework assumes the validity of quantum mechanics, the validity of general relativity and fundamental Planck-scale units of length and time. It reformulates quantum evolution on a time lattice, constructs a discrete QFT with scalar, U(1), SU(2) and SU(3) gauge sectors, and uses a scalar phase field to build an effective tensor metric whose claimed continuum dynamics recover the Levi-Civita connection, Ricci curvature and Einstein equations. Topological/network degrees of freedom are then related to internal fermionic state structure. The paper also advertises orbital, photon-decoherence and short-GRB tests.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is a genuine gravity-plus-gauge unification attempt rather than a quantum-gravity-only proposal. Its primary source is explicit about the assumptions, mathematical objects and several discriminating observables. The gauge and gravitational sectors are presented in a sufficiently concrete form that an outside group could in principle reconstruct or break the central derivation. That auditability is real scientific value even before the claims are independently confirmed.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The framework pays a substantial explanatory-compression cost by assuming quantum mechanics and general relativity before later presenting their discrete/emergent forms as recovered results. The inclusion of U(1), SU(2) and SU(3) does not by itself derive the observed chiral Standard Model: representations, anomaly cancellation, generations, masses, mixings and running couplings remain major gates. A fundamental lattice also has to recover Lorentz symmetry to precision constraints without preferred-frame or dispersion artifacts. This audit located no independent reproduction of the central gauge, metric or phenomenological chains and no mature peer-reviewed research programme around the framework. The paper's Mercury/LEO and related tests therefore count as within-programme claims, not independent empirical confirmation.

**Placement.** **C scientific / A interest, medium placement confidence.** C matches a concrete mathematical proposal with true ToE-scale scope and named falsifiers, but weak independent scrutiny and incomplete low-energy recovery. A interest reflects the unusually compact discrete-spacetime route and its useful comparison surface; it is not an endorsement of the ontology.

**Clash — Computational Finitism.** Battleground: discrete substrate -> precision Lorentz/QFT/Standard-Model/gravity recovery. Quantized Spacetime has the cleaner direct gauge-and-metric construction in its decision-bearing source, but inherits the awkward fact that QM and GR are assumptions as well as claimed recovered limits. Computational Finitism has a broader runnable simulation corpus, but its current recovery of precision known physics is weaker and its own audit records load-bearing rule modifications after explicit failures. This supports C/A for Quantized Spacetime versus D/S for Computational Finitism without moving the latter. An independent frozen-rule reconstruction of either programme would be the meaningful discriminator.

**Movement history.** The theory was explicitly left untiered on the 2026-09-23 watchlist pending primary-source reconstruction. This audit completes that reconstruction at Arena level and promotes it from watchlist to canonical contender at C/A. That is a catalogue-status change, not a scientific promotion based on new evidence.
