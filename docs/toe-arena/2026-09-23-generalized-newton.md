# ToE Arena scan — 2026-09-23 — Generalized Newton candidate

This incremental scan applies `franklinbaldo/skills/toe-arena-tiering`: scientific strength and interest are separate comparative judgements, never probabilities that a theory is true. A new paper alone does not move a tier; a movement or entry needs an auditable change in evidence, consistency, scope or falsifiability.

## Entered: Generalized Newton's Laws / A Candidate Theory of Everything — F scientific / A interest, high confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Dipeng Li, *A Candidate Theory of Everything*, Cambridge Open Engage, Version 1, published **2026-07-20**, DOI `10.33774/coe-2026-hcrbq`: <https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6a5c9571d1922e37d59f96be>. Cambridge labels it a working paper and explicitly states that it had not been peer reviewed at posting. The synthesis cites a self-authored 2025-2026 sequence on generalized Newton laws, symmetry of interactions, gravity/inertia and a Koide-formula interpretation.

**Proposal.** The paper generalizes Newtonian organization from mass to electric charge, color and fermion-generation properties. It divides interactions into active forces (gravity, Coulomb, weak) and passive forces (inertia, magnetism, strong), associates the dimensionality of passive-force action with the number of intrinsic property types, and argues that active/passive pairs should be symmetric. From this it proposes: (1) a high-energy strong/weak symmetry with massless neutral X/Y mediators and colored high-energy leptonic/weak states; (2) a gravity/inertia unification in which the Higgs field mediates inertial force and gravitational/inertial total energies are equal; and (3) a staged cosmic-origin picture in which 1D, 2D and 3D space appear together with successively richer particle/interaction content.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The framework is explicit enough to fail. It states four principles, publishes equations and several quantitative or qualitative consequences rather than using the ToE label as branding alone. It exposes a sharp cosmic-budget consequence — 50% dark energy from equal total gravitational and inertial energies — and openly records that the result disagrees with the observational value used by the paper. The 2026 synthesis also connects multiple earlier manuscripts into a single dependency chain, so the Arena can state concrete recovery gates instead of evaluating a one-off essay.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The decisive problem is self-reported. The paper states that its gravity-inertia symmetry predicts dark energy should contribute **50%** of total mass-energy and then states that current observations give about **68%**, calling this a conflict. It conjectures observational/computational flaws or cancellations, but does not derive a quantitative correction from the stated rules. This is not merely an unsolved side calculation: equal gravitational/inertial total energies are presented as a central consequence of the generalized laws. Planck/ESA's concordance cosmology independently places dark energy near 68%, so the discrepancy is not a tiny precision residual.

The strong/weak construction has separate structural problems. The paper treats the three QCD color labels as positive scalar numbers in a Koide-style formula and argues that QCD requires a resulting `Q` near `2/3`; it also proposes that at sufficiently high energies leptons and W/Z bosons gain color and decay into quark-based states and that massless neutral X/Y bosons mediate the symmetric weak interaction. No gauge Lagrangian, representation derivation, anomaly cancellation, threshold calculation, cross section or collider-compatibility analysis is supplied. Principle 2 additionally says acceleration can be converted to an average velocity through a relation written as `a/2` without the time factor required dimensionally. These issues would block a high tier even without the cosmological conflict.

**Relationship to established physics.** The proposal does not recover the Standard Model or general relativity as controlled limits. It reorganizes familiar objects into active/passive analogies, then infers new symmetry statements. The strongest current scientific test is therefore not whether the analogies are suggestive, but whether the additional consequences survive existing data. On the current version, the flagship 50% cosmic-budget consequence does not. The Arena treats the author's own acknowledgement of that mismatch as evidence against the current formulation, while treating the openness about it as a positive feature of the programme's auditability.

**Placement.** **F scientific / A interest, high confidence.** F is scoped to the **current published formulation**. Under the Arena rubric, a theory can be F when it is contradicted by strong evidence as presented; here the theory itself identifies a load-bearing cosmological prediction and acknowledges its disagreement with observation, without a calculated repair. The high placement confidence comes from the contradiction being stated in the decision-bearing source rather than inferred from a fragile external interpretation. **A interest** is separate: the active/passive-force taxonomy, strong/weak symmetry and staged dimensional cosmology are compact and generative enough to produce many clean adversarial tests, but that does not upgrade scientific strength.

**Distinctive tests / recovery gates.** The fastest recovery gate is a pre-specified quantitative derivation of the cosmic energy budget from the same gravity-inertia rules, including perturbation/expansion observables rather than only a revised headline fraction. A second gate is a QFT-level strong/weak construction that produces the proposed X/Y sector or colored high-energy leptonic states with calculable masses, widths and cross sections while preserving known gauge/chiral structure. A third gate is dimensional and dynamical consistency of the four generalized principles themselves. Independent reproduction is required before any movement beyond an initial recovery tier.

**Clash — Shaking Tangled Dimensions.** Battleground: **incompleteness versus contradiction with established physics**. Shaking Tangled Dimensions remains **D scientific / A interest**. It is less compact and still lacks controlled continuum recovery of Lorentz invariance, GR and the full chiral Standard Model, but its current Arena record is primarily incomplete rather than built around a load-bearing prediction the source itself says disagrees with observation. The Generalized Newton candidate's advantage is sharper claims and a smaller conceptual rule set; its liability is that one of those sharp central consequences already fails its own observational comparison. The clash therefore justifies the D/F distinction but does not move Shaking Tangled Dimensions.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at **F/A, high confidence**. This is an entry, not a relegation. If a future version derives the observed cosmic budget from frozen rules and replaces the strong/weak analogies with a consistent field-theoretic construction, F should be reconsidered; a repaired but still founder-only, unreviewed framework would normally return first to D/C rather than leap to a mature tier.
