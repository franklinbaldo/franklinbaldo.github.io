## Entered: Weyl–Dirac–Born–Infeld (WDBI) unification — B scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Source / date.** D. M. Ghilencea, *Unification of gravity and standard model: Weyl-Dirac-Born-Infeld action*, Physical Review D 112, 125015, published **2025-12-10**, DOI 10.1103/n6b5-v8dn. The line remained active in 2026 with *The fall and the rise of Weyl gauge theory* (arXiv:2604.07508, **2026-04-08**) and Ghilencea & Mandric, *Weyl conformal geometry vs Riemannian geometry of Weyl invariant dressed metric* (arXiv:2606.08080, **2026-06-06**).

**Proposal.** WDBI generalizes Dirac–Born–Infeld structure to the Standard Model and Weyl gauge geometry. Its determinant action is constructed to remain both SM-gauge and Weyl-gauge invariant in arbitrary dimension. The low-order expansion yields a Weyl-invariant Standard Model plus Weyl quadratic gravity; Stueckelberg breaking gives a massive Weyl gauge field, restores Riemannian geometry and recovers Einstein–Hilbert gravity and ordinary Standard-Model physics below the Planck scale. The strongest quantum claim is that spacetime curvature itself supplies the ultraviolet regulator, eliminating the external regulator scale that would otherwise break Weyl gauge symmetry.

**Evidence / strengths.** This is not a one-off speculative manuscript: the construction sits on peer-reviewed work on the Standard Model in Weyl geometry, spontaneous Weyl breaking, gauge formulations of Weyl quadratic gravity and Weyl-anomaly control. The PRD paper gives a concrete unified action and a defined low-energy limit. Related Weyl-geometric gravity has also been studied by authors outside the WDBI paper in black-hole and galactic contexts, so at least portions of the surrounding gravitational machinery have an external technical literature.

**Limits / uncertainties.** WDBI unifies an already-specified Standard Model with gravity; it does **not** derive the observed gauge group, chiral representations, three generations, anomaly cancellation, Yukawa structure, CKM/PMNS or the measured dimensionless couplings. The action still contains dimensionless couplings, so generating scales geometrically does not solve the full parameter problem. The curvature-as-UV-regulator / no-Weyl-anomaly claim is mathematically substantive but has not become an independently reconstructed, empirically selected ultraviolet completion. Nor is there yet a crisp WDBI-specific observation that distinguishes the framework from neighboring Weyl-gauge or quadratic-gravity models.

**Tier decision.** **B scientific**: a serious peer-reviewed gauge–gravity–SM construction with nontrivial formal consistency and a concrete Einstein/SM infrared limit, but without independent end-to-end reconstruction, derivation of realistic matter from a smaller principle, or theory-specific empirical success. **A interest**: geometric regularization and scale generation are unusually economical and make the programme highly useful for comparison, but that interest score is not evidence of truth.

**Clash — Geometric Action Principle for Gauge–Gravity–Matter Unification (B/A).** Battleground: how much structure a single geometric action genuinely explains. WDBI is more mature, peer-reviewed and already embeds the complete Standard Model while providing an explicit Stueckelberg route to Einstein gravity. The Alvarez–Izaurieta–Quinzacara action potentially compresses more because gravity, Yang–Mills and fermion kinetic structure descend from one Clifford invariant, but it still lacks a realistic Standard-Model representation. Neither has a distinctive empirical discriminator or independent end-to-end reconstruction, so the comparison does **not** move the existing B/A tier.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at **B/A**, medium confidence. This is an omission correction, not a scientific promotion. Promotion toward A would require an independent reconstruction of the ultraviolet regularization claim plus either a substantially deeper derivation of the Standard Model or a frozen discriminating prediction that succeeds prospectively.
