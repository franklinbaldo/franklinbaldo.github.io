### Tomislav Mandić, *Theory of everything* / “circulatory force” — provisional F scientific / D interest, low confidence; no canonical card

**Source / date.** Tomislav Mandić, *Theory of everything*, aiXiv `aixiv.260923.000002`, v1.0, published 2026-09-23. The public record labels the item **Human PROPOSAL** and reports an official review as completed. Primary: https://aixiv.science/abs/aixiv.260923.000002

**What it claims.** The accessible abstract proposes a fifth fundamental interaction called the **circulatory force** and says it explains dark matter, dark energy and subatomic interactions; it also makes claims about healing oncology patients and people with psychiatric disorders.

**Scope.** The advertised scope is broad enough to trigger ToE screening because it claims a new fundamental force plus dark-sector and microscopic explanatory reach. However, the public material accessible in this audit does not expose a gravity + chiral Standard-Model reconstruction, a Lagrangian or equivalent dynamical definition, coupling structure, symmetry content, recovery limits, or quantitative discriminating predictions.

**Evidence for / strengths.** There is a stable public identifier, explicit authorship/date and a concrete central claim — a new fifth force — rather than only a generic statement that “everything is connected.” That makes later version tracking possible. No independent empirical confirmation was located in this pass.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** On the accessible public record, the proposal is not developed enough to evaluate as a physical theory: no equations or mathematical definition of the circulatory force were available to this audit, no derivation of GR or `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)` was visible, and no numerical fifth-force signature was stated. The medical-treatment claims are especially high-burden claims and the accessible record provides no clinical evidence or mechanism adequate to count them as scientific support. An aiXiv “proposal/review completed” label is provenance about the platform workflow, not peer review or experimental validation.

**Provisional placement.** If forced through the Arena letters using only the currently accessible formulation, the result is **F scientific / D interest, low confidence**. F follows the rubric condition “not developed enough to count as a scientific theory” in the relevant form; it is not a claim that every future formulation of a circulatory-force idea is false. Interest is D because the current abstract offers too little technical attack surface to be strongly generative despite its very broad claims. Confidence is low because the audit could not inspect a sufficiently detailed full derivation; a later public version with mathematics or data should trigger re-evaluation.

**Discriminating / recovery gate.** A canonical Arena card would require, at minimum: (1) a mathematical definition of the circulatory field/force and its dimensions, symmetries and couplings; (2) a clear relation to known gravity and Standard-Model interactions; (3) at least one frozen quantitative prediction that differs from existing fifth-force/dark-sector models; (4) confrontation with existing fifth-force and cosmological constraints; and (5) removal of, or appropriate clinical evidence for, the medical claims.

**Clash — Generalized Newton's Laws / A Candidate Theory of Everything (F/A).** Battleground: minimum specification and falsifiability for a presently non-viable broad-unification proposal. The Generalized Newton candidate is also F scientifically, but it exposes equations and numerical consequences that can be checked — including its own 50% dark-energy consequence — so it has a much larger adversarial test surface and remains A interest. Mandić's currently accessible proposal is less developed: the fifth force is named but not yet publicly specified well enough for comparable calculations. This clash does **not** move Generalized Newton; it explains why equal scientific letters need not imply equal interest or maturity within F.

**History.** 2026-09-23 public v1.0 proposal → 2026-09-24 discovered and screened → no canonical card; provisional F/D, low confidence, pending a technically inspectable formulation.

### Drewes et al., *Sterile Neutrino Dark Matter Cries for GeV Heavy Neutral Leptons* — no ToE tier

**Source / date.** Marco Drewes, Yannis Georis, Juraj Klarić, Mikhail Shaposhnikov and Inar Timiryasov, arXiv:2609.28457, submitted 2026-09-23 and updated 2026-09-24. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28457

The paper performs a new parameter scan of the νMSM with improved quantum kinetic equations and finds a bounded region in which right-handed neutrinos can jointly account for neutrino masses, baryogenesis and sterile-neutrino dark matter, with GeV HNLs accessible to SHiP, LHC and future lepton-collider searches. This is unusually broad and testable particle/cosmology unification, but it has no quantum-gravity or gravitational-unification sector. It is therefore **rejected from canonical ToE ranking on scope, not scientific quality**.

### Turiaci & Wu, *Collapse to extremality: The third law of black hole thermodynamics in quantum gravity* — no ToE tier

**Source / date.** Gustavo J. Turiaci and Chih-Hung Wu, arXiv:2609.26875, submitted 2026-09-22 and updated 2026-09-24. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26875
