## Entered: Stochastic Helicity Unification (Ghose) — C scientific / A interest, medium confidence

**Origin / dates / sources.** The decision-bearing synthesis is Partha Ghose, *A New Approach to Unification*, first submitted 2025-08-24 and revised 2025-08-29: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19280>. Its immediate technical precursor is Partha Nandi & Partha Ghose, *Stochastic Quantization of Electrodynamics and Linearized Gravity*, submitted 2025-08-13: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10190>. The spin-network continuation is Nandi, Ghose & Francesco Petruccione, *Spinning into Quantum Geometry: Dirac and Wheeler-DeWitt Dynamics from Stochastic Helicity*, submitted 2025-10-12: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10836>. A 2026 continuation, Ghose, *Electrodynamics as a Theory of Persistent Stochastic Processes*, submitted 2026-05-23, develops the same ontology while explicitly stating that it is not intended to alter successful QED predictions: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24494>.

**Proposal.** The programme takes finite-speed stochastic processes rather than canonical quantum mechanics as fundamental. Velocity/helicity reversals driven by Poisson/Kac-type dynamics generate telegrapher/master equations; analytic continuation is used to obtain Dirac- and Maxwell-like evolution. The synthesis then generalizes the Riemann-Silberstein construction toward non-Abelian fields and couples internal gauge labels to spin networks. Helicity-resolved stochastic evolution on the network is proposed as a common dynamical mechanism for matter and geometry, with long-time equilibrium producing a Wheeler-DeWitt-type condition and large-scale limits intended to recover quantum-field and gravitational behavior.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The proposal is mathematically concrete enough to audit: there are explicit master equations, finite propagation speeds, a fundamental length scale, a weak-coupling non-Abelian expansion and a later spin-network stochastic model. The follow-up papers develop different load-bearing pieces rather than only repeating the synthesis, and Petruccione's participation broadens the technical team beyond the original pair. Relative to many Arena C/D entries, the programme also has a clear relationship to established stochastic mechanics, Riemann-Silberstein field descriptions and loop-quantum-gravity kinematics, so its assumptions and inheritance can be compared rather than guessed.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The central unification chain is incomplete. In the synthesis, the non-Abelian construction is explicitly left as something whose gauge-invariant realization and stochastic origin remain to be established; the weak-coupling expansion only suggests the desired interaction structure. The spin-network section is presented as a blueprint/conceptual framework, while full Einstein recovery is expected from semiclassical spin-network behavior rather than derived from the new stochastic dynamics. The Standard-Model groups `SU(3)`, `SU(2)` and `U(1)` are assigned as internal labels rather than emerging uniquely, and the observed chiral representations, three generations, anomaly cancellation, masses, mixings and running couplings are not derived. I did not locate an independent end-to-end reproduction of the stochastic-to-Standard-Model-plus-gravity chain. Empirical discrimination is also weak: the 2026 electrodynamics continuation says the ontology is meant to preserve QED's successful predictions rather than produce a modification.

**Placement.** **C scientific / A interest, medium confidence.** C fits a structured mathematical speculation with multiple technical papers and meaningful derivation targets, but missing full low-energy recovery, weak independent scrutiny and little novel empirical discrimination. It is above D because the programme has explicit equations and follow-up machinery across field and spin-network sectors; it remains below B until at least one load-bearing bridge is independently completed. A interest is separate: using the same stochastic/helicity mechanism across quantum foundations, fields and quantum geometry is unusually generative even if it currently has limited evidential force.

**Clash — Loop Quantum Gravity / spinfoams.** Battleground: **background-independent quantum geometry with dynamics and realistic matter**. Ghose's programme has the concrete advantage of proposing a microscopic stochastic/helicity dynamics and attaching gauge/matter labels to spin-network edges; its liability is that it inherits the hard semiclassical-gravity problem while adding a new stochastic layer and still imports the observed gauge groups. LQG/spinfoams have much deeper mathematical development, quantum-geometry kinematics and gravitational calculations; their liability is that complete low-energy recovery and realistic Standard-Model matter incorporation remain difficult. A controlled derivation from frozen stochastic rules to Einstein gravity plus an anomaly-consistent chiral Standard Model, followed by independent reproduction and a genuinely prospective discriminator, would materially narrow the gap. The clash does **not** warrant a tier movement for the existing LQG/spinfoam entry.

**Movement history.** The programme was placed on the Arena watchlist earlier on 2026-09-23 because the abstract-level scope was broad enough to merit a dedicated audit. This full-text pass moves it from watchlist to a canonical **C/A** entry. That movement is an inclusion decision, not a scientific promotion from a previous letter tier.
