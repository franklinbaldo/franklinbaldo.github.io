### Observer Patch Holography — enters B / interest S, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance:** Bernhard Mueller and collaborators, Pragma Research and associated groups. Earliest public provenance securely located in this audit is the public GitHub repository creation on 2026-01-18; the decision-bearing source for this round is the current technical release **r2045, published 2026-09-22**, containing sixteen first-party papers. The flagship is *Finite Observer Consensus as a Reconstruction Principle: Normal Forms, the Standard Model Lie Type, and a Route to the Einstein Field Equation*.

**Proposal:** OPH treats finite observer patches, their boundary readbacks, overlap consistency, durable records and authorized repair moves as the primitive architecture. The programme argues that public normal forms and finite response structure can reconstruct quantum-record identities, an intrinsic rank-three spatial carrier, Lorentzian kinematics, the Standard Model gauge Lie type and an anomaly-free fifteen-state generation; a separately premised physical/geometric reconstruction route yields the Einstein field equation. The broader claim is that these are different readouts of one observer-consensus architecture rather than independent physical inputs.

**Evidence for:** the programme is unusually explicit and auditable for a new unification claim. The r2045 library contains sixteen technical papers; several results are machine-checked or supplied with executable certificates; and the flagship classifies claims as finite theorems, certified computations, reconstruction implications, diagnostics, unsupplied realization maps or prospective tests. Within the declared finite model it reports non-trivial results including density-operator probability identities, the Tsirelson ceiling, no-cloning, a rank-three carrier, the local `u(1) + su(2) + su(3)` Lie type and one anomaly-free fifteen-state generation. It also exposes a branch-scoped frozen anisotropy prediction rather than counting measured-value fits as predictions.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** the physical bridge is explicitly incomplete. The flagship lists five standing unsupplied realization obligations: physical position/length/refinement, clock and energy units, finite response to physical gauge current plus matter action and continuum operator, one common physical refinement carrying all sectors, and an action generated by the source rather than declared. Particle masses are not derived, the fine-structure and cosmological-constant surfaces consume measured coordinates and are labelled diagnostics, and completed cosmology is not claimed. The twelve-port oriented-icosahedral carrier and spherical support are substantive architectural choices; independent reconstruction must establish that the Standard Model output is not effectively encoded by these choices. Independent empirical validation and external technical scrutiny remain far below mature A-tier programmes.

**Distinctive tests / falsifiers:** the framework itself distinguishes branch-level failure from whole-framework failure. The cleanest current prospective surface is the primitive-port propagation branch whose first symmetry-allowed anisotropic harmonic is fixed at angular rank six, conditional on the named physical branch. More important scientifically is whether the five physical-realization maps can actually be constructed together; failure there would undercut the unification interpretation even if the finite theorems remain correct mathematics.

**Placement:** **B/S, medium confidence.** B is justified by the breadth, non-trivial formal structure, public multi-paper programme, executable/machine-checked surface and explicit falsification bookkeeping. It is not A because the common physical realization is still missing and there is little independent validation. Interest S is deliberately separate: observer-consensus reconstruction, formal verification and the breadth of explicit discriminants make the programme exceptionally generative even under substantial scientific uncertainty.

**Clash — Geometric Action Principle:** battleground is *physical attachment versus reconstruction breadth*. OPH currently reconstructs a much wider chain and exposes its theorem/falsification ledger more sharply. The Geometric Action Principle, however, starts much closer to conventional continuum gauge fields, fermions and gravity and therefore carries less physical-identification debt. A common OPH refinement that simultaneously realizes events, currents, fields, action and scales would materially strengthen OPH; inability to construct it would push the comparison toward the Geometric Action programme. No movement of Geometric Action Principle from B/A follows today.

**Clash — String/M-theory:** OPH has a compact, inspectable finite architecture and unusually explicit failure conditions; String/M-theory has vastly deeper independent mathematical development, ultraviolet machinery and decades of scrutiny. OPH's auditability and low number of declared continuous inputs do not compensate for its immature physical bridge. String/M-theory remains A/S.

**History:** 2026-09-22 enters B/S, medium confidence. This is a first Arena placement, not a claim that r2045 makes OPH scientifically established. A future failure of the physical-realization programme could move it down to C even if the finite theorems survive; independent physical reconstruction plus discriminating empirical success would be the kind of evidence needed for A.

### Causal Fermion Systems — enters A / interest S, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance:** Felix Finster and collaborators. The explicit paper *Causal Fermion Systems as a Candidate for a Unified Physical Theory* first appeared on arXiv on **2015-02-12**, although the fermionic-projector programme is older. This is not a newly invented theory: the Arena is correcting a backlog omission because CFS is being discussed again this week at the 2026-09-21–25 conference *Curvature, Optimal Transport, and Gravity*, where a current 2026 derivation of Einstein dynamics from the causal action is on the programme. The decision-bearing recent paper is Finster & Krpoun, *A Geometric Derivation of the Einstein Equations from the Causal Action Principle*, submitted **2026-07-15**.

**Proposal:** CFS takes a Hilbert space, a set of finite-rank self-adjoint operators and a universal measure as fundamental. Spacetime, causal and metric structure, particles and fields arise from the resulting causal fermion system, whose dynamics are selected by the causal action principle. In the continuum-limit programme, the effective macroscopic equations recover interacting Dirac matter and classical bosonic fields; the published construction reaches the interactions of the Standard Model plus gravity, while the 2026 geometric paper derives a Lorentzian metric and Einstein equations under a smooth-support hypothesis.

**Evidence for:** this is a mature technical programme with monographs, a recent textbook, peer-reviewed mathematical work and a continuing research community. The project's continuum-limit account is unusually broad: it reports the Standard Model interactions plus gravity, three generations as a condition for deterministic effective equations, the post-symmetry-breaking Standard Model Lagrangian, and Einstein equations with higher-curvature corrections. The 2026 record adds a peer-reviewed relational many-body ontology for emergent spacetime and a direct geometric Einstein-equation derivation from the causal action.

**Evidence against / uncertainty:** CFS does not derive all low-energy numbers from one microscopic theory. Its continuum-limit documentation states that the precise coupling constants, gauge-boson masses and mixing angles depend on the ultraviolet regularization, which stands for unknown microscopic spacetime structure, so those quantities are taken as empirical parameters. The vacuum construction also requires choices such as sectors, numbers of Dirac seas/generations, masses and chiral-symmetry breaking, and the cited Standard-Model construction says the Higgs-sector field equations are not yet fully worked out. Distinctive experimentally confirmed microscopic signatures remain limited, and much of the deep technical development is still concentrated in the CFS research network.

**Distinctive tests / falsifiers:** the most valuable current discrimination surface is whether the regularization-dependent correction hierarchy can be turned into source-level predictions rather than merely absorbing measured low-energy parameters. The 2026 Einstein derivation identifies systematic power-series corrections in the regularization length; a robust, regulator-controlled prediction that disagreed with gravitational or particle data would be a meaningful falsifier of the corresponding physical realization.

**Placement:** **A/S, medium confidence.** A follows the skill's “mature research programme” criterion: CFS is technically deep, long-running, connected to GR, QFT and the Standard Model, and still actively producing new mathematics and physics. It is not S scientific because the microscopic regularization is not uniquely fixed, important observed parameters remain empirical at the effective level, and there is no distinctive empirical confirmation comparable to the programme's theoretical breadth. Interest S is separate: the causal-action ontology, emergent spacetime, broad force-plus-gravity recovery and systematic correction programme are exceptionally generative.

**Clash — Asymptotic Safety:** battleground is *ultraviolet completion and parameter origin*. CFS provides a broader emergent-spacetime architecture and an explicit Standard-Model-plus-gravity continuum programme, but some effective parameters are inherited from unknown regularization data. Asymptotic Safety stays closer to conventional QFT/RG machinery and has a sharp fixed-point criterion, but must establish the fixed point and full matter-sector predictivity. No movement of Asymptotic Safety from A/A follows.

**Clash — Observer Patch Holography:** battleground is *mature continuum attachment versus finite reconstruction auditability*. CFS has vastly deeper literature and stronger connection to conventional field equations. OPH has a more compact finite theorem/certificate ledger and a more explicit named failure surface. CFS would gain by producing regulator-independent discriminating predictions; OPH would gain by closing its five physical-realization obligations in one common refinement. OPH remains B/S.

**History:** 2026-09-22 enters A/S, medium confidence as a backlog correction prompted by current discussion and substantial 2026 work. This is not a promotion caused merely by a conference appearance: the A placement is based on the programme's accumulated technical maturity and recovery of known physics; the current discussion is only what surfaced the omission.
