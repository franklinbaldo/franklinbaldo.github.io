## Computational Finitism — enters D / interest S, medium confidence

**Origin / provenance.** Néstor E. Ramos. The physical programme located in this audit has a public 2026 sequence beginning with *The Physical Singularity: Why Infinities in Physics Signal Model Boundaries, Not Reality* (March 2026, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.19357723`), followed by *The Infinity Crisis* (April 2026, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.19373909`), finite-horizon and coarse-graining papers in June, and the decision-bearing *Emergent Quantum Mechanics from a Finite Alphabet: Computational Verification of Mass, Forces, and Gauge Symmetries in a Finite Substrate* (July 2026, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21325151`; author full-text upload dated 2026-07-12). The accessible papers are preprint/self-published programme material rather than an independently established literature.

**Proposal.** Computational Finitism replaces continuum ontology with a finite-state computational substrate. The corpus uses finite-alphabet/modular arithmetic, causal graphs, Golden-Fano-style automata, discrete coarse-graining, topological defects and information geometry to claim emergent spacetime, matter, forces, gauge symmetry, gravity and physical constants. Companion papers extend the same principle to black-hole singularities, renormalization, string/AdS infinities, cosmology and mathematical existence questions.

**Scope.** The programme explicitly aims at both gravity/spacetime and particle/gauge physics, so it passes the Arena's scope gate as a ToE contender rather than a quantum-gravity-only adjacent entry.

**Evidence for / strengths.** The programme has a much larger calculational surface than a manifesto. It supplies runnable finite models, intermediate simulations, explicit coarse-graining constructions, causal-graph experiments and quantitative targets. The July paper reports wave propagation, topological-defect mobility, particle-hierarchy proxies, arithmetic interaction models, gauge-channel constructions and multi-base tests. It also preserves failed intermediate iterations, which is epistemically useful because the reader can see where a proposed mechanism did not work before a later rule was introduced. More broadly, the theory makes many claims that could in principle fail rather than protecting itself behind an unfalsifiable ontology.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The audit located no independent reproduction of the central Computational Finitism calculations. The mapping from finite arithmetic to actual Lorentz/QFT/Standard-Model structure is not controlled at the precision expected of established physics. In the decision-bearing paper, a light-fermion construction moves far below its own target interval; a later collective tensor-transfer rule is then introduced and interpreted as mandatory hardware physics. That may be a legitimate model revision, but without a frozen derivation it does not establish that the original substrate predicted the successful mechanism. Likewise, a massless propagation speed around `0.9540 c` is treated as emergence of Maxwell-like physics even though a several-percent discrepancy is enormous for relativistic precision physics.

A second load-bearing issue is the electromagnetic coupling. Parts of the programme derive an approximately `1/137` active channel from finite geometry. The low-energy inverse fine-structure constant is known far more precisely, approximately `137.035999...`, and the effective QED coupling runs with momentum transfer. A unification claim therefore has to recover the precision endpoint and the running, not simply land near the integer 137. The programme contains a companion proposal for discrete running, but this remains internal to the same research corpus rather than an externally reproduced derivation.

**Placement.** **D scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** D recognizes a genuine, executable and unusually broad early hypothesis while emphasizing that its connection to precision known physics, its load-bearing repair mechanisms and its physical interpretation of numerical rules remain substantially under-validated. It is below Lucron's current C because the latter is narrower about its unresolved Lorentz/Yang-Mills bridge, whereas Computational Finitism currently advertises much stronger completion claims than its evidence supports. **S interest** reflects the enormous falsification surface, computational accessibility and the number of useful clashes it creates with discrete-pregeometry approaches. It is not a scientific-strength bonus.

**Clash — The Lucron Model.** Battleground: discrete pregeometry -> relativistic continuum + Standard Model + gravity. Computational Finitism has a wider runnable simulation corpus and more aggressive parameter-level claims, but it currently relies on stronger interpretive jumps from automaton behavior to established physics and lacks independent validation. Lucron also lacks complete Lorentz/Yang-Mills recovery, but Version 4 records those gaps and quantitative failures more explicitly. A decisive comparison would freeze both microscopic rule sets and ask independent groups to recover the same precision low-energy observables without post-hoc mechanism changes. No Lucron tier movement follows from this clash.

**Distinctive tests / promotion gate.** A move toward C would require an external end-to-end reimplementation from frozen rules that simultaneously recovers: (1) relativistic dispersion to realistic precision, not only order unity; (2) a realistic chiral gauge/matter sector rather than qualitative channel analogies; (3) the precision and scale dependence of the electromagnetic coupling; and (4) a controlled gravitational limit. A genuinely prospective prediction fixed before new data would add much more weight than additional within-programme numerical correspondences. Conversely, failure of a frozen central claim under faithful reproduction would justify reconsideration downward.

**Movement history.** The broad 2026-09-23 scan placed Computational Finitism on the watchlist because snippets were insufficient for a tier. This dedicated primary-source pass moves it from watchlist to the canonical OKF corpus at D/S, medium confidence. That is a cataloguing decision, not a promotion from a previous scientific tier.
