## Entered: PIU-Ψ / Universal Integrity Principle — C scientific / S interest, low confidence

**Origin / provenance.** PIU-Ψ is the independent research programme of Manuel Alberto Celedón Mejía. The decision-bearing public source is **PIU-Ψ V5.5**, published on **2026-09-05** and archived on Zenodo as DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22313421`, CC BY 4.0: <https://piuniversal.com/> and <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22313421>. The project describes V5.5 as a new version of an earlier V5.1 record, but this audit did not establish the exact original publication date of V5.1; that date is therefore left unresolved rather than inferred. A new general-audience introduction is dated 2026-09-21, so the programme is also actively being revised and discussed this month.

**Proposal.** PIU-Ψ postulates one physical elastic medium, the **Pleno**, represented by a complex scalar field `Psi = S exp(i theta)`. A single fundamental Lagrangian F1 combines a kinetic term, a nonlinear/topological potential, a Planck-suppressed higher-derivative bending term and an affine `1/S^2` wall. The programme claims different regimes of this same substrate yield an effective Einstein-gravity sector, nonrelativistic Schrodinger/Madelung-Bohm dynamics, U(1) and non-Abelian gauge structure, Skyrmion baryonic matter, Q-ball dark matter, a condensate-like dark-energy ground state and nonsingular cosmological dynamics. It also presents identities relating the medium scales to `G` and `hbar` and a substantial catalogue of numerical and cosmological claims.

**Evidence for / strengths.** PIU is unusually inspectable for a new founder-led unification programme. The published V5.5 package contains a 581-page Spanish / 584-page English corpus, 307 labelled equations, a 141/142-page mathematical compendium, LaTeX sources and three executable verification notebooks. The programme publishes epistemic tags result-by-result and an explicit open-end ledger. Its own numerical machinery reports 551 numerical recalculations, 290 symbolic checks with many paired negative controls, and a cross-document audit of 4332 printed figures. These results establish internal reproducibility of the author's stated calculations, not independent confirmation, but they make errors and hidden changes considerably easier to audit than in a prose-only proposal.

The framework also takes falsifiability more seriously than many broad ToE submissions. Its gravity section states that the lattice route must reproduce `gamma_G = 1` and the same structure must deliver the Bekenstein-Hawking `1/4`; failure is declared a falsification point. In cosmology it commits to an exact late-time `w = -1` and says a persistent raw-observable `w(z) != -1` signal would contradict that interpretation. The dark-sector papers publish fixed numerical relations such as `Omega_DM/Omega_b = sqrt(3) pi` and a condensate fraction near the observed dark-energy fraction. These are useful attack surfaces even though most current numerical comparisons are retrospective.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The main limitation is independent scrutiny. This scan located the author-maintained website, Zenodo corpus, thematic manuscripts and reproducibility package, but not an independent end-to-end reconstruction or peer-reviewed validation of the central F1 -> gravity + quantum mechanics + realistic Standard Model chain. The corpus itself says its final epistemic weight depends on independent scrutiny and peer review. Internal notebooks can catch transcription/algebra/code inconsistencies, but they cannot by themselves establish that the physical assumptions, identifications or interpretation of observables are correct.

The gravity normalization also remains a real load-bearing exposure. PIU explicitly records that the Sakharov induced-gravity `a1` calculation gives `gamma_G = N/(12 pi) = 0.1326` and is scheme-dependent; that route supplies sign/scale/TT structure but does **not** fix the required coefficient. The programme therefore moves the decisive normalization into a separate lattice calculation that must give `gamma_G = 1`. That is a legitimate falsifiable strategy, but until the lattice result is completed and independently reconstructed, the advertised recovery of the Einstein coefficient is not at mature-programme level.

The Standard-Model bridge is likewise incomplete. The corpus develops phase-circulation/gauge constructions associated with U(1), SU(2) and SU(3), and uses established topological mechanisms for fermionic/baryonic matter. But it explicitly says the seventeen-decade bridge to the renormalized couplings at `M_Z` is not derived. This audit also did not locate a comparably complete F1-only derivation of the observed chiral representation content, anomaly cancellation, three generations, CKM/PMNS flavour structure and electroweak/Higgs phenomenology. The distinction matters: embedding or reconstructing familiar gauge structures is not yet the same as deriving the low-energy Standard Model.

There is also unresolved explanatory-compression debt around the primary dimensional scales. Relations such as `G = c^2/(rho_P l_P^2)` and `hbar = rho_P c l_P^4` are mathematically definite once `rho_P` and `l_P` are specified, but an external audit must establish that those quantities are fixed independently rather than functioning as a reparameterisation of the constants being explained. The programme's own calibrated/derived status system is helpful here and should be preserved in any replication.

Finally, the reported anti-coincidence Bayes calculation is an internal heuristic, not a conventional marginal-likelihood comparison between fully specified statistical models. PIU itself notes that common verbal Bayes-factor scales were designed for a different setting. The Arena therefore does not count the reported factor as independent confirmation. Current DESI evidence also does not settle PIU's exact-`w=-1` prediction: DESI's July 30, 2026 DR2 Lyman-alpha full-shape analysis shifted toward LambdaCDM and agrees with the standard model in that probe, while the collaboration says the broader evolving-dark-energy story remains open and full five-year dark-energy results are expected in 2027. That is compatibility, not a PIU-specific success.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, low confidence.** C reflects a concrete, extensive mathematical proposal with unusually good self-audit infrastructure and meaningful falsifiers, but weak independent uptake, an unresolved gravity-normalisation gate, incomplete low-energy Standard-Model recovery and substantial retrospective/model-identification debt. D would understate how much formal and executable structure is already present; B would overstate the external validation and completeness of the recovery chain. Confidence is low because the corpus is very large, fast-moving and almost entirely founder-authored; this pass deliberately audits several load-bearing claims rather than pretending to have independently rederived hundreds of pages.

S interest is deliberately independent of scientific maturity. PIU is unusually generative for adversarial work: one elastic substrate attempts to touch gravity, quantum foundations, gauge structure, matter and cosmology; the project exposes source, scripts, status labels, withdrawals, open ends and named falsifiers; and its strongest claims can be split into small reconstruction tasks. That makes it an excellent Arena object even if future independent checks push its scientific tier down rather than up.

**Clash — Refined Space-Time Membrane Model (RSTM), currently C/S.** Battleground: elastic/continuum substrate -> quantum behaviour + Einstein gravity + realistic Standard Model + discriminating tests. PIU's concrete advantage is the breadth and explicitness of its derivation/status ledger, public verification package and frozen failure conditions; its liability is that the `gamma_G` coefficient is still exposed, the `M_Z` coupling bridge is openly underived and the corpus has essentially no independent reconstruction. RSTM's advantage is a more compact one-PDE/RG/FRG/EFT scaffold and a direct membrane/metamaterial laboratory route; its liability is the re-entry of standard spin-2 bootstrap, gauge/quantisation structure and phenomenological flavour scans. Neither currently has independent end-to-end recovery of GR plus a realistic chiral Standard Model. The clash therefore moves **neither** tier.

**Movement history.** First canonical Arena placement on **2026-09-23** at C/S, low confidence. This is an entry, not a promotion. Movement toward B would require at minimum: (1) independent reconstruction of a load-bearing F1 chain, especially the gravity-normalisation or low-energy gauge/matter route; (2) a complete anomaly-safe chiral spectrum and coupling/flavour calculation from frozen assumptions rather than a correspondence layer; and (3) a successful prospective discriminator. A failed frozen `gamma_G` lattice calculation, persistent model-independent evidence incompatible with exact `w = -1`, or a demonstrated circularity/inconsistency in a central F1 derivation would push the scientific tier downward.
