## Entered: Selective Transient Field (STF) — C scientific / S interest, medium confidence

**Origin / date / source.** The project history dates Z. Paz's Selective Transient Field programme to 2025, but this audit does not reconstruct a trustworthy exact first-publication day from an independent archive. The decision-bearing source is the current Theory Edition, *The Selective Transient Field from First Principles — The Two-Clock Theory*, V9.7.2, dated **2026-09-05**: <https://existshappens.com/papers/first-principles/>. The current project overview is <https://existshappens.com/>. The particle-unification branch is *Standard Model Unification — Particle Masses and CP Violation from 10D Compactification*, V3.5: <https://existshappens.com/papers/standard-model-unification/>.

**Proposal.** STF introduces a scalar response field whose activation depends on changing curvature rather than static curvature alone. The present formulation uses two clocks — a universal ordering variable and an internal phase — together with a curvature-state quantity, a causal finite-memory/high-pass response and a regulated readout. The wider programme then attempts to connect that same substrate to cosmology and, conditionally, to a 10D compactification construction from which particle masses, couplings and flavour quantities are claimed. The September 2026 revision explicitly carries the scalar mass as one measurable free scale rather than claiming it as derived.

**Evidence for / strengths.** This is not merely a verbal ontology: the current technical corpus gives explicit state variables, kernels, response laws and parameter formulae that can be inspected. It also exposes many places where the proposal can fail — transient-gravity timing, cosmological evolution, dark-sector abundance and particle/flavour relations — and the project has a visible history of correcting itself. In particular, later versions withdraw the earlier physical kinetic-energy-transfer interpretation of the flyby mechanism, close the all-dark-matter reading of the quoted ultralight scalar after Lyman-alpha constraints, and retract a prior DHOST Class-Ia classification while retaining a narrower gravitational-wave-speed result by direct calculation. That willingness to preserve negative results is a positive epistemic feature even though the retractions themselves weaken earlier evidence claims.

**Evidence against / unresolved.** The end-to-end unification chain still lacks independent reconstruction and, in this scan, no peer-reviewed validation of the central STF framework was located. Several load-bearing structures are inputs or conditional bridges rather than deductions from a smaller principle: GR/Peters dynamics are used as an input, the particle paper starts with substantial Standard-Model fermionic structure, the scalar mass is one free scale, the 10D matching is conditional, and SI normalization/stability remain explicitly open. Independent Lyman-alpha analyses strongly constrain canonical ultralight-field dark matter in the mass range relevant to the project; STF's own current dark-matter paper therefore limits the scalar to a subdominant fraction rather than a full dark-matter solution. A further audit concern is version drift: some older project surfaces still contain superseded 'confirmed' or 'zero-free-parameter' language that the September Theory Edition has narrowed.

**Placement.** **C scientific / S interest, medium confidence.** C matches a structured and unusually large speculative corpus with real mathematics and testable interfaces, but weak independent scrutiny, conditional derivation steps and no externally reproduced gravity-plus-Standard-Model chain. It is above D because the programme has explicit equations, versioned calculations and concrete falsification surfaces; it remains below B until at least one central derivation is independently reproduced and the open normalization/stability/compactification bridges are closed. S interest is a separate judgement: the combination of transient-curvature response, a two-clock ontology, cosmology, particle-parameter claims and a public failure ledger is exceptionally generative for comparison and experimental design even without strong evidential support.

**Clash — USMEG-EFT.** Battleground: **how much gravity-plus-Standard-Model structure is genuinely derived rather than imported, and how sharply the framework exposes failure.** STF's advantage is its broader prospective prediction register and unusually visible correction/falsification history; its liability is that many bridges remain self-authored, conditional or imported from established physics, with little independent reconstruction. USMEG-EFT has the concrete advantage of peer-reviewed path-integral/RG/BRST machinery and an explicit statement that its effective geometric description breaks down near the high-energy gravitational scale; its liability is precisely that it is not yet an ultraviolet completion and it keeps the Standard Model as an input. A peer-reviewed independent STF reconstruction, or conversely a demonstrated contradiction in one of its frozen prospective predictions, would move this comparison materially. The clash does **not** warrant moving USMEG-EFT from C/A.

**Movement history.** This is STF's first canonical Arena placement: **unranked/watchlist → C/S** on 2026-09-23. That is an inclusion decision, not a scientific promotion from a previous letter tier. The placement deliberately uses the September 2026 corrected Theory Edition rather than rewarding older stronger claims that the programme itself has since withdrawn.

## Fresh screen: *Spacetime Grand Unified Theory*

Gonçalo M. Quinta's *Spacetime Grand Unified Theory* was first submitted **2025-07-14** and revised to v4 on **2026-09-03**: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11146>. It derives a rich particle/gauge construction from an eight-dimensional free Dirac Lagrangian and `Cl(8,0)`, with Pati–Salam-like structure, triality, four families, a stable-neutrino dark-matter candidate and mass/mixing relations. It is technically relevant to unification, but the inspected work does not supply a quantum-dynamical gravity sector or recover Einstein dynamics. It is therefore **screened out of the canonical ToE ranking by scope**, not declared scientifically false; it remains a useful adjacent matter/spacetime-unification comparison.
