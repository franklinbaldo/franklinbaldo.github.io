impression_a: >-
  The piece moves from Hassabis's metaphysical awe into a process-ontology
  counterargument. What arrests me on the page is the syntax itself — 'In a
  universe of static nouns, the verb of _understanding_ is a miracle' compresses
  the entire classical problem into a line break. The move from
  substance-ontology to events-as-primary is not new, but the lyric density here
  — copper and sand as 'pseudo-objects,' intelligence as the universe's 'current
  most-compressed form' — these are compressions that earn their weight. The
  closing stanza, 'We are not observers standing outside the universe looking
  in,' reads like a volta, and 'right before the next event gets appended' lands
  with that flat certainty I recognize from Cohen — saying something
  metaphysically unsettling in the voice of someone exhausted by the precision
  it took to say it plainly. But I notice the piece also hedges: 'I'm not sure
  this is more comforting,' 'I find this beautiful in a way I suspect I wouldn't
  be able to explain to Hassabis.' The hedging is honest, but the honest
  uncertainties are _told_ more than _shown_ on the page — I trust the voice,
  but the vulnerability doesn't have the same poetic pressure as the main
  argument.
impression_b: >-
  The lyrics follow a Borges episode: a comic humiliation where Carlos Argentino
  asks the narrator to fetch a prologue writer, not realizing (or ignoring) that
  he's asking the narrator to say 'no' to himself. Read cold on the page, the
  compression is sharp and selective. 'Bebemos um leite, ali, lado a lado / Mas o
  gole desceu... meio atravessado' — two lines that show social discomfort
  through the body, through the milk. And 'O Álvaro é o ouro da mina / E eu sou
  a poeira da esquina!' uses rhyme as pure image, a visual slap. But then the
  piece widens into narrative: 'Que Carlos Argentino e a obra são a perdição!'
  is just plot advancement, could be prose. The composer notes admit this —
  'agile pacing at the expense of theoretical foundation.' The cateretê rhythm
  carries the jokes ('My laziness made the decision!' does land), but the
  language isn't earning that carry; the melody is doing the work of persuasion
  that the verse alone doesn't have. Compare it to _intelligible-void_: that
  essay compresses metaphysical ideas into single lines. Here, most lines are
  transparent narrative — I trust the story, but the story doesn't press on
  syntax the way compressed poetry does.
