  literatura me justifica.' However, as a musical post, the lyric relies
  entirely on borrowed genius. The author did not write this text; he merely
  arranged it for spoken word over classical guitar. While the composer notes
  are thoughtful in connecting the essay to his own work on process ontology,
  evaluating the *lyric* means evaluating Borges, not the composer. It is a
  masterpiece of prose poetry, but it feels like a curation exercise rather than
  an original act of lyrical compression for this project.
