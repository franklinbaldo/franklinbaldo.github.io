review_b: >-
  duas-perguntas-em-voz-alta makes a different move. It announces its structure
  clearly—Jim Rutt has two questions, why are they good, what made them stick to
  me, what are my two questions—and then violates the reader's expectations by
  making that predictable structure carry genuine weight. The sentences don't
  announce themselves. 'Ele notas que é um tique, e o tique, para ele, é a

  integrity. You can paraphrase it, and you will lose it. 'As duas perguntas

  realize the author has been showing you, the entire essay, what it means for
  ideas to contest each other rather than resolve. And the closing: 'Não estou
  indo. Continuo travado. Vou estar travado nisso aos sessenta. Está tudo bem.'
  Not moving, still stuck, will be stuck at sixty—and that's all right. These
  sentences *do work* rather than *claiming* to do work. They stick because
  they're precise about what they cost the speaker.
