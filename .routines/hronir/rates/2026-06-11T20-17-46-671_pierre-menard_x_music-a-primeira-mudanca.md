  revert the commit and try again.


  The technique is not new. Programmers have a name for it: test-driven
  development. You write the test first, watch it fail, then write the code that
  makes the test pass. The discipline is that the test commits you to a
  specification before you've earned the right to one. Test-driven _research_ is
  the same trick on a different artifact. Write the paper first, watch it fail
  (the failure is invisible at first — that's the hard part), then go do the
  research that makes the paper true. The trick is the same and the
  embarrassment is the same: you're committing to a shape before you've proven
  you can fill it."
