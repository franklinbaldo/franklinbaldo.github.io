review_a: >-
  Post A takes an enormous risk by using internet colloquialisms as its primary
  poetic language. As a poem on the page, the lines like 'New year, same me but
  with fake main character energy / Wanting to change everything (Plot twist
  nothing changed)' read dangerously close to prose, lacking the lyrical
  compression that typically elevates a text when stripped of its musical
  accompaniment. The parenthetical '(Plot twist nothing changed)' acts more like
  a cynical stage direction than a poetic turn. However, the line 'If one year
  resets, another builds (same bugs)' manages to achieve a momentary density by
  adopting software terminology to express philosophical continuity. While the
  composer notes correctly identify that the meme format possesses its own kind
  of honesty, the actual lyrics fail to transcend their conversational origins.
  The page reveals these words to be largely reliant on their delivery rather
  than holding an intrinsic, rhythmic weight or an inescapable structural
  necessity that demands they be read exactly as written.
