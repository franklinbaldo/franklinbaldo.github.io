  it demands the page. The compression is severe and effective. 'Vidro não
  sonha: executa. / Água não pensa: copia. / Ébano liso: reimprime.' This is
  language doing heavy lifting—it is not merely singing, it is defining a
  metaphysical terror through the mechanism of three materials. The stanza
  breaks exactly where it must to pivot from description to threat: 'o
  multiplicador sem ruído.' The line 'A escrita anda ao contrário, / ando rabino
  lendo de trás' is the kind of image that prose cannot achieve with this
  economy. It forces the reader to stop and reread. The composer's notes deepen
  the reading by explaining the intellectual constraint (Borges’s terror of

  density through structural rigor, not melodic disguise.
