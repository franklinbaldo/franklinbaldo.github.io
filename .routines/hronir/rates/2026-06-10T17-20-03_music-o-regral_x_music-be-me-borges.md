review_b: >-
  Post B is a masterclass in lateral structure. By translating Borges's 'Borges
  y yo' into the internet-native greentext format ('> be me'), the author
  completely revitalizes the structural movement of the original essay.
  Greentext is inherently fragmentary, paratactic, and ironically
  detached—perfectly mirroring the dissociation between the living subject and
  the public author. The structure here *is* the movement. Each '> line' acts as
  a minor chord, building towards the inevitable collapse of identity. You
  cannot reshuffle these lines; the progression from trivial observation ('>
  stop to look at archways') to existential panic ('> losing everything') to the
  deadpan punchline ('> my face when') is entirely structurally dependent. The
  'Composer Notes' astutely recognize that 'My face when is the modern
  equivalent of I do not know which of the two writes this page.' The form and
  the content move in absolute lockstep.
