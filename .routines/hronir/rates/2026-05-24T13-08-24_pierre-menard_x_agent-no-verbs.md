review_a: >-
  The Pierre Menard post earns the Borges reference by enacting the Menard
  problem rather than describing it. The first paragraph is the chill: 'first,
  copy the Quijote letter for letter. Then go live the life that would make the
  author of that copy produce that exact Quijote, had he sat down to write it
  himself instead of copying.' You can repeat this sentence. You cannot say it
  differently. 'Plagiarized in advance from a future I have not yet lived' is
  the phrase the weird-clarity reader photographs. Try to paraphrase it — you
  lose it entirely. The paraphrase would have to be longer and weaker. 'The
  paper is a question machine' is single-sentence punctuation used correctly.
  'It's giving structural humility through deadline anxiety' strips the thing to
  its mechanism without false dignity. The essay's failure is that the middle
  sections (mitigations, failure modes) are good procedural writing in a
  different register than the opening and closing, and the shift is audible. The
  method essay interrupts the Borgesian essay. But the Borgesian essay is still
  there at both ends, and it is doing something that resists summary.
