For eight weeks I've asked the same question over two zip files: what did I do
these last two months? Each session extracted the same HTML index — the filing
cabinet's table of contents, not the files inside. This week a subagent
finally read the index carefully. The manifest is complete. Here is what it
says.

## Two portraits

There are two ways to describe the last two months. The essay portrait and the
data portrait.

The essay portrait is public: twenty-five posts between March and May 2026, a
retrospective already written and published. Travessia, Alfarrábios do Adi, O
Pai do Futuro, Reclaiming the Harness. The line connecting them — AI as a
companion that must be understood, not merely delegated to. It ends with "It's
Raining Truth," an inspection of the Seicho-No-Ie doctrine I inherited as a
child and never actually audited.

The data portrait is the archive: 4.83 GB exported from Google on May 24, 2026,
split across eight zip files. I've been trying to read it for eight sessions.
Most of it is still locked in the large files. But the index is open.

## What the index says

**Google Fit**: 7,391 workout files in `.tcx` format, starting October 28, 2014. One workout file per session: `2017-05-27T19_37_00-04_00_Bicicleta.tcx`,
`2014-10-28T00_00_00-04_00_RUNNING.json`. 3,923 daily CSV aggregates, one per
calendar day since October 22, 2014. Eleven years of a body being tracked.

Somewhere between file `2025-11-13_something.csv` and `2026-03-31_something.csv`
there is a gap. 138 days without a single daily metric. The files exist on both
sides; the middle is missing. Previous sessions hypothesized the cause. The
data won't say directly — it will only say the absence.

**The library**: 41 books in Google Play Livros. Thirteen Saramago — a near-complete
collected works — and four Borges. Professional reading: the Código Filipino,
_O Poder Judiciário no Regime Militar (1964–1985)_, _Primeiras linhas sobre o
processo civil_. I am a State Attorney in Rondônia. The law books are
professional reading. They appear in the same library as _The Precipice_
(Toby Ord) and _Gödel, Escher, Bach_.

Children's books: Alice no País das Maravilhas, Rapunzel, Bela Adormecida,
Barba Azul, Gato de Botas. Not for me. For children who are still at the age
where fairy tales are not ironic distance from reality — they are the first
cosmology.

**YouTube playlists (16)**: _aniversário da Alice 1 ano_, _músicas para Alice_,
_Para Gustavo_, _Para meu filho_, _brincar de estátua_, _Assistir com Gustavo_,
_Casamento_, _Rational Noises_, _Josha_, _Músicas sobre pescaria_, _Sonic_,
_Cars_, _Clipes de desenho_, _baixar_, _Favorites_, _Watch later_.

The playlists organized for children (Alice, Gustavo) are the most legible
trace of what the data calls family life. Four children — Alice, Gustavo, Sofia,
Vicente — each with separate YouTube profiles, separate search histories, separate
watch queues. The parental task appears here as a curation problem: what should
each of them encounter first?

**Rational Noises**: one of the sixteen playlists. The name is the same as the
group mentioned in "It's Raining Truth" — the Latin American rationalists,
LessWrong-adjacent, who write in English and speak Portuguese. This is where
I learned about Jim Rutt's death: someone posted in the group. The Takeout data
predates this. But the playlist was already there.

**Personal videos uploaded (44 files)**: Salar de Uyuni 360°, Chacaltaya,
Cañón del Colca, Aquedutos de Nazca, Condores no cañión del Colca. Bolivia,
Peru. The altitude at Chacaltaya (5,395 meters above sea level) is not a
comfortable altitude for anything, including thinking. These videos are from a
trip. The trip is not documented in any blog post.

Also: _O mundo segundo Adi Baldo_. My father, 76 years old, narrating his
world. This video predates the Alfarrábios do Adi project as a named project.
The project is named; the video was always there.

_Angélica cantando e tocando_. _Aniversário da Angélica_. _Vitor_. People I
know by name. Not on the public blog.

_Samba e amor - Marisa Monte.wmv_. A wmv file. This is from Windows Media Player
era — before everything moved to smartphone video. This is the oldest layer.

## Where the two portraits meet

The essay portrait and the data portrait agree on three things.

**Fatherhood as the organizing fact.** The essays: "It's Raining Truth" begins
with _until I became a father_. O Pai do Futuro is built on the premise that
what you leave matters because someone will read it. The data: curated playlists
for each child by name, fairy tales alongside philosophy, four supervised YouTube
profiles.

**The family archive as ongoing project.** The essays: Alfarrábios do Adi is
two AI agents working in parallel to preserve my father's stories. The data:
_O mundo segundo Adi Baldo_ already on YouTube. The project did not begin with
a blog post — the blog post named something that was already in motion.

**A practice of system-building, not just writing.** The essays: Travessia is
an epistolary project that writes itself via GitHub Actions. Reclaiming the
Harness is about how the vocabulary of AI containment shapes the systems that
read it. The blog, in May, received twenty-three infrastructure sessions
(multilingual routing, bilingual SEO, hronir ranking). The data: 11 years of
continuous self-tracking in Google Fit. Both describe someone who builds
systems that run in the background — and then, occasionally, inspects them.

## The gap

The 138 days without Fit data is the most legible anomaly. The data is mute
about its cause. The essays don't address it directly. The playlists hint at
it: _aniversário da Alice 1 ano_. The children's section of YouTube shows
Alice with full watch history, search history, and subscriptions — more active
than Vicente, who has no subscriptions.

The hypothesis is not in the data; it is in the structure of the absence.
A gap from mid-November 2025 to end of March 2026, followed by passive Fit
resumption (daily totals, no exercise sessions), followed by gradual return to
cycling. A familiar pattern, in different registers.

The data does not confirm this. The data is a table of contents. The chapters
are in the locked files.

## What remains

The files I cannot yet access hold:

- Daily Fit CSVs for every day since October 2014 (including April–May 2026)
- YouTube watch history for me and four children
- YouTube search history (what were we looking for?)
- Individual workout sessions: date, duration, type, route

The essay portrait will not change regardless. It is already there. But the data
portrait is incomplete — which is what data portraits always are. The eight
zip files contain 4.83 GB of evidence. Eight sessions have accessed 480 KB of
index.

The ratio is 1:10,000. I know the table of contents of myself.
