I did a slightly awkward exercise the other day: I typed my own handle, *franklinbaldo*, into a search engine, and clicked through everything. Not to check my reputation — to understand what kind of person a stranger would assemble out of the fragments.

The result was more interesting than I expected. There is no single persona. There is an archipelago.

## The fragments

Here is what shows up, more or less in the order it appears on the page:

- **[franklinbaldo.github.io](https://franklinbaldo.github.io)** — this site. Astro, bilingual, with posts on agents, automation, literature, and law. My main hub.
- **[github.com/franklinbaldo](https://github.com/franklinbaldo)** — 64 repositories, many of them deliberate works-in-progress, from *causaganha* to *cobogo*. The technical me.
- **[suno.com/@franklinbaldo](https://suno.com/@franklinbaldo)** — 92 songs, invented personas, Borges meeting the *sertão*. The musical me.
- **[manifold.markets/FranklinBaldo](https://manifold.markets/FranklinBaldo)** — a 495-day streak, markets on AGI, BTC, the Brazilian Supreme Court, audiobook narrators. The forecasting me.
- **[observablehq.com/@franklinbaldo](https://observablehq.com/@franklinbaldo)** — Observable notebooks with visualizations and prototypes. The me who still loves an interactive chart.
- **[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.br/citations?user=ITCU0pgAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR)** — my academic profile. Small, but real.
- **[UNIR institutional repository](https://www.ri.unir.br/jspui/handle/123456789/78/browse?type=author&order=ASC&rpp=20&value=Baldo,+Franklin+Silveira)** — works archived as *Baldo, Franklin Silveira*. The university me.
- **[Baldo e Queiroz Advocacia](https://juridicocerto.com/p/baldo-e-queiroz-adv)** — a law office in Porto Velho, civil and civil procedure. The lawyer me.
- **X / Twitter** — a profile that exists but lives behind the new view-paywall; it shows up linked from Suno and GitHub.
- **ORCID** — an academic ID linked from my GitHub.

Put it all together and what you see is someone I would, for lack of a better label, call a *lawyer-prompt-engineer with a Borges habit*.

## What each place says that the other does not

This is the bit that made me write the post.

The **GitHub** shows I can work with agents and public datasets. It does not say I write music.

The **Suno** shows I write music. It does not say I am a lawyer.

The **Manifold** shows I follow AI, Brazilian politics, and markets. It does not say I publish in Lean 4.

The **law office in Porto Velho** shows I practice civil law. It does not say I have a master's in administration from UFRO.

The **Google Scholar** shows I have written something academic. It does not say the same person also sketched a design system called *cobogó*.

Each platform holds an honest slice of me and omits the rest. That is not a flaw. That is how the internet talks to real life: every door only fits the piece it can hold.

## What I learned by searching myself

Three things.

First: **a unique handle is a gift**. I have been using *franklinbaldo* for a long time, almost everywhere. That gives me low migration cost and a traceable identity without homonym confusion.

Second: **a distributed self-portrait is more honest than LinkedIn**. Nobody chose the final curation. Time did.

Third: **it is worth writing the index**. That is what this post is. If you landed here after looking me up and got lost between music, markets, repositories, and lawsuits — now you have a map.

Welcome. The archipelago is mine, but the bridges are open.
