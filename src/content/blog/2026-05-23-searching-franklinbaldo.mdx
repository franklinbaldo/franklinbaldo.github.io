I did a slightly awkward exercise the other day: I typed my own handle, _franklinbaldo_, into a search engine and clicked through everything. Not to check my reputation — to understand what kind of person a stranger would assemble out of the fragments.

There is no single persona. There is an archipelago.

Roughly in click order, top of the page: the site itself, [franklinbaldo.github.io](https://franklinbaldo.github.io), Astro, bilingual, posts on agents and literature — the hub. Then [github.com/franklinbaldo](https://github.com/franklinbaldo), 64 repositories from _causaganha_ to _cobogo_ ([I wrote the full tour here](/blog/2026-05-22-github-a-tour-of-the-repos/)). [suno.com/@franklinbaldo](https://suno.com/@franklinbaldo) with 92 songs, Borges meeting the _sertão_ ([I opened the notebook here](/blog/2026-05-20-suno-borges-and-the-caipira/)). [manifold.markets/FranklinBaldo](https://manifold.markets/FranklinBaldo), a 495-day streak, the forecasting me ([495 days reading back the obsessions](/blog/2026-05-21-manifold-betting-on-ideas/)).

Further down: [observablehq.com/@franklinbaldo](https://observablehq.com/@franklinbaldo), notebooks with visualizations. A small but real [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.br/citations?user=ITCU0pgAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR). The [UNIR institutional repository](https://www.ri.unir.br/jspui/handle/123456789/78/browse?type=author&order=ASC&rpp=20&value=Baldo,+Franklin+Silveira) with works archived as _Baldo, Franklin Silveira_, the university me. And [Baldo e Queiroz Advocacia](https://juridicocerto.com/p/baldo-e-queiroz-adv), a law office in Porto Velho, civil and civil procedure, the lawyer me. X/Twitter shows up linked, but behind the new view paywall. ORCID, tied to the GitHub, closes the list.

Put it all together and what you see is someone I would call, for lack of a better label, a _lawyer-prompt-engineer with a Borges habit_.

The GitHub shows I work with agents and public datasets, but gives no hint that I write music. The Suno escapes the lawyer. The Porto Velho office doesn't know about Lean 4. And Google Scholar — well, Google Scholar seems to belong to a different person who happens to share my name.

More interesting is what _doesn't_ appear. There is no LinkedIn — one of the upsides of being a tenured public servant is not having to pretend you need one. There is no active Substack or Medium, although I drafted accounts on both and abandoned each within a week. There is no YouTube with my voice on it, and I don't miss it. There is no public Discord, but there are three private ones where I talk more than here. Every platform I'm not on is also self-portrait — possibly more accurate than the ones I stayed on.

A unique handle is a gift. I have used _franklinbaldo_ on almost everything for a long time, and that gives me low migration cost and a traceable identity without homonym confusion. It also gives me a high privacy cost: anyone assembles the whole of me with a click. The distributed self-portrait is more honest than LinkedIn precisely because nobody chose the final curation. Time did — and time edits worse than the author.

The archipelago is mine, but the bridges are open.
