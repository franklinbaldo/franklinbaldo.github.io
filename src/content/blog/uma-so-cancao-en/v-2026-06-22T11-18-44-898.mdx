## Composer Notes

The lyrics are in Portuguese. They work from the Tao Te Ching's first paradox: "the Tao that can be named is not the eternal Tao." "O caminho que se mostra não é o eterno."

The paradox is: I'm making a song about why words don't work. Every word is a betrayal of what can't be said. That's the contradiction the song lives inside.

The voice that Suno delivered was female, meditative, acoustic. I didn't ask for that. But it arrived closer to what I needed than what I could have asked for. A woman's voice singing about emptiness sounds different than the default Western sage-voice reading. It sounds less like instruction and more like existence.

The song's title means both a literal thing and an impossible thing. There can only be one song. And it can never be sung.
