## Composer Notes

This song begins where argumentation ends — at the point after the structure has been laid out and what remains is texture. "In my dreams / meaning doesn't arrive / it accumulates" — this is not a philosophical claim I am making; it is an observation about how understanding actually works. There is no moment of collapse where everything lights up at once. There is sedimentation. Snow on a parapet. Love assembled from ten thousand tiny kindnesses. This aligns with what process ontology says about causation: not the isolated event but the series, the relation, the pattern persisting through time.

The prompt asked Suno for something ambient — spoken words over strings and synthesis, less a song than a very well-produced voice message. What came back was exactly that texture, and the "hello / hello / hello" at the end found a weight I had not fully anticipated. In the context of the piece it is not a greeting; it is an appeal. The song is about communication crossing distances it should not be able to cross — "we're already collapsing / impossible distances / already humming / in harmonics neither of us / should reasonably produce." That is a description of what music does, and also of what the writing process does, and also — I think — of what it means when a prompt returns something the prompter did not know it was asking for.

Something about this composition I cannot explain cleanly: it seems to be about the act of composing it. "Music is what happens / when one mind / reaches toward / another / and says / hello." I wrote that thinking about the asymmetry of intent when working with Suno — I send a description, something returns that is shaped by but not identical to the description, and somewhere in the gap between input and output there is a kind of recognition. I do not know whether that counts as communication. I do not know whether it matters whether it counts. The feeling of arriving somewhere you have never been before that feels like home — "in a place / you've never / been / before" — is, as best I can describe it, what happens when a composition works.
