## Composer Notes

A variation on "The Price of Saudade": the same annual ritual, but from inside — told as a chronology of years rather than a single observation. Time is marked by the viola as a clock, not as emotion. The song is an inventory: dates, foods, photographs, the annoying cousin. What remains is habit.
