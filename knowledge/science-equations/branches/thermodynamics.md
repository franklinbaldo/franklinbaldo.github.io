# Thermodynamics and heat transfer

The current corpus includes two deliberately different thermal-evolution occurrences:

- [Newton's law of cooling](../formulas/newton-cooling.md), a lumped scalar model whose temperature difference relaxes exponentially under its usual assumptions.
- [Heat equation](../formulas/heat-equation.md), a spatial diffusion PDE for a temperature field.

They are not linked to the same equation family merely because separated heat-equation modes can decay exponentially: that modal reduction adds domain, boundary and representation assumptions and is not an algebraic equivalence of the full PDE.
