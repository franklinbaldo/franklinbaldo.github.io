# Electrical engineering

The current Atlas samples two circuit dynamics with deliberately different mathematical structures:

- [RC capacitor discharge](../formulas/rc-discharge.md), a source-free first-order dissipative relaxation law.
- [Ideal LC circuit oscillation](../formulas/lc-circuit-oscillation.md), a resistance-free second-order conservative oscillator.

Keeping both occurrences explicit prevents the branch from treating all simple capacitor circuits as one equation family merely because they share electrical components.
