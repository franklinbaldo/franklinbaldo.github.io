summary: >-
  A full retained MaleCNS model tests an internally causal question:
  whether silencing mAL inhibitory output unmasks spikes in eight literature-
  mapped P1-like candidate cells under fixed modeled sensory inputs. Paired
  intervention results support a small modeled disinhibition effect, not a
  demonstrated shift in real male courtship, preference, gender or behavior.
  The public follow-up preserves an unsuccessful original sign/readout pilot,
  an explicitly corrected protocol launch, raw outcomes and model assumptions.
strongest_evidence:
  - >-
    The published 36-run factorial design is 2 inhibitory reversal choices
    (-70/-80 mV) x 3 drive families (candidate male/female, direct vAB3) x
    3 seeds (11/12/13) x intact/blocked mAL; each pair holds graph, inputs,
    initial state and signs fixed, with raw input hashes.
  - >-
    For candidate-male drives, the prespecified eight-cell P1-like readout
    produced 0/0/0 spikes intact and 4/5/1 blocked at -70 mV; 0/0/0
    intact and 4/7/1 blocked at -80 mV. Candidate-female drives remained
    stronger in the same settings: 11/8/8 vs 18/18/20 at -70 mV.
  - >-
    Separate artificial tonic-drive brake assays, bounded-voltage checks,
    earlier failed pilot and side-by-side source/protocol hashes make the
    conditional mechanism and modeling revisions inspectable.
limitations:
  - >-
    This is a simulated neural response for assumed transmitter/receptor
    effects and hand-identified cells, not biological replication, animal
    choice or genetic fruitless manipulation. A published mapping discrepancy
    leaves identity of the eight-cell P1-like subset uncertain.
  - >-
    vAB3 and candidate-female outgoing excitatory sign overrides are modeled,
    not experimentally measured receptor signs. There are only three technical
    seeds, not six biological replicates; the three drive families are distinct.
  - >-
    The browser fly's target animation is illustrative choreography: the
    neural output does not autonomously control actual courtship behavior.
    Source experiments and huge input data were not rerun independently here.
controls:
  - >-
    Matched intact/mAL-blocked input schedules, two inhibitory reversals,
    intact/blocked vAB3 mediation probes, no-input and mAL-only checks, and
    public failed exploratory configurations preserved as negatives.
  - >-
    Missing cross-model and biological reproduction, matched structural
    rewiring and anatomically measured synaptic polarity under the same
    sensory conditions. The comparison identifies a conditional in-model
    intervention, not intrinsic specialness of the full MaleCNS topology.
