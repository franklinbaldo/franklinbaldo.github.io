summary: >-
  A full retained MaleCNS graph is connected to real public Coinbase market
  observations and a fixed neural proposal interface, with paper trading,
  resumable state and explicit safety/audit boundaries. The project
  demonstrates an unusually complete end-to-end experimental apparatus, but
  neither profitable learning nor a MaleCNS-specific trading advantage has been
  demonstrated.
strongest_evidence:
  - "The importer retains 166,700 MaleCNS nodes and 25,582,938 directed connections, with source hashes and derived graph arrays checked against committed locks."
  - "Public Coinbase observations are rendered into an RGB stimulus, propagated through the retained graph, and converted by a fixed descending-neuron readout into buy, sell or hold proposals; the external guard may veto but not invent a replacement trade."
  - "The paper-mode system preserves local neural state, audit logs, fees and resumable execution, and exposes a frozen-memory control mode for mechanism checks."
limitations:
  - "The chart-to-photoreceptor adapter and DNp20/DNpe017 trading readout are engineered interfaces rather than discovered biological market semantics."
  - "Profit/loss dopamine assignments and the KC-to-MBON memory rule are engineered; useful credit assignment and strategy improvement are explicitly unproven."
  - "No held-out chronological result establishes profit, and no matched no-connectome, rewired or random-recurrent controller has demonstrated a MaleCNS-specific advantage."
controls:
  - "A frozen-memory fixture mode is available to separate plasticity plumbing from weight-changing runs."
  - "The repository explicitly requires independent starts, frozen-weight and shuffled-reinforcement controls, equal budgets, retention/reset tests, fees/slippage and cash/simple-exposure baselines before any learning claim."
  - "A matched topology-null or no-connectome policy comparison remains missing."
