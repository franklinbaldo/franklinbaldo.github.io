# U(1,3) × U(1,3) Algebraic Grand Unification

The September 2026 synthesis is broader than ordinary grand unification: it tries to place electroweak, QCD, lepton-mass, baryon, quantum-gravity and string-theory structures inside one noncompact-unitary representation framework. Its current scientific rank stays low because the decisive bridge is still missing: the public material does not yet demonstrate that the same microscopic dynamics recovers the observed chiral Standard Model, its CP-violating flavour structure and Einstein gravity without re-importing those structures.

Against the **Geometric Action Principle (B/A)**, Liu's programme has the advantage of a much denser set of parameter-level and cross-domain numerical claims. Its liability is that many of those claims currently look like representation/arithmetic correspondences rather than an independently reconstructed physical dynamics, and the QCED precursor carries a direct CKM/CP problem. The Geometric Action Principle has the opposite profile: a more explicit single-action derivation with much less phenomenology and an unfinished realistic Standard-Model representation. No tier movement follows for the Geometric Action Principle.
