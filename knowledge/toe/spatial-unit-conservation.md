The decisive issue is now prior to the earlier LQG/Holomorphic-UFT maturity comparison. Version 12 defines the Shielding Principle as one of three fundamental axioms and says a fluctuation of rest-frame duration `τ` is seen by a moving observer with duration `τ/γ`, tending to zero as `v→c`. For two events at the same place in the rest frame, special relativity says the opposite: `Δt = γΔτ`, and the proper interval `Δτ` is the shortest. The cited shielding mechanism therefore does not follow from Lorentz kinematics.

That is why the scientific tier moves from C to F for the **current formulation**, not because discrete-spacetime or emergent-gravity programmes are generically ruled out. The interest tier stays S: the model still exposes many concrete cross-sector claims that are useful to test. A future version that corrects the relativity step and rederives the dependent results rather than merely changing one equation could move back out of F.
