The decisive comparison is with Loop Quantum Gravity and Holomorphic Unified Field Theory. The Qian framework advertises a wider set of concrete parameter and cosmology claims than either, but breadth is not maturity: LQG has far deeper independent mathematical development, and Holomorphic UFT has a multi-paper peer-reviewed programme. A B-tier case would require external reconstruction of the topological/constraint calculations and a fully traceable prospective empirical test rather than a fit whose underlying observational table cannot yet be independently matched to the cited data source.
