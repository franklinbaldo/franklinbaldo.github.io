open_problems: ["WDBI embeds the known Standard Model gauge structure and matter content; it does not derive SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), observed chiral representations, three generations, anomaly cancellation, Yukawa textures, CKM/PMNS or measured couplings from a smaller principle. The mechanism generates scales after symmetry breaking but does not eliminate the Standard Model's parameter problem.","The theory-specific phenomenology is not yet sharp enough to distinguish WDBI decisively from other Weyl-gauge, quadratic-gravity or scale-invariant frameworks. Compatibility of related Weyl gravity models with data is not unique confirmation of WDBI.","The 2026 dressed-metric formulation introduces a non-local map and ultraviolet non-commutativity in the symmetric phase; the physical interpretation, observables and uniqueness of this representation remain active theoretical questions rather than settled empirical facts."]

# Weyl–Dirac–Born–Infeld unification

WDBI is a technically mature action-level unification of Weyl-gauge gravity with the Standard Model. The Arena records it at B scientific / A interest: the peer-reviewed formal programme, low-energy Einstein/SM recovery and quantum-regularization claim make it a serious scientific contender, while the imported Standard-Model structure, unresolved parameter problem and lack of theory-specific empirical confirmation keep it below the Arena's stronger tiers.
