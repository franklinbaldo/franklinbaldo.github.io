# Selection–Stitch Model (SSM)

The Selection–Stitch Model is a discrete-vacuum programme in which a close-packed FCC/D4 substrate, error-correcting structure and incomplete crystallization are used to connect quantum mechanics, particle defects and gravity. The Arena places it at B scientific / S interest: the programme now has enough peer-reviewed technical surface and falsifiable sector claims to count as a serious contender, while the full microscopic-to-Standard-Model-and-nonlinear-GR bridge remains unclosed and largely unreproduced outside the originating corpus.
