# Dead Universe Theory (DUT)

DUT is tracked as an adjacent cosmology and modified-gravity programme rather than a complete gravity-plus-Standard-Model theory. Its most useful Arena feature is the combination of a compact microscopic coarse-graining story with unusually sharp observational failure conditions.
