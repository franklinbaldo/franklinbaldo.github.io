# GL(4,C) Unified Geometric Theory

Dimitris Mastoridis and Konstantinos Kalogirou propose a unification based on a four-complex-dimensional GL(4,C) geometry, broken to U(4), with the compact sector identified with particle/gauge physics and the Hermitian coset sector with gravity and the dark sector. The preprint advertises a parameter-light or parameter-free chain of predictions for particle masses, gauge coupling unification and cosmological observables from the symmetry-breaking geometry.
