# Quantum Collapse Geometry

**Arena placement: C/A, medium confidence.** QCG has enough explicit mathematical and operational structure to count as structured speculation, but its own current status statement correctly stops short of presenting it as a finished predictive theory.

Phase 1 organizes relational configuration space, admissibility, collapse-selection, invariant persistence, access regimes and projection. An operational paper introduces scale-local generators induced by a global collapse operator under coarse-graining. Phase 2 then asks whether standard physical structures can be reconstructed from those native primitives rather than assumed.

**Clash:** The Architecture of Reality D/A. QCG currently has the sharper operational generator/projection scaffold; Architecture has a broader differentiation/coarse-graining ontology. Neither yet supplies an end-to-end recovery of GR plus realistic chiral matter and decisive empirical tests. No tier movement.
