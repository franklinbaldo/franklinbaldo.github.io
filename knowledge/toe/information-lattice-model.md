# Information Lattice Model

ILM is best read today as an experimentally suggestive braneworld/emergent-gravity framework rather than a complete Theory of Everything. Its promotion gate is clear: derive the permeability dynamics and spectral functions from a full action, freeze quantitative predictions before data comparison, and show that the dark-sector and Standard-Model interfaces arise from the lattice rather than being assumed.
