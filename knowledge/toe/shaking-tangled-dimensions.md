Shaking Tangled Dimensions enters as an early but genuinely full-scope ToE proposal. Its D tier is not a dismissal: the framework is more developed than a one-paragraph hypothesis, but most of the burden that separates a geometric picture from a physical theory still lies ahead.

Against the Lucron Network, both use a discrete substrate and try to make matter and continuum physics emerge from local microscopic structure. Lucron currently sits at C/A because it has a more explicit relational dynamics/falsifier surface and a clearer list of continuum-recovery obligations. Tangled Dimensions has a broader intuitive particle catalogue, but the nine-dimensional Core assignments and the map to precise Standard-Model representations remain much less constrained. Lucron therefore stays C/A.

Against Unified Emergence, the ontology is almost the opposite: Tangled Dimensions starts with a richly structured discrete lattice, while Unified Emergence starts from a continuum preontology and successive coarse-graining. Both remain early because the operators that must produce the observed low-energy theory are not yet sufficiently fixed. Tangled Dimensions' greater microscopic specificity is useful for falsification, but it also creates more assumptions that must be independently justified. Unified Emergence remains D/A.
