# Pole Theory

Pole Theory asks whether a single discrete polar field on a Planck-scale lattice can underwrite quantum dynamics, geometry, matter and cosmology. The Arena records the current public programme as an early but auditable unification hypothesis, not as evidence that its microscopic ontology is physically realized.

The decisive next test is not another layer of reinterpretation. It is an end-to-end reconstruction in which the microscopic rules are frozen first and the observed chiral Standard Model, precision Lorentz behavior and Einstein limit are then recovered without feeding measured masses, mixings or continuum geometric objects back into the derivation.
