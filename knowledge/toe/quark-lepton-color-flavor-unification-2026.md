# Quark-lepton color-flavor unification

Antonio Delgado and Seth Koren's SU(12)×SU(2)L×U(1)R model was accepted by Physical Review D on 14 September 2026. It develops a novel quark/lepton flavor and color unification with instanton-generated Yukawas and proton stability, but it does not include gravity, so it is screened out of the ToE ranking despite being a relevant unification paper.
