# SO(2,16)/SO(1,17) Gauge Unification

This four-dimensional programme enlarges tangent-space gauge symmetry to place gravity and SO(10) internal interactions in one parent structure. The latest branch replaces conformal gravity as the load-bearing gravitational route with an explicit SO(1,5) -> Lorentz breaking that yields Einstein gravity plus a cosmological constant, then embeds the result in SO(1,17). The price is equally explicit: the current Weyl-Majorana construction produces two fermion families, not the observed three, so the stronger gravity bridge does not yet close realistic matter recovery.
