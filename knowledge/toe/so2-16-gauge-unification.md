# SO(2,16) Gauge Unification

A four-dimensional gauge-unification programme enlarges the tangent-space symmetry to SO(2,16), embedding conformal or fuzzy/noncommutative gravity together with SO(10) internal interactions. Spontaneous symmetry breaking is used to recover Einstein or Weyl gravity and descend toward Standard-Model gauge structure, with detailed attention to fermion embeddings and low-energy phenomenology.
