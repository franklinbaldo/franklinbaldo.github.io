# Shadow Theory

Shadow Theory treats observed physics as a structured readout of a richer source and develops exact mathematics for when information loss matters, what a minimal completion must restore, when projected dynamics close and what internal observers can identify. Its current canonical foundation has a concrete Randall–Sundrum gravitational witness and technically explicit measurement branches, but it still presents Standard-Model structure and several other decisive physical sectors as open research targets. The Arena therefore records it as structured speculation with very high comparative interest rather than as a completed unification.
