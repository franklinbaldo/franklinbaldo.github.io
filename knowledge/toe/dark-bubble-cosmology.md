# Dark Bubble Cosmology

Adjacent B/S, medium confidence.

## r321 — first inspectable M-theory dark-bubble construction (8 October 2026)

**Programme and provenance.** Dark Bubble Cosmology has been discussed in peer-reviewed braneworld work since 2018. The r136 evaluation had only an abstract for the recent M-theory extension. Menet, Tomasiello and Van Hemelryck posted the full manuscript *Non-supersymmetric M-theory vacua and dark bubbles* on **6 October 2026**, arXiv:2610.08939v1. This is a genuinely new, inspectable calculation, not independent observational evidence.

**Positive result.** An explicit eleven-dimensional supergravity construction compactifies on the six-dimensional flag manifold F6 = SU(3)/(U(1) x U(1)), with quantised fluxes. In portions of the solution family a wrapped M5-brane can nucleate, creating an expanding four-dimensional de Sitter bubble. The near-extremal ratio can be approached along a sequence of integer fluxes, giving a small induced dark-energy scale with **m_KK ~ Lambda_4^(1/4)** (in four-dimensional Planck units). For the measured cosmological constant, this suggests extra dimensions at **tens-of-microns** scales, a potentially discriminating short-range gravity regime. The exact extremal point is *not* itself allowed by flux quantisation; the mechanism requires selecting nearby quantised fluxes.

**Evidence limits / counterevidence.** The paper derives the requisite field equations and M5 nucleation conditions but does not construct a realistic four-dimensional matter spectrum, settle nonstandard mixed AdS boundary conditions, check full Kaluza-Klein perturbative stability, or establish cosmological observables. It finds instabilities in some alternative uplift models. The authors explicitly ask for matter interactions and KK constraints before claiming viability. An effective cosmological constant achieved by flux selection is not a parameter-free prediction; no empirical confirmation of this bubble mechanism is reported.

**Clash — Dark Dimension (B/S) and Phantom-Brane Metastable Dark Energy (B/A).** The battleground is a testable extra-dimensional explanation of late-time acceleration. The M-theory dark bubble has a more explicit microscopic compactification/nucleation channel and a possible micron-scale force signature, but much less direct cosmological fitting and incomplete matter/KK control. Dark Dimension also links vacuum energy to micron-scale physics, but does not share the same M5-nucleation dynamics; Phantom Brane has contemporary late-time cosmological fits yet no comparable M-theory UV construction. Independent tests of short-distance gravity, equivalence-principle constraints, linear growth/CMB, and a full KK-stable matter embedding would decide which advantages survive. **Tier B/S, confidence medium, unchanged.**
