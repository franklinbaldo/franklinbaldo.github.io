# Lorentzian Spacetime Quasicrystals

**Adjacent framework · scientific C / interest S · medium placement confidence.** Mathematical geometry is explicit; its physical interpretation remains conjectural.

## Scope and provenance

Latham Boyle and Sotirios Mygdalas first posted *Spacetime Quasicrystals* on **12 January 2026** (revised 12 February), arXiv:2601.07769. A symmetric cut-and-project procedure generalizes Euclidean quasicrystals to Minkowski point sets: (1+1)-dimensional constructions use the Lorentzian lattice I_(3,1), and (3+1)-dimensional examples use II_(9,1). Unlike familiar Euclidean patterns, the Lorentzian versions need not admit a corresponding symmetric tiling, and global scale invariance does not imply local scale invariance.

Mygdalas's **September 2026** PhD thesis was posted on arXiv **7 October 2026** as arXiv:2610.09855. Its abstract reports **suggestive**, not conclusive, number-volume correspondence improvements over Poisson sprinkling for selected quasi-crystalline causal sets in (1+1) and (3+1) dimensions and a (1+1)-dimensional spacetime-hyperuniform example. These are theoretical/model comparisons, **not observed gravity**. The full 342-page thesis was not independently recomputed or reviewed in its entirety in this intake.

A separate Part I of the thesis argues that semiclassical Einstein coupling to a quantum qubit could conditionally enable polynomial-resource computation of an NP-complete problem, in tension with the *physical* Extended Church–Turing Thesis. This is neither experimental evidence for quantum gravity nor a proof about P versus NP, and supplies no dynamical confirmation of the quasicrystal proposal.

## Physical claim and evidential firewall

The original paper speculates that an irrationally embedded (3+1)-dimensional universe within a symmetric (9+1)-dimensional torus could illuminate the approximate hierarchy relation M_Pl × M_vac ≈ M_EW². It gives no physical principle selecting this embedding, no gravitating quantum dynamics, no observed Standard Model masses, and no unique experimental or cosmological prediction. The logical gap between a mathematically legitimate point set and a candidate theory of everything is therefore large.

The positive evidence is explicit Lorentzian geometry and model-specific, potentially independently testable mathematical counting claims. The principal unresolved objection is the **absence of dynamics and low-energy recovery**. A next discriminating calculation would define local field propagation and a dynamical ensemble, demonstrate a Lorentzian continuum limit and Einstein-like gravity, and preregister independent tests of the counting/hyperuniformity results.

## Clash — Quasicrystalline String Landscape (B/A)

**Battleground: physical consistency of a quasicrystalline construction.** The existing *Quasicrystalline String Landscape* derives controlled Narain/string worldsheet sectors and later D-brane/orientifold, tadpole, and anomaly checks; its outstanding liability is a realistic four-dimensional Standard Model vacuum. Lorentzian Spacetime Quasicrystals directly concerns Minkowski causal geometry and proposes a new toroidal embedding, but lacks comparable dynamics and consistency checks. These projects are **not duplicates** merely because both say “quasicrystal.” A realistic matter-coupled action and new observation would change the balance. Neither prior tier changes.

## Clash — Causal Set Theory (A/A)

**Battleground: locality and continuum recovery from discrete Lorentzian data.** Causal Set Theory has decades of independent research, quantum dynamics approaches and continuum operators; it still lacks complete low-energy recovery. Deterministically structured Lorentzian quasiperiodic point sets might have more favorable number-volume fluctuations than random Poisson causal sets in certain models, but have no corresponding complete dynamical framework. An independent numerical/theoretical replication plus demonstrated continuum dynamics would make the comparison consequential. Existing A/A stays unchanged.
