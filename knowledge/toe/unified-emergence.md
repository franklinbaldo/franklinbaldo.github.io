# Unified Emergence / Continuum-First Ontology

The current manuscript is retained in the Arena because it is unusually explicit about its ontology, equations and open problems, but its scientific placement changes materially after a propagator-level audit of the Photonic-Higgs refractive layer. The stated massive PHRL equation cannot mediate ordinary long-range gravity when its mass parameter is fixed to the Higgs mass. That is a load-bearing consistency failure, so the current formulation is F/A rather than merely incomplete D/A. A repaired version would need to rederive the gravitational channel and then recheck every downstream claim that depends on the present PHRL dynamics.
