The model treats the universe as a rotating spacetime fluid: gravity as pressure, the other interactions as local pressure/wave phenomena, accelerated expansion as centrifugal splitting, and the Riemann critical line as a cosmic equilibrium boundary.

Initial Arena placement: **F scientific / B interest**, high confidence. The manuscript uses the Riemann Hypothesis as if it were already a mathematical truth proving cosmic stability even though it remains an open Millennium Prize problem; it also describes centrifugal acceleration as exponential in radius and ends its announced mathematical-formulation section before supplying the promised net-force equation. The force-unification claims remain qualitative analogies rather than quantitative recovery of known gauge theories.

Clash: **Stiff Medium Model (C/A)**, battleground continuum substrate → quantitative recovery of gravity and matter. Stiff Medium exposes a candidate action, executable checks and an explicit negative-result ledger; Cosmic Typhoon currently lacks a comparable mathematical bridge. No tier movement for Stiff Medium.
