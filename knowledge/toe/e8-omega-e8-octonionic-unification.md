E8 × ωE8 enters as a serious but still incomplete unification programme. Its scientific tier is not based on the programme's own sweeping scope or on AI-generated appraisals; it is based on the existence of a multi-year technical line, explicit formal machinery and falsifiable claims, discounted for the missing end-to-end derivation and sparse independent replication.

Against String/M-theory, the superficial commonality is the exceptional E8 corridor. The octonionic programme explicitly argues that this does not make the two theories dynamically identical: heterotic string theory has worldsheet dynamics, anomaly cancellation and a vast compactification literature, while E8 × ωE8 uses generalized trace dynamics and emergent spacetime. String/M-theory therefore retains a large maturity advantage even though the octonionic programme exposes some lower-energy numerical and collapse claims more directly. String/M-theory remains A/S.

Against Causal Fermion Systems, both try to make spacetime and conventional physics emergent from a deeper variational/dynamical substrate. CFS has much deeper continuum-limit machinery and stronger established recovery of Standard-Model-plus-gravity field equations; E8 × ωE8 has a broader explicit catalogue of particle, collapse and cosmological claims, but more of the common derivation remains prospective. CFS remains A/S.
