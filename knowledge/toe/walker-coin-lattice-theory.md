# Walker-Coin Lattice Theory

Walker-Coin Lattice Theory asks whether local quantum-walk dynamics can do some of the work normally assigned to spacetime geometry. The current public programme is strongest as a reproducible emergent-gravity calculation: its code exposes Hessians, dispersion relations, continuum-scaling probes and one-loop fermionic effects rather than leaving the proposal at the level of analogy.

The decisive next gate is recovery rather than another internal consistency test. A substantial promotion would require an independently reproduced continuum construction that survives nonlinear and precision-gravity checks while also producing realistic chiral gauge and matter structure without encoding those targets in the coin architecture. Failure of the claimed spin-2/continuum recovery under faithful replication would instead move the scientific tier downward.
