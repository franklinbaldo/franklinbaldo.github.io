# Chrono-Grid Dynamics

CGD is catalogued as a quantum-gravity contender with an explicit scope warning: its current published core aims to make general relativity emerge from a discrete unitary spin-network dynamics, not yet to derive the full Standard Model. The Arena therefore credits the formal microdynamics without treating the label, journal venue or breadth of companion claims as evidence that the framework is correct.
