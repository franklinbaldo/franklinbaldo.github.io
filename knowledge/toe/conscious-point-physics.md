# Conscious Point Physics

Contender C/S, low confidence.

## r298 — clock/ruler calibration reversal and wavefront test

Patches 4385–4389 retract an earlier assumption about ruler response and make an important distinction: a coordinate-speed law `1−U` yields only half the solar deflection/Shapiro effect, while `1−2U` can recover the observed first-order values. That coefficient is **fixed by the data**, not derived from CPP primitives. [Patch 4389](https://github.com/Hyperphysics-Institute/CPP/commit/4a69f2762fa3916f81aae1fd0cc388327827872f).

Two physical pictures remain compatible with that first-order calibration: fewer Planck-sphere steps per universal Moment while crystal PSR count stays fixed, or one step per Moment with different crystal/PSR shrinkage. The project explicitly withdraws the assertion that only one picture survives. Its second-order behavior and a unique dynamical mechanism are unresolved, so this is a useful methodological correction rather than independent confirmation of general relativity.

**Clash — Grid Universe (C/S):** CPP pursues clock/light recovery but currently fits a known observable with competing mechanisms; Grid preserves preregistered empirical no-go checks but its microscopic substrate and cluster-scale gravity remain unestablished. No tier movement.
