Causal Fermion Systems enters as a mature unification programme, not as a newly invented theory and not as a claim of scientific truth. The Arena had simply omitted it. The current discussion made that omission conspicuous: a September 2026 gravity conference features a new geometric derivation of Einstein dynamics from the causal action, while 2026 peer-reviewed work develops the relational many-body ontology of spacetime.

Against Asymptotic Safety, CFS has broader explicit Standard-Model-plus-gravity ambition and makes spacetime itself secondary to a causal variational structure. Asymptotic Safety, however, stays closer to conventional quantum field theory and renormalization-group machinery, with a clearer ultraviolet fixed-point criterion. CFS must show that its unknown microscopic regularization does more than relocate low-energy free parameters; Asymptotic Safety must establish the required fixed point and full matter-sector predictivity. The clash does not move Asymptotic Safety from A/A.

Against Observer Patch Holography, CFS has much deeper literature, established continuum machinery and stronger attachment to conventional field theory. OPH currently has the sharper finite theorem/certificate ledger and a more compact explicit failure surface. CFS gains if microscopic regularization yields discriminating, source-level predictions rather than empirical effective parameters; OPH gains if its five physical-realization obligations can be closed in one common refinement. OPH remains B/S.
