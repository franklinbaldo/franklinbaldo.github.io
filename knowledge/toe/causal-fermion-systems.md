Causal Fermion Systems is a mature unification programme, not a claim of scientific truth. The Arena first added it on 2026-09-22 as a backlog correction. The 2026 research programme remains active: a July paper gave a geometric derivation of Einstein dynamics from the causal action, and the September Finster–Gresnigt preprint now makes the gauge-sector selection mechanism substantially more systematic.

The September paper matters because it is not merely another application of a fixed Standard Model gauge group. It starts from general chiral `U(N)_L × U(N)_R` potentials, extracts a quadratic Clifford-compatibility condition from the causal-action continuum analysis and proves a classification theorem for admissible Lie algebras. The authors explicitly connect this theorem to the earlier CFS result in which `U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3)` and the electroweak/strong coupling pattern are recovered from the Standard-Model vacuum configuration. That strengthens the claim that gauge structure is constrained by the causal action rather than simply appended by hand.

The same paper also makes the current boundary unusually clear. It assumes an invertible mass matrix commuting with the chiral potentials and deliberately removes generation mixing from the calculation; CKM/PMNS mixing is left to future work. Moreover, the vacuum configuration still supplies sectors, Dirac seas, masses and chiral asymmetry, while the microscopic regularization remains unknown and controls important effective parameters. The Arena therefore records a **material technical strengthening without a scientific-tier move**: CFS remains **A scientific / S interest, medium confidence**.

Against Asymptotic Safety, CFS has broader explicit Standard-Model-plus-gravity ambition and makes spacetime itself secondary to a causal variational structure. Asymptotic Safety stays closer to conventional quantum field theory and renormalization-group machinery, with a clearer ultraviolet fixed-point criterion. CFS must show that its unknown microscopic regularization does more than relocate low-energy free parameters; Asymptotic Safety must establish the required fixed point and full matter-sector predictivity. The clash does not move Asymptotic Safety from A/A.

Against Hypercomplex Steering–Spinor Unification, the battleground is **whether observed gauge structure is selected rather than renamed**. CFS now has the stronger mature bridge: a continuum-limit Standard-Model construction plus a general Lie/Clifford admissibility theorem tied to the causal action. Its liability is that the analysis begins with a fermionic vacuum architecture and still omits flavour mixing and parameter derivation. Hypercomplex Steering–Spinor has a more compressed algebraic ambition — sedenion/E6 structure is intended to account simultaneously for gauge curvature, generations and gravity — but it lacks a comparably mature end-to-end recovery of the chiral Standard Model and controlled Einstein dynamics. The September CFS paper therefore sharpens this clash but does **not** justify moving either CFS from A/S or Hypercomplex Steering–Spinor from C/S.
