# General Quantum Gravity / Metric Mechanics

Vadurie's programme develops a metric-mechanics quantum-gravity framework and extends it toward gravity, dark energy and Standard-Model unification.

**Arena placement: F/A, high confidence for the current full-scope formulation.** A load-bearing unification claim identifies SU(5) with a construction containing SU(3) and two SU(2)-type factors. Taken literally as a group/algebra equality, the dimensions do not match: dim SU(5)=24, while 8+3+3=14. Without a different precise embedding or symmetry-breaking statement, that identity cannot support the advertised gauge unification.

**Clash:** General Standard Model / GQFT A/S. GQFT has a sustained peer-reviewed lineage and phenomenological constraints; the present Metric Mechanics unification has a central group-structure blocker. No existing tier movement.
