# General Quantum Gravity / Metric Mechanics

Vadurie's programme develops a metric-mechanics quantum-gravity framework and extends it toward gravity, dark energy and Standard-Model unification.

**Arena placement: F/A, high confidence for the current full-scope formulation.** A load-bearing unification claim identifies (SU(5)) with a construction containing (SU(3)) and two (SU(2))-type factors. Taken literally as a group/algebra equality, the dimensions do not match: (dim SU(5)=24), while (8+3+3=14). Without a different precise embedding or symmetry-breaking statement, that identity cannot support the advertised gauge unification. The broader recovery of realistic chiral Standard-Model matter and independently reproduced quantum-gravity predictions also remains open.

**Clash:** General Standard Model / GQFT A/S. Both aim to put gravity and particle physics into a common field-theoretic framework, but GQFT has a sustained peer-reviewed lineage and phenomenological constraints, whereas the present Metric Mechanics unification contains a central group-structure blocker. No existing tier movement.
