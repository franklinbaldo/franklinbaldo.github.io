# Living Phi Physics / Phi-Harmonic Field

The Arena records the current public formulation at **F scientific / S interest**. The F is not a probability of falsity and does not score creativity: it records that a central quantitative claim in the present formulation conflicts directly with established precision data, while the validation pipeline does not repair that conflict. The separate S interest score reflects the unusually large, explicit and attackable corpus, which gives the Arena many concrete places to test what is claimed.
