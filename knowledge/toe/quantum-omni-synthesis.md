# Quantum Omni-Synthesis

QOS introduces a dimensionless local implosive-energy fraction and uses it to weight the scalar kinetic sector and its gravitational response. The 2026 core paper develops the construction covariantly for a real scalar field and recovers standard limits.

**Arena placement: adjacent C/A, medium confidence.** The mathematical proposal is concrete and peer reviewed, but the published theory is still a scalar-sector EFT. It does not yet derive gauge fields, fermions or realistic Standard-Model matter, and its gravitational equations extend an already metric-based framework rather than supplying a demonstrated microscopic quantum-gravity completion.

**Clash:** Universal Thermodynamic Functional C/A. QOS has the stronger peer-reviewed scalar-field implementation and explicit GR limits; UTF has a cleaner information/variational starting point. Neither currently closes the gravity-plus-realistic-matter bridge.
