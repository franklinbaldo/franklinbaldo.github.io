# Holographic Fiber Theory

Holographic Fiber Theory is a topology-first unification programme built around a trivalent informational substrate realizing Hopf-bundle and braid structure. It attempts to recover quantum correlations, Standard-Model gauge and mass structure, gravity and cosmological observables from the same weaving rule. The Arena ranks the current programme as structured speculation with exceptionally high comparative interest: there is enough formal and computational machinery to audit, but the decisive bridges to established physics remain unreplicated and some numerical targets have changed materially across versions.
