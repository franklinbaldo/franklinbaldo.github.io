# Grand Geometric Theory (GG-Theory)

GG-Theory asks whether ordinary four-dimensional physics is the boundary projection of one six-dimensional geometry, with the two additional directions interpreted as scale and gauge phase rather than hidden spatial dimensions. Its public 2026 corpus attempts to turn that constitution into quantum mechanics, gravity, Standard-Model structure and parameters, cosmology and new particles with a small fixed integer ledger instead of a conventional landscape of tunable couplings.

The Arena's F/S split is deliberate. The programme is exceptionally interesting because it publishes source, named dependencies and unusually sharp falsifiers; scientific viability is a different question. In the current collider paper the same fixed tower that supplies the flagship 3.849 TeV prediction is reported to generate an electroweak T parameter far outside the quoted precision fit, without a custodial symmetry to suppress it. Until that conflict is resolved by a complete, independently reproducible calculation rather than an added tuning freedom, the current implementation is ranked as presently non-viable while remaining an unusually fertile target for adversarial testing.
