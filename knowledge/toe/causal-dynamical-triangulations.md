# Causal dynamical triangulations

A non-perturbative, background-independent lattice path-integral approach to Lorentzian quantum gravity. Numerical work has produced quantitative Planck-scale observables and emergent large-scale geometries. A peer-reviewed curvature-correlator study, highlighted again at the September 2026 ÖPG-CMD meeting, reports behavior compatible with a massive geon-like state; this is a useful new observable and possible pure-gravity dark-matter bridge, but not yet a continuum-established particle. The programme remains chiefly a theory of quantum spacetime rather than a complete unification of matter and forces.
