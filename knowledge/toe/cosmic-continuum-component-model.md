# Cosmic Continuum Component Model (CCCM)

CCCM asks whether one compact axiomatic language can bridge continuum spacetime, quantum discreteness, gauge interactions and thermodynamics. Its current realization uses a scale topos and a braided/fibred categorical structure based on `U_q(e8)`, with the basic component written as a particle-gauge tensor product and interactions represented through fusion and braiding.

The Arena's D/A split is intentional. The proposal is much more concrete than a purely verbal Theory of Everything and publishes explicit axioms, proof-status tables and future observational targets. Scientific maturity is lower: the July 2026 manuscript itself calls the construction conceptual and leaves full formalization open, while several supposed recoveries of known physics are already stipulated in the axioms or correspondence conditions. The most useful next test is therefore not another broad unification claim, but an external reconstruction showing that realistic chiral Standard-Model data and the Einstein limit actually follow from the frozen categorical rules without being reintroduced by assumption.
