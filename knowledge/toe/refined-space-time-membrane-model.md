# Refined Space-Time Membrane Model (RSTM)

RSTM asks whether a single deterministic higher-derivative elasticity field can serve as a microscopic substrate from which quantum-like statistics, gauge structure and gravity emerge after coarse-graining. Its present form is substantially more concrete than the original membrane analogy: the v7 paper gives one master PDE, explicit ultraviolet regulators, spinor/gauge constructions, coarse-grained measurement dynamics, gravity-sector dispersion relations, FRG calculations and phenomenological bridges to flavour and collider observables.

The Arena's C/S split is deliberate. There is enough mathematics and numerical machinery for serious adversarial testing, but several advertised recoveries still contain the structures they are meant to explain as assumptions, calibration anchors or phenomenological ansatzes. The decisive next step is therefore not another wider claim: it is an independent reconstruction showing that frozen membrane dynamics produce a realistic chiral Standard Model and a prospective observable that distinguishes RSTM from ordinary higher-derivative EFT alternatives.
