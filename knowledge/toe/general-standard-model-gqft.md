# General Standard Model / Gravitational Quantum Field Theory

Yue-Liang Wu's Gravitational Quantum Field Theory (GQFT) treats gravity as a gauge interaction based on spin/scaling symmetries and develops a biframe/gravigauge description intended to share the quantum-field-theory footing of the other fundamental forces. The later General Standard Model (GSM) enlarges this programme to the Standard Model, cosmology and additional gauge sectors, while a hyperunified branch explores a still more radical higher-dimensional unification. The programme now has weak-field, frame-dragging, gravitational-wave and dark-sector phenomenology that can be confronted with experiments.
