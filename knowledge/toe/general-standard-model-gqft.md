# General Standard Model / Gravitational Quantum Field Theory

A/S, medium confidence.

## r284 — breathing-mode constraint

A new GWTC analysis tests an added scalar breathing polarization motivated in part by GQFT. None of the eleven analyzed events favors it. The best 90% limits on the scalar-to-tensor amplitude are approximately 0.15, 0.18 and 0.23, which disfavors order-one amplitude for the strongest events. Because masses and spins are fixed at posterior medians and the test is a phenomenological polarization model rather than a complete GQFT inference, this is meaningful negative evidence without a tier change.
