# Information-Complete Quantum Theory (ICQT / ICQFT)

ICQT is a long-running quantum-information approach in which quantized spacetime, matter fermions and their gauge fields form a single information-complete trinity. The Arena places it at B scientific / A interest: there is enough peer-reviewed formal development and gravity-plus-matter structure for a serious-contender classification, while the full observed Standard Model, independent reconstruction and theory-specific empirical confirmation remain open.
