ODTOE enters as an explicit full-scope observer-centred ToE proposal, but at D rather than C/B because its main burden is still ahead: showing that the observer formalism actually generates established physics quantitatively rather than redescribing it in a new vocabulary.

Against Observer Patch Holography, both challenge an observer-free starting point, but the mechanisms are substantially different. ODTOE promotes observer state, contextual belief and coherence to fundamental variables; OPH builds from finite public patches, overlap consistency, records and repair operations and already has machine-checkable/certified reconstruction results for parts of quantum and gauge structure. OPH's physical-realization bridge is incomplete, but it is still much closer to a falsifiable derivation pipeline, so OPH remains B/S while ODTOE enters D/A.

A future promotion would require an explicit common derivation of standard quantum/field/gravitational observables from the ODTOE variables, with calibrated physical measurement protocols and predictions that distinguish the programme from ordinary relational/operational interpretations without using observer belief as a flexible fit variable.
