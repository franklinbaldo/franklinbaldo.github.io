# Emergent Gravity from Quantum Information Flow

Yang, Yang and Mei propose a low-energy EFT in which an entropy-current vector couples to Dirac fermions and has Proca-type dynamics. They argue that integrating its fluctuations can induce an Einstein-Hilbert term and discuss spin-precession and gravitational-wave observables.

**Arena placement: adjacent C/A, medium confidence.** The construction is concrete and testable, but it is not yet a full gravity-plus-Standard-Model theory: the manuscript does not derive SM gauge/matter content, uses curved-spacetime and curvature-coupling ingredients before the induced-gravity step, and has not yet received independent validation.

**Clash:** Universal Thermodynamic Functional C/A. This proposal has a more explicit fermion/vector EFT; the thermodynamic-functional entry has a clearer variational quantum-mechanics sector and states its gravitational assumptions more explicitly. No tier movement.
