## r283 — projected Yang–Mills and MacDowell–Mansouri (2026-10-06)

Alvarez's 67-page preprint (arXiv:2610.08566) analyzes SO(2n) gauge theories with a degenerate curvature kinetic operator set by an orthogonal complex structure. With the adjoint Higgs *exactly constrained* to SO(2n)/U(n), nonlinear Dirac constraint analysis identifies a regular weak-curvature branch with 2(n^2−1) physical degrees of freedom in four dimensions, locally equivalent to SU(n) Yang–Mills. A Clifford-involution representation links the projected gauge action to a Stelle–West/MacDowell–Mansouri gravity analogue. This supplies a genuine restricted-sector consistency check, not just group-theoretic resemblance.

**Limitations:** unrestricted Higgs displacements may change the kinetic rank, and this work neither establishes full coupled nonlinear stability nor derives realistic chiral Standard Model families. No independent empirical confirmation follows. **Clash:** GQFT/GSM (A/S) has more extended phenomenology, whereas Geometric Action (B/A) supplies a relatively economical single-action route but weaker empirical contact. **B/A remains unchanged, medium confidence.**
