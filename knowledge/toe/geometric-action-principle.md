Alvarez, Fernando Izaurieta and Cristian Quinzacara construct a Clifford-algebraic invariant action in four dimensions that generates gravity, Yang–Mills fields and fermionic kinetic terms from a single gauge curvature, without extra dimensions or an externally inserted symmetry-breaking field. Neither has the empirical discriminator or independent reproduction needed for A.
