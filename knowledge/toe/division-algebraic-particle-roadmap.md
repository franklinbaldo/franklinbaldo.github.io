# Division-Algebraic Particle Roadmap / Nested Division Symmetries

Adjacent B/S, medium confidence.

The programme treats the nested division algebras and their multiplication/Clifford structures as an organizing principle for particle symmetries. Its strongest claim is structural: constrained algebraic constructions recover substantial Standard-Model internal symmetry and chiral organization without simply starting from the final gauge group.

The 2026 revision adds a fully connected nested division-algebraic Hopf-fibration tree and a construction tied to the Cl(0,8) Bott-periodicity clock. That is a meaningful mathematical extension, but it does not yet close the three-generation problem, derive observed flavor parameters, or add a gravitational sector.
