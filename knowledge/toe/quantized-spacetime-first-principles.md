# Quantized Spacetime from First Principles

The proposal asks how much of quantum field theory, gauge structure and gravity can be recovered if spacetime itself has irreducible Planck-scale cells. The Arena records the current v4 as a structured unification programme, not as evidence that spacetime is in fact discrete.

The most informative next step is an independent end-to-end reconstruction from the stated assumptions with no rescue terms added after comparison: recover precision Lorentz behavior, a genuinely chiral Standard-Model matter sector and the emergent Einstein limit, then freeze one prediction before new data are examined.
