# CronNet-Holo / 600-Cell Spectral Unification

CronNet-Holo is a geometry-first unification programme based on the regular 600-cell, binary icosahedral symmetry and spectral constructions. Its current synthesis makes the Standard Model and gravity conditional on five explicit physical-realization hypotheses and offers sharp discriminators, most notably a predicted 12.8 THz vacuum-absorption feature. The Arena therefore treats it as structured speculation with unusually high experimental and comparative interest, not as an established derivation.
