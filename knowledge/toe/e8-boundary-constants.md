# E8 Boundary-Constants Programme

The programme claims to derive Standard-Model and gravitational parameters from the dimension, root length, dual Coxeter number and central charge of E8.

**Arena placement: adjacent D/A, medium confidence.** The formulas are unusually explicit and easy to audit, but the corpus has not yet supplied a complete dynamical bridge from the E8 lattice to realistic low-energy physics. The stated count of independent predictions also needs care: for example, the electron gravitational coupling and electron mass are linked by the defining relation alpha_G=(m_e/m_Pl)^2.

**Clash:** Geometry of Everything C/A. Both seek extreme parameter compression; the E8 programme is narrower and numerically explicit, while Geometry of Everything supplies a broader dynamical framework. No existing tier movement.
