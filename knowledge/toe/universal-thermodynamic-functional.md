# Universal Thermodynamic Functional

Bougueroua proposes one free-energy functional on probability distributions combining Fisher information, potential energy and Shannon entropy. With a quantum normalization it recovers continuity plus quantum Hamilton-Jacobi/Schrodinger structure; at macroscopic scale it invokes local thermodynamic assumptions to recover Einstein equations.

**Arena placement: adjacent C/A, medium confidence.** The variational construction is explicit and exposes crossover/Landauer tests, but its gravity sector assumes local Lorentz invariance, Clausius-Unruh thermodynamics and an area entropy law. The manuscript explicitly says the area law is not derived from the microscopic functional, and it does not derive Standard-Model matter. The scale-dependent cosmological term is phenomenological.

**Clash:** Geometry as Thermodynamics B/A. The latter is narrower but more controlled about what horizon thermodynamics can and cannot determine; this framework is broader, while its gravitational bridge imports more assumptions. No existing tier movement.
