# Computational Finitism

Computational Finitism treats continuum mathematics and physical infinities as effective descriptions of a finite computational substrate. The Arena records it because the 2026 programme attempts genuine gravity-plus-particle unification and supplies runnable constructions, not because its claims are considered established.

The most informative next test is an independent reimplementation from a frozen rule set. A successful reproduction would need to recover precision relativistic propagation, a realistic chiral gauge/matter sector, the measured and scale-dependent electromagnetic coupling, and a controlled gravitational limit without inserting new rescue mechanisms after each mismatch. Failure of those load-bearing claims under faithful replication would move the scientific tier downward; successful prospective recovery would justify reconsideration upward.
