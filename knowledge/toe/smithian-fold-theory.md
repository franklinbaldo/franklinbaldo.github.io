# Smithian Fold Theory of Everything

The Smithian Fold Theory starts from the One and a doubling fold on exact positive rational quantities and claims that the resulting structure fixes particle physics, gravity, cosmology and further domains without fitted continuous parameters.

The Arena separates scientific strength from interest. The present formulation is **F scientific** because fixed numerical outputs in the public corpus conflict with established precision measurements. It remains **S interest** because its open implementation and timestamped prediction ledger make it unusually testable and suitable for independent reconstruction.
