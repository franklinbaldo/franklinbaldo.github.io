EMEO proposes a single continuous electromagnetic medium: matter as toroidal light vortices, gravity as refractive steering in a variable-permittivity medium, and nuclear binding without independent strong-force degrees of freedom.

Initial Arena placement: **F scientific / A interest**, high confidence. The current manuscript explicitly removes gluons/color and the strong interaction without reproducing the established quantitative QCD phenomenology that those degrees of freedom describe, while its gravity and particle claims remain first-party mechanical identifications rather than end-to-end recoveries. The interest score is separate: the one-medium ontology is unusually broad, concrete, and easy to attack experimentally.

Clash: **Vortex Cosmos of Vacuum Superfluid (D/A)**, battleground single-medium substrate → realistic particle physics + gravity. Vortex Cosmos also has major recovery debts, but EMEO makes the stronger conflicting move of deleting QCD's established color/gluon structure. No movement for Vortex Cosmos.
