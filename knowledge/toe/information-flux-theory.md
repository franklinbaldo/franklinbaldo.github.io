# Information Flux Theory

IFT proposes a single-fermion/operator reinterpretation of particle physics and an emergent route to gravity. Its first public Preprints.org version appeared on 2025-05-14; the current v9 was posted on 2026-05-27. The Arena treats the programme's claimed mass/mixing agreements, emergent-gravity relation and Millennium-problem proofs as claims requiring independent reconstruction, not as established results.

The most informative next test is not another breadth claim. It is an external, end-to-end reproduction of one load-bearing chain—preferably the parameter-free fermion masses/mixing matrices or the proposed color-tension-to-Newton-coupling derivation—followed by a genuinely prospective prediction.
