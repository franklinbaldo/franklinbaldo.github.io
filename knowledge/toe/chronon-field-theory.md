summary: "Bin Li's Chronon Field Theory takes a future-directed unit timelike vector field—the 'Real Now'—as the single substrate from which effective geometry, gauge interactions and matter are meant to emerge. Gravity is associated with Chronon alignment/curvature, electromagnetism with phase, weak interactions with shear, strong confinement with flux tubes, and fermions with topological solitons. The current v3 unification manuscript contains an unusually decisive mathematical failure in a load-bearing step: it correctly defines the target of the unit timelike vector as the future unit hyperboloid H^3, but then states that H^3 is homotopy equivalent to S^2 and assigns nontrivial π2 and π3 groups. This breaks the stated topological basis for soliton protection, fermion number and the generation construction in the present formulation."

# Chronon Field Theory (CFT)

The Arena records CFT at F scientific / S interest. The scientific tier is driven by a load-bearing topology error in the current broad v3 unification manuscript, while the separate S interest tier reflects how unusually compact and testable the one-field temporal ontology is. The existence of narrower peer-reviewed CFT work raises the programme's maturity but does not repair that specific contradiction. A mathematically repaired target-space construction would deserve a fresh audit rather than being permanently bound to this placement.
