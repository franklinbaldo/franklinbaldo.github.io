# Macroscopic Gravitational Symmetry Breaking

Lentz proposes that the very small macroscopic gravitational coupling is the residual isotropic variance of much larger one-dimensional QCD flux-tube tension. Extreme rotation is claimed to bias the available angular phase space, expose part of that tension through a Finslerian metric response and generate a nonlinear thermodynamic backreaction called quantum drag.

**Arena placement: adjacent C/A, low confidence.** The programme has a real mathematical mechanism, a laboratory target and a related July 2026 torsion-balance preprint claiming a rotation-dependent anisotropic signal above 5 sigma after several environmental controls. That is meaningful empirical contact, but not yet strong evidence: the experiment is unreviewed, no independent replication was located, and the September theory uses Crab-pulsar data to bound the same effect it later invokes for pulsar braking. A much stronger case would require blind independent replication and a successful prediction outside the calibration data. The framework is also gravity-adjacent rather than a complete ToE because it does not derive the full Standard Model.

**Distinctive tests:** independent torsion-balance replication with preregistered vibration/thermal/electromagnetic vetoes, angular-scaling tests, and comparisons with stringent Lorentz/equivalence constraints.

**Clash:** Emergent Gravity from Quantum Information Flow C/A. Both add a microscopic substrate behind effective gravity; the information-flow model has a cleaner low-energy EFT language, while this programme has a more direct laboratory anomaly claim. Neither has independent validation sufficient for a tier move.
