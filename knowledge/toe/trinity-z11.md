# Trinity / Z11 Operator-Algebraic Theory of Everything

Trinity asks whether a small operator-algebraic structure built on the cyclic group `Z11`, together with a seven-axiom Sphere-Point-Cone ontology, can generate gravity, the Standard Model, generations, cosmology and a large catalogue of constants without continuous fit parameters. The programme is unusually public and computationally explicit: its repository contains a long bilingual treatise, Python checks, Lean 4 arithmetic verification and concrete predictions.

The Arena nevertheless places the current v1.2 formulation at **F scientific / S interest**. The split is intentional. A central theorem says the quadratic residues form the *unique* proper nontrivial subgroup of `Z_11*` and uses that uniqueness to force the metric carrier. But `Z_11*` is cyclic of order 10: besides the order-5 quadratic-residue subgroup `{1,3,4,5,9}`, it has the distinct order-2 subgroup `{1,10}`. That is a direct counterexample inside the theory's own chosen group, so the advertised uniqueness and the downstream physical interpretation are not established as written. The extensive machine checks remain valuable artifacts, but they do not turn an unencoded false semantic premise into a theorem.
