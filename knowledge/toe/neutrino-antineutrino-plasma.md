# Neutrino–Antineutrino Plasma Unification

The Arena records the present v5 formulation at F scientific / S interest. The low scientific placement is driven by a concrete empirical collision, not by unconventional ontology: the public energy-cutoff argument is already in tension with KM3NeT's ultra-high-energy cosmic-neutrino observation. The S interest score is deliberately independent and reflects the proposal's unusually broad, quantitative and falsifiable claim surface.
