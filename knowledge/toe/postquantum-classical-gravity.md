# Postquantum Classical Gravity

A serious alternative in which gravity remains classical but its coupling to quantum matter is fundamentally stochastic.

**Arena placement: adjacent A/S, medium confidence.** The programme is technically mature, peer-reviewed, independently scrutinized and experimentally constrainable, but it does not derive the full Standard Model.

**Clash:** String/M-theory A/S on whether gravity must be quantum. String theory has far broader unification and UV machinery; postquantum gravity is narrower but exposes nearer-term stochastic/decoherence tests. No tier movement.
