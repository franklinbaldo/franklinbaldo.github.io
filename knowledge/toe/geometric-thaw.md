# Geometric Thaw Theory of Everything

Garstin models the vacuum as a finite-temperature superfluid/BEC and connects collective-fluid behaviour to gravity, cosmology, dark sectors, decoherence and particle structure.

**Arena placement: D/S, medium confidence.** The programme has explicit Gross-Pitaevskii/Madelung and acoustic-metric machinery plus many possible tests, but it has not yet derived the bridge from analogue-fluid geometry to physical 3+1 Einstein gravity or a realistic chiral Standard Model from one controlled microscopic action.

**Clash:** Elastic Membrane Cosmology D/S. Both are broad hydrodynamic unification programmes whose decisive open gate is recovery of Lorentz/GR/SM dynamics rather than analogy-level correspondence.
