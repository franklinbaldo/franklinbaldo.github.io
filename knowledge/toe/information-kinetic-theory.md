# Information-Kinetic Theory (IKT)

The Arena records IKT at **C scientific / S interest**. The scientific tier recognizes a concrete and unusually broad mathematical programme without treating its internal anchors or interface matches as external validation. The interest tier is deliberately separate: a single dynamic-graph/quantum-walk substrate that exposes gravity, gauge sectors, particles, dark sectors and several prospective tests is highly generative even while its central derivations remain incomplete.
