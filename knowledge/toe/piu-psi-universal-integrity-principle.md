# PIU-Ψ / Universal Integrity Principle

PIU-Ψ asks whether one complex elastic medium can replace the usual separation between spacetime, quantum fields, matter and the dark sector. Its F1 Lagrangian combines kinetic, elastic/topological, higher-derivative and affine-wall terms; different limits are then connected to Madelung-Bohm quantum mechanics, Sakharov-style emergent gravity, phase/gauge structure, Skyrmion matter, Q-balls and condensate cosmology.

The Arena's C/S split is deliberate. PIU is far more developed and falsifiable than a verbal "everything from one medium" story, and its public audit machinery is unusually serious. But executable self-consistency checks do not substitute for independent physics validation. The decisive next step is narrower than another extension of scope: freeze the rules and have an external calculation reproduce a load-bearing gravity or realistic chiral-Standard-Model derivation, then survive a prospective observable that was not used to build the framework.
