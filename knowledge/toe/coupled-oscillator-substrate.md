# Coupled Quantum Oscillator Substrate

**Arena placement: adjacent D/A, high confidence.** The current paper earns an Arena entry because it is a concrete, falsifiable microscopic hypothesis, but it explicitly establishes only the first kinematic layer of the proposed emergence hierarchy.

Its exact dispersion relation approaches relativistic propagation at long wavelength and produces anisotropic quadratic corrections. Those are useful, testable outputs. The same fixed lattice, however, builds in the dimension, connectivity and preferred microscopic frame that a deeper pregeometry would ideally derive, and no gravitational dynamics or realistic matter sector has yet been obtained.

**Clash:** Elastic-Substrate LEDM D/A. The oscillator proposal has the cleaner first-step calculation; LEDM reaches further into gravity. Both remain early hypotheses, so no tier movement.
