# The Emergent Frame

Xiaodan Wu's TEF programme separates persistent closed matter topology, source-local spacetime rollout and excitations supported by that structure. Follow-up September papers conditionally construct three-dimensional effective geometry and a compact connection compatible with classical Maxwell-Lorentz electrodynamics.

**Arena placement: adjacent D/A, medium confidence.** TEF is unusually careful about the distinction between geometric correspondences and physical derivations, and it preserves source, checksums and numerical verification code. But its own current status says that strong, weak, electromagnetic and gravitational mechanisms are research directions rather than a completed unified dynamics. The helix does not itself imply a wave equation or Lorentz covariance, and a realistic chiral Standard Model is not derived.

**Clash:** Geometric Thaw D/S, on the battleground of emergent-medium geometry versus recovery of physical Lorentz/GR/SM dynamics. TEF has cleaner epistemic boundaries and a more modular programme; Geometric Thaw has more explicit fluid dynamics. Neither closes the microscopic-to-observed-physics bridge, so no tier movement.
