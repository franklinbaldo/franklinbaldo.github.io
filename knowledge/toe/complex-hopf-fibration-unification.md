# Complex Hopf Fibration Gauge–Gravity Unification

This topology-first programme treats the universal complex Hopf fibration and its finite shells as the common geometric source of gauge sectors, matter spectral data and gravity. The Arena now places it at C scientific / S interest: the formal construction is substantial enough to audit canonically, but the step from universal bundle mathematics to uniquely compelled physical dynamics remains the decisive unverified bridge.
