# One-Octonion Brane-Bulk Framework

**Arena placement: C/S, low confidence.** OOB is far too developed to dismiss as a slogan: the current omnibus contains hundreds of linked technical notes and a very large set of concrete claims. But volume is not independent evidence. The load-bearing question is whether the octonionic/G2 brane-bulk seed uniquely and reproducibly yields the claimed Standard-Model, gravitational and cosmological outputs.

The correction history is scientifically useful. The current omnibus records a corrected uncertainty product and withdrawal of a prior curvature-radius prediction, while a June paper explicitly retracts two stronger Klein-quartic identifications after comparison with an external octonionic spectrum. That makes the programme unusually auditable, but also argues for conservative evidential weighting until external groups reconstruct frozen claims.

**Clash — E8 × ωE8 Octonionic Unification (B/S):** both use exceptional/octonionic structure as a bridge toward matter and gravity. OOB currently exposes more numerical and phenomenological targets; E8 × ωE8 has the stronger established technical lineage and clearer generalized-trace-dynamics machinery. Independent reconstruction of a realistic chiral Standard Model plus Einstein limit from either common substrate would materially change this comparison. No tier movement.
