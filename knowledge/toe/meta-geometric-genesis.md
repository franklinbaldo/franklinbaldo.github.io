# Meta-Geometric Genesis of Spacetime

This entry is **adjacent B/A**, not a full-ToE promotion. Its strength is a serious peer-reviewed mechanism for geometrogenesis: geometry, the Einstein sector and topological gravitational terms arise after gauge-symmetry breaking from a pre-geometric BF phase. Its limitation is equally explicit: a complete quantum transition and realistic chiral matter sector are still missing.
