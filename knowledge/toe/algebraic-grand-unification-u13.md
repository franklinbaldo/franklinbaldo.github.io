# Algebraic Grand Unification via U(1,3) x U(1,3)

Shifa Liu proposes that representation theory of a noncompact U(1,3) x U(1,3) structure generates common arithmetic patterns across electroweak masses, charged-lepton logarithms, the QCD beta-function, a mass-spin gravity relation and an intrinsic reinterpretation of string theory.

**Arena placement: C/A, low confidence.** The proposal is structured mathematical speculation rather than a verbal unification claim, but the full theorem chain and physical recovery have not yet been independently reconstructed. The current public record is one non-peer-reviewed working paper with zero listed citations, so the advertised parameter-free coincidences are not yet enough for B.

**Clash:** E8 Boundary-Constants D/A, on whether a small group-theoretic seed genuinely derives rather than retrospectively organizes measured constants. U(1,3) offers a more explicit representation-theoretic narrative; both still owe independent reconstruction and distinctive prospective tests. No existing tier movement.
