The scientific and interest letters answer different questions. **F scientific** records that the present July 2026 formulation is not viable as written because one of its central gravity-inertia consequences is already acknowledged by the paper to miss the observed cosmic energy budget, and the proposed repair is conjectural rather than calculated. It is not a statement that every attempt to generalize Newtonian organizing principles is impossible.

**A interest** reflects generativity rather than evidential support. The framework makes a small number of organizing claims carry an unusually large explanatory load: active/passive force pairs, strong-weak symmetry, a Higgs-mediated interpretation of inertia and a staged dimensional cosmology. Those claims generate clear adversarial questions even though they currently fail the scientific-maturity gate.

### Clash: Shaking Tangled Dimensions

**Battleground: incompleteness versus conflict with established physics.** Shaking Tangled Dimensions remains **D/A**: its Core-lattice ontology is highly incomplete, with no controlled continuum recovery of Lorentz invariance, GR and the chiral Standard Model, but the current Arena audit does not identify a comparably direct load-bearing observational contradiction in its defining equations. The Generalized Newton candidate is more compact and makes a sharper cosmological statement, but that sharpness currently hurts it: its own paper says the resulting 50% dark-energy prediction conflicts with the observed value and does not derive the required correction. The clash therefore explains the D-versus-F distinction rather than moving Shaking Tangled Dimensions.

A future version could be reconsidered if it derives, before looking at the target, a quantitative cosmological budget consistent with CMB/large-scale-structure constraints and supplies a field-theoretic strong/weak sector that reproduces observed representations, symmetries and scattering. Independent reproduction would then matter for movement beyond an initial recovery to C/D.
