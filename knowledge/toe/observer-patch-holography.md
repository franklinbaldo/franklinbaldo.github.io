Observer Patch Holography is entered as a serious contender, not as a validated physical theory. The strongest feature of the current programme is epistemic bookkeeping: its flagship distinguishes finite theorems from reconstruction implications, diagnostics and unsupplied realization maps, so a mathematical success inside the twelve-port observer architecture is not automatically promoted to a statement about nature.

Against the Geometric Action Principle, OPH currently offers a much broader reconstruction chain and a stronger explicit theorem/falsification ledger, but the Geometric Action programme begins much closer to conventional continuum gauge, fermion and gravitational fields. OPH therefore has more explanatory ambition and more physical attachment debt. A common realized refinement carrying events, currents, fields, scales and action would materially strengthen OPH; failure to produce that bridge would push it toward C.

Against String/M-theory, OPH has a compact and highly inspectable finite architecture with unusually explicit failure conditions, while string theory has vastly deeper independent mathematical development, ultraviolet machinery and decades of scrutiny. The current comparison does not warrant movement of String/M-theory from A/S.
