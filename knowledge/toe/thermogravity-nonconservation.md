# Thermogravity

Adjacent programme **B scientific / S interest**, confidence **medium**. It proposes cosmological dynamics arising from a preferred thermodynamic frame and nonconservation of matter energy, with possible acceleration without gravitating vacuum energy. This is not yet a quantum-complete theory of gravity or a theory unifying Standard-Model interactions.

## New evidence (r317)

Brereton, Magueijo and Alí Caballo, [arXiv:2610.08853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08853), submitted **3 October 2026** and shown in the **8 October** gr-qc listing, analytically classify flat homogeneous dust solutions.

The universal nonconserved-dust sector admits an exact first integral c and three futures: c > -1/6 leads to asymptotically de Sitter expansion; c = -1/6 is the previously highlighted analytic trajectory; c < -1/6 recollapses. Accordingly, the prominent a(t) ∝ t^(2/3) exp[t/(3L_2)] solution is a **critical separatrix**, not a generic late-time attractor.

When baryons are conserved and only CDM is nonconserved, vacuum endpoints with 0 < u_0 < 3 have two-dimensional stable sheets. Yet the exactly zero-CDM boundary can require a positive cosmological-constant-like integration constant for finite-u endpoints: sending CDM to zero after late-time evolution differs from setting CDM to zero initially.

**For:** exact classification, nontrivial stable sheets and an explicit identification of phenomenologically important boundary conditions. **Against/unresolved:** the universal implementation was already observationally disfavored, the surviving CDM-only model has not passed perturbation/CMB/growth tests, and the phase-space calculation deliberately leaves radiation and curvature out.

**Discriminating checks:** propagate the allowed phase-space sheets through full radiation-to-present dynamics; calculate CMB anisotropy and growth of structure with CDM-only nonconservation; constrain preferred-frame effects, and test whether the integration constant is fixed or tuned.

## Clash

Against **Nonconservative Unimodular Gravity**, the battleground is whether nonconservation and integration constants yield a physical account of late-time acceleration rather than relocating a free parameter. Thermogravity now has a precise separatrix classification but still has a narrow empirically surviving branch. Against **Einstein-Scalar-Gauss-Bonnet Quintessential Inflation B/A**, it has a more economical late-dust setup but lacks the latter's explicit inflationary multi-field narrative. Neither clash warrants a tier movement.

## Historical baseline

The 24 September [observational analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29206) found the universal implementation strongly disfavored and a CDM-only background alternative still admissible in the datasets tested. r317 retains that result; the October phase-space classification is **additional theoretical information**, not new observational confirmation.
