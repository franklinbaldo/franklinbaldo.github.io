# AME(4,6) Theory of Everything

The AME(4,6) programme treats a highly entangled four-quhex state and associated topological data as a possible generative substrate for particle physics and gravity. The mathematical AME object is independently established, but the physical mapping is not. Its strongest Arena feature is epistemic rather than confirmatory: the corpus exposes failed fits and partial retractions alongside surviving claims, making it unusually suitable for adversarial comparison.
