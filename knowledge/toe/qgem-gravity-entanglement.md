# QGEM Gravity-Entanglement Programme

B/S as an adjacent experimental programme, medium confidence.

The 5 October 2026 white paper proposes a 1-10 Hz matter-wave interferometer programme using masses around 10^-15 to 10^-14 kg, separations around 30-50 micrometres and coherence around 0.1-1 s. For representative parameters, differential acceleration noise must be kept well below 10^-15 m s^-2/sqrt(Hz).

A positive result would strongly constrain gravity/quantum models, but it would not by itself prove a unique microscopic theory of gravity.
