# Quantum-Gravitational-Informational Theory (QGI)

QGI treats information as a primordial substrate and uses informational geometry to relate gravitation, particle parameters and cosmology. Its current formulation is concrete and falsifiable enough for Arena inclusion, but its strongest validation claims remain substantially weaker than its rhetoric: important low-energy structures are still imported, several comparisons are anchored to known data, and no independent reconstruction of the full unification chain was located.
