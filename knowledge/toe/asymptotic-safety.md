## r357 — Scalar–tensor fixed points beyond the usual gauge class (7 October 2026)

**Evidence-driven placement: A scientific / A interest, medium confidence; unchanged.** Samuel M. P. Antunes and Antonio D. Pereira, [arXiv:2610.10791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10791), investigate the functional RG for scalar–tensor gravity using a **dynamical-scalar-dependent** gauge-fixing condition rather than another parameter choice in the commonly used linear-covariant family. In the approximation used, Gaussian-matter fixed-point candidates reappear across several metric-field parametrizations, adding a meaningful test of their robustness.

**Unresolved:** Exponents for scalar mass and non-minimal scalar-curvature coupling have real parts very close to zero. Depending on graviton anomalous-dimension and other choices, the model has **two or four** relevant directions. This is not a proved universal count, nor does it establish regulator/truncation independence, realistic matter parameters or new empirical support.

**Clash — Low-Energy GR EFT (A/A):** Asymptotic Safety aims for a UV-complete gravity–matter trajectory; low-energy GR EFT has better controlled low-energy expansion but does not claim its own UV completion. Demonstrating regulator/truncation stability and UV-to-IR predictions for observed matter could strengthen Asymptotic Safety's placement. This single new independent gauge-class calculation does not change A/A.
