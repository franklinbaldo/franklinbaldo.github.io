# Asymptotic safety

A quantum-gravity programme in which gravity remains predictive at arbitrarily high energies through an interacting ultraviolet fixed point. September 2026 results strengthen both gauge-control and UV-to-IR phenomenology, including a possible asymptotic-safety route to a Horndeski scalar-tensor sector.
