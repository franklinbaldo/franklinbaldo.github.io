Adjacent **B/S**, medium confidence.

## r357 — Spin-1 microcausality and observable degeneracy (7 October 2026)

**Evidence-driven placement: B scientific / S interest, medium confidence; unchanged.** Trevor Cheung, Carlos Duaso-Pueyo, David Stefanyszyn and Dong-Gang Wang, [arXiv:2610.10693](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10693), classify a five-parameter *Boostless Proca* effective spin-1 field on inflationary backgrounds, under scale invariance, rotations, Bunch–Davies initial conditions and microcausality. In this setting, the allowed kinetic terms must be gauge invariant. **No new independent tree-level scalar bispectrum shapes** emerge beyond a more general cosmological-collider framework; **trispectra and higher-point correlators distinguish** models by admitting transverse-helicity exchange contributions.

**Evidence limits:** This is a theory of potential primordial observables, not a detection of new spin-1 fields or a derived microscopic quantum-gravity completion. Signals can be suppressed and the results assume specific symmetry, initial-state and local tree-interaction conditions. The absence of new bispectrum shapes is a conditional degeneracy, not a general impossibility theorem for all cosmologies.

**Clash — String/M-theory (A/S):** A UV construction may predict heavy inflationary matter, while cosmological collider models organize which multipoint signals would distinguish it. A reliable mapping from a concrete string compactification to a measurable trispectrum, and then actual data, would alter the comparison; this new classification alone does not.
