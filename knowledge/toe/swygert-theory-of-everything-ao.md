TSTOEAO enters as a full-scope contender because it explicitly claims a substrate-level unification of physical law rather than merely an analogy within one sector. The Arena does not treat the breadth of that claim as evidence by itself: the current D tier records that the load-bearing physical bridge is still a research programme.

The most scientifically useful part of the corpus is its recent move toward explicit epistemic bookkeeping. The 167X series distinguishes ontology, phenomenology, derived physics and experimental heuristics, states failure modes, and requires a pre-specified scaling signal rather than an isolated anomaly. That is genuine progress in falsifiability, but it does not turn a phenomenological scaffold into a derived fundamental law.

Against Observer Patch Holography, both programmes treat relations, boundaries and observer-accessible structure as more fundamental than familiar continuum descriptions. TSTOEAO has a wider cross-domain ontology and more immediately legible experimental ledgers; OPH has substantially stronger finite reconstruction machinery, machine-checkable components, and a more explicit route toward Standard-Model and Einstein structure. OPH remains B/S. A TSTOEAO move toward C would require a fixed physical state space and dynamics, an end-to-end recovery of at least one load-bearing GR/SM bridge without post-hoc normalization, and independent reproduction or a successful prospective discriminator.
