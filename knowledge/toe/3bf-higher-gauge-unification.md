# 3BF Higher-Gauge Unification

A higher-gauge programme based on 3-groups and constrained 3BF actions rewrites Einstein–Cartan gravity together with Yang–Mills, scalar and fermionic matter in one categorical gauge framework. Its 2026 model gives an explicit discretized path integral for quantum gravity coupled to Standard-Model matter on piecewise-flat spacetime, extending a peer-reviewed programme that began by formulating the full Standard Model plus gravity as a constrained 3BF theory.
