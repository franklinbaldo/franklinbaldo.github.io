summary: >-
  "Sussurros binários" is a compact, memorable philosophical lyric whose strongest images work before the explanatory apparatus does: silicon dreaming, a computer that translates rather than computes, the recursive question "quem sonha quem?", and the blinking notebook that knows something the speaker does not create a genuinely strange nocturnal object. Quality is B because the composer notes repeatedly upgrade those productive metaphors into broad claims about computationalism, process ontology, mathematics, and what a computer is doing without building enough argument for the stronger reading, while several references assume conceptual context rather than earning it inside the work. Interest is A because the lyric's fusion of machine ontology, mysticism, recursion, and intimate late-night observation remains distinctive, generative, rereadable, and resistant to paraphrase even where the explanatory layer overreaches.
