summary: >-
  "Menino Que Você Foi" is a strong guided-memory piece whose best writing turns a familiar childhood-regression format into a formally patient lyric: sensory details, line breaks, silence and the final invitation to say "eu me lembro de você" or "obrigado" make the meditation do more than merely describe nostalgia. Quality is B because the achieved lyric is stronger than the philosophical explanation around it: the notes move from an evocative process intuition to the claim that past events "persist as structure in the present" and invoke Whitehead without specifying the psychological or metaphysical bridge. Interest is A because the work redescribes a familiar emotional domain through the unusual conjunction of guided meditation, autobiographical context and process philosophy, and multiple Hrönir perspectives find that redescription generative rather than merely sentimental.
