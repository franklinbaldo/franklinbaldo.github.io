summary: >-
  "Beatriz" is a high-concept adaptation experiment whose real invention is not new lyric writing but collision: the opening grief and portrait catalogue of Borges's "O Aleph" are carried almost unchanged into distorted trap/phonk, forcing polished literary mourning through an abrasive contemporary container. Interest is A because that collision is distinctive, memorable, and unusually generative across Hrönir perspectives; several reviewers independently find that the musical form changes how the inherited text is felt. Quality is B because the strongest verbal density belongs to Borges, while the composer notes overgeneralize from one successful juxtaposition into claims that phonk is a "more honest" sonic form and that a good idea survives any container. The experiment is good and worth revisiting, but its explanation is less calibrated than its central artistic gesture.
