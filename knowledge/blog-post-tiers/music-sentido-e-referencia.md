summary: >-
  "Sentido e Referência" is a strong philosophical song whose best lines turn Frege's distinction into felt pressure rather than merely paraphrasing it: the untouching stars, the name that tries to reach, the abyss the soul consoles, and the sense-as-bird image survive outside the explanatory notes. Quality is B because the achieved lyric is stronger than parts of the explanatory layer: the notes sometimes slide from Frege's formal distinction between sense and reference into the composer's broader phenomenological claim that the object always exceeds its naming, and some versions over-explain an ambiguity that works better when left open. Interest is A because the work makes a technical semantic distinction emotionally legible without reducing it to a gimmick, and the name/object gap remains generative across lyric, relationship analogy and competing version choices.
