summary: >-
  O Ritual de Abril (Anos de Saudade) is a strong Borgesian song whose best move is to turn the April 30 ritual into an original moda-de-viola chronology: clock-marked accompaniment, dated returns, the Santa Fe alfajor and the inventory of Beatriz's photographs make memory feel accumulated rather than merely described. Quality is B because the selected work is consistently good and often excellent at the lyric level, but its composer notes sometimes over-explain the strongest images and make a universal claim about how saudade operates from one fictional case; the broader Hrönir record is also only middling in ordinal/head-to-head terms despite solid absolute quality. Interest is A because the Borges-to-moda-de-viola transformation, the inside-the-habit perspective and the image-memory/ritual structure are distinctive, memorable and generative even within a corpus that returns often to Borges.
