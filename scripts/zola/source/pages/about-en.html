I write, build software, and make music at the border of AI, philosophy, and literature. This space is where those threads collide: essays, technical experiments, songs, and tools for thought.

What lives here

This space is a digital garden — a slow accumulation of essays, technical guides, music, and philosophical fragments on how we inhabit a world increasingly mediated by intelligence. A few recurring threads:

  • AI & agency — agents, models, alignment, semantic maps, and strange machine behavior.
  • Process & metaphysics — Whitehead, Heraclitus, and computation as becoming.
  • Software & tools for thought — experiments, protocols, parsers, agents, and small systems.
  • Literature & music — Borges, adaptation, songs, and experiments where form carries the argument.
  • Law & institutions — when technical systems collide with public institutions and legal design.
  • Family memory — what it means to archive a life with help from a machine.

Most essays are in English; key essays have Portuguese versions.
