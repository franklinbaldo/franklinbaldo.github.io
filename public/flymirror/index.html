What this demo really measures. Fly-likeness is a rendered-mask overlap against a simple canonical Drosophila-like silhouette; it is a proxy, not a claim that the fly has a semantic concept of “fly”. Selfness is deliberately different: it is estimated from temporal correlation between changes in left/right motor commands and pixel motion on the mirrored opposite side. An independent motor-babbling term helps distinguish causal correspondence from coincidental symmetric motion. The body topology is constrained in this first demo, while proportions, appendage count, actuator coupling and dynamics evolve.
