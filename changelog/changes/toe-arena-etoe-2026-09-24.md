# ToE Arena: Ether Theory of Everything enters at F/A

The Arena adds **Ether Theory of Everything (ÆToE)** after its seed document advanced to v0.1.25 on 2026-09-24.

The programme is active, explicit and simulator-oriented, but its current physics formulation asserts tired-light cosmological redshift and a proton-decay origin for the CMB without reproducing observed cosmological time dilation or the near-perfect CMB blackbody spectrum. It also has not yet recovered the chiral Standard Model or a controlled Lorentz-invariant continuum limit. Those are present-formulation scientific blockers, so the scientific tier is **F**.

Interest remains **A** rather than tracking the scientific tier: the discrete etheron ontology, cyclic lattice/time construction, metrological grammar and breadth of concrete claims create a large adversarial-testing surface.

**Clash:** The Lucron Model remains **C/A**. Both are discrete fundamental ontologies, but Lucron currently exposes substantially stronger quantitative Standard-Model/cosmology correspondence and explicit failure accounting. No existing tier changes in this pass.
