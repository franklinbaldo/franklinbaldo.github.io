# Computational Finitism enters the ToE Arena

Computational Finitism moves from the watchlist into its own canonical OKF card at **D scientific / S interest**, medium confidence. The scientific tier reflects substantial unresolved recovery and independent-validation gaps; the interest tier reflects the programme's unusually broad computational and falsification surface.

The same audit screens recent emergent-spacetime and superfluid/twistor proposals without promoting them into the ToE ranking where gravity-plus-Standard-Model scope is not yet demonstrated.
