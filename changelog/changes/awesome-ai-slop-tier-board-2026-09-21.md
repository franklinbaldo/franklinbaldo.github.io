# Awesome AI Slop

- Adds the normative `awesome-ai-slop` OKF type.
- Defines independent `slop_tier`, `interest_tier` and `confidence`.
- Makes `knowledge/awesome-ai-slop/*.md` the semantic source of truth.
- Adds `/pt/awesome-ai-slop/` with an empty-state tier board ready for reviewed cases.
- Defines S as canonical/high-density slop, not "best".
- Requires direct evidence of material AI mediation; writing style alone is not enough.
- Evaluates artifacts, not people.
