# Equation Atlas gains an interactive UEKG universe

The Portuguese Equation Atlas now opens with a full-width interactive graph. The same formula nodes can be regrouped by scientific domain or by verified mathematical family, making cross-domain structure visible without replacing the underlying OKF source of truth. Visitors can focus nodes, inspect relationships, follow sources, and spotlight verified families before continuing into the existing taxonomy, occurrence, and family sections.
