date: 2026-10-18
description: Preserve search queries and restore language switching in paginated archives
tags: [ui, search, navigation]
---

Search queries now stay in the URL while typing or clearing, so reloading,
returning from a result, sharing a link and changing language retain the intended
query. The language selector is available on every archive page and opens the
other language's archive, whose pagination may differ.
