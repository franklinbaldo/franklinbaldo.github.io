# Public research publication is now opt-in

- Upstream research no longer implies a public portfolio card.
- Research whose publication approval was withdrawn, plus stale public references to it, is removed from the current public tree.
- Historical routine/rate traces that referenced withdrawn research were removed from the current public tree as an explicit publication-withdrawal exception.
- The source research repository remains unchanged.
