# Tier `three-hammers`

Adds one canonical `blog-post-tier` card keyed by the shared `translationKey` for the English and Portuguese versions of **Three Hammers Walk Into a Bar / Três Martelos Entram num Bar**.

Placement: **quality A / interest A / confidence high**.

The review uses the current top-cluster OpenSkill result, the documented de-confounded signal, broad perspective coverage, representative duel reviews, and the post's version history. Quality S is withheld because skeptical review identifies an under-supported Hofstadter/content-addressing lineage and returning-reader review identifies repeated house-style devices. Interest S is also withheld because those repeated devices materially reduce distinctiveness even though the core three-hammer frame remains memorable and generative.
