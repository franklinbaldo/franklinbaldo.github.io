# ToE Arena r215

Reconstructed the Arena and franklinbaldo/papers before scanning. The papers repository had no new fundamental-physics candidate after 79c1c76a9cec3d73083175f0e50563e3acf04e37.

The objective eligible pool contained six non-duplicate recent candidates. A single binding `shuf -n 3` selected Curvature-Induced Dynamical Spacetime Dimension, Analytic Rotating Black Holes in Higher-Derivative Gravity, and Minimal Supersymmetric SU(5).

- Added **Curvature-Induced Dynamical Spacetime Dimension** at **C/A**, medium confidence. The model is peer-reviewed and explicitly covariant, but remains classical and phenomenological rather than microscopically derived.
- Added **Parity-Even Cubic Gravity EFT** at **B/A**, medium confidence. Recent waveform work and the first analytic generic-spin black-hole metric materially improve strong-field calculability, while the framework remains an EFT with undetected couplings.
- Added **Minimal Supersymmetric SU(5)** at **A/A**, medium confidence. Modern minisplit/threshold analyses reopen narrow testable regions despite historic proton-decay exclusions; no SUSY particle or proton decay has been observed.

No existing tier changed.
