# Scientific Equation Atlas bootstrap

- Adds OKF types for science branches, scientific formula occurrences, recurring equation families and research runs.
- Seeds a small taxonomy spanning physics, chemistry and engineering.
- Adds four sourced scientific occurrences: radioactive decay, first-order chemical kinetics, Newton cooling and RC discharge.
- Connects all four to a verified first-order linear-decay family with explicit substitutions in authored Markdown.
- Adds the recurrent research routine and a prior-art bootstrap run covering OpenAlex, MSC2020, Wolfram Formula Repository, EqWorld, OntoMathPro and Bootstrap.
- Publishes the first reader-facing projection at `/pt/atlas-das-equacoes/`.
