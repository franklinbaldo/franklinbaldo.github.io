The observatory now has a card type for a population its vocabulary-based discovery could not see. A **solo-builder** card records a public account producing substantially, alone, with AI assistance, on work that is not a commodity, and receiving little or no interaction from other people.

Searching for `consciousness`, `ontology` and `soul` turned out to be a proxy that fails in both directions: it admits ordinary ontology engineering, and it misses someone writing their own operating system, their own Lisp or their own file system entirely. The defining observation is production without reception, not what the work is about.

It is a separate type rather than a widening of **ai-epistemic-world**, whose tiers measure whether an AI-mediated epistemic world exists and can be reconstructed — a question that means nothing for a BSD fork. An account holds one card or the other, never both, and a card keeps its slug when the framing changes.

The field the type exists for is `missing_resource`: what the apparent blockage implies is absent — knowledge, a tool, a reviewer, a user, a peer. It aggregates, which is the point. Several builders blocked for want of a reviewer is a fact about the environment; one builder unaware that a library exists is a single message.

`reception` must distinguish a measured zero from an unmeasured one and record its window, because zero interaction across a few sampled hours is not zero accumulated stars. Volume remains a triage band only, never a rank and never a characteristic of a person, and contact stays gated by **ai-epistemic-intervention** with its existing disclosure and follow-up limits.
