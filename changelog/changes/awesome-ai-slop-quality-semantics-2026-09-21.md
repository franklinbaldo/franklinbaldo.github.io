# Correct Awesome AI Slop semantics

- Treats "slop" as the collection's playful label for AI-made culture, not as a negative quality dimension.
- Replaces `slop_tier` with `quality_tier`.
- Replaces negative `slop_signals` / `redeeming_features` framing with `quality_signals` / `limitations`.
- Defines S as exceptional AI-made culture that stands on its own beyond the novelty of AI production.
- Keeps `interest_tier` independent for novelty, cultural significance and memetic/technical interest.
- Clarifies that strong human direction, editing and curation are compatible with — and may improve — an Awesome AI Slop entry.
