# AI-mediated epistemic worlds: interventions and bridges

- Adds an OKF-native `ai-epistemic-intervention` contract for public issues, PRs, comments, controls and replication requests.
- Adds a strict engagement gate: after one initial public touch, the target moves to `awaiting_response`; no bump, duplicate issue, PR or new comment is allowed until material engagement or genuinely new evidence.
- Preserves pre-intervention baselines so later adoption is not miscounted as independent convergence.
- Adds an `ai-epistemic-convergence` contract for recurring ideas, mechanisms and cross-project research opportunities.
- Seeds three convergence hypotheses: memory→continuity→identity, endogenous self-correction, and relational alignment.
- Seeds three transparent pilot interventions that transfer methods and controls rather than beliefs.
