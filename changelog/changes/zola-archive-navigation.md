Adds a bilingual full-archive search form, page and publication counts, and an
explicit explanation that type filtering applies to the current page. Existing
pagination and article routes remain unchanged. Groups the More menu into reading,
experiments, and site information with semantic headings and keyboard focus.
The standalone Fourier laboratory links back to its accompanying article, including
when opened inside an iframe. Adds browser/build assertions and desktop/mobile
screenshots for both archive languages, expanded menus, and the Fourier demo.
