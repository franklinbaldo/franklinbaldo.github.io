The Portuguese essay now asks how much of an inflation decline would remain
if Treasury transfers compensated bondholders for lost interest income after
a permanent rate cut. It distinguishes a conditional neo-Fisherian equilibrium
prediction from a proposed fiscal-income mechanism, and explains the
interventions and controls needed to separate their contributions.
The additional section is editorial and does not claim new Lean results.
