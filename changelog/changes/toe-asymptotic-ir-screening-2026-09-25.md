# ToE Arena: Asymptotic Safety gains IR-screening evidence

- Records Branchina, Gandolfo and Pernace, *Infrared Screening of the Cosmological Constant*, arXiv:2609.29979v1, posted 2026-09-24.
- Keeps **Asymptotic Safety at A scientific / A interest, medium confidence**.
- Treats the result as material adjacent RG-gravity evidence, not empirical confirmation: it finds an analytic infrared attractor for the cosmological constant in an Einstein-Hilbert plus R-squared truncation, but freezes other running couplings and uses a one-mode saddle ansatz.
- Notes the same research line's measure/cutoff sensitivity and therefore does not count this result as an independent confirmation of the canonical ultraviolet fixed point.
- Fresh screening found no new full-scope gravity-plus-realistic-Standard-Model contender in the 25 September hep-th listings or in the newest franklinbaldo/papers commits.
