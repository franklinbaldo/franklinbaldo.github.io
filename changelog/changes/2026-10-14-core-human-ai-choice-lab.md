A new English post, "Are AI Decisions Human at Their CORE? An Interactive Choice
Lab", uses the CORE research project to separate matching human choices from
matching human cognition. It embeds a browser choice lab at
`/demos/core-choice-lab/` for trying a reward-learning task directly in the
page.
