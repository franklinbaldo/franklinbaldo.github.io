Three legacy YouTube iframes in **The Game on the Folded Grid** and **The Gap at the Center of the World** now expose descriptive `title` attributes and use `loading="lazy"`. The videos, prose, layout, URLs, and editorial claims are unchanged; the embeds are simply named for assistive technology and no longer need to compete for network work before they approach the viewport.
