# Bounded TAP diagnostics

- Adds fixed read-only TAP availability probes with response size and time limits
- Preserves an eight-record Gaia-ID-selected fixture and authored execution reports
- Separates HTTP and TAP failures from scientific nondetections
- Does not establish unbiased completeness, independent Gaia matching, or physical transients
