description: Add a structured articulated shader scene with independent visual controls
---

The proprioception laboratory gains a procedural 3D scene with a leg-shaped
articulated form, floor, material and studio lighting. Inspection and expressive
modes share the same physical pose and neural data. Curvature, roughness, light
direction, palette and surface detail are appearance-only controls, separate
from the passive stimulus. A labeled neural marker uses an explicit display
scale; absent data remains distinct from zero activity.

The numerical panels and SVG fallback remain available without WebGL or after
context loss. Rendering is on demand, resolution-limited, and has no autonomous
decorative motion. The encoder, connectome, input mapping and readouts are
unchanged. Shader Skills informs the visual approach; no external runtime or
package is added.
